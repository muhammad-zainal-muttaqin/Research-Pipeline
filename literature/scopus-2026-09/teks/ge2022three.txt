A B S T R A C T
The automatic classiﬁcation of apple tree organs is of great signiﬁcance for automatic
pruning of apple trees, automatic picking of apple fruits, and estimation of fruit yield. How-
ever, there are some problems of dense foliage, partial occlusion and clustering of apple
fruits. All of the problems above would contribute to the difﬁculties of organs classiﬁcation
and yield estimation of the apple trees. In this paper a method based on Color and Shape
Multi-features Fusion and Support Vector Machine (SVM) for 3D apple tree organs classiﬁ-
cation and yield estimation was proposed. The method was designed for dwarf and densely
planted apple trees at the early and late maturity stages. 196-dimensional feature vectors
composed with Red Green Blue (RGB), Hue Saturation Value (HSV), Curvatures, Fast Point
Feature Histogram (FPFH), and Spin Image were extracted ﬁrstly. And then the SVM based
on linear kernel function was trained, after that the trained SVM was used for apple tree
organs classiﬁcation. Then the position weighted smoothing algorithm was used for clas-
siﬁed apple tree organs smoothing. Then the agglomerative hierarchical clustering algo-
rithm was used to recognize single apple fruit for yield estimation. On the same training
and test set the experimental results showed that the SVM based on linear kernel function
outperformed the KNN algorithm and Ensemble algorithm. The Recall, Precision and F1
score of the proposed method for yield estimation were 93.75%, 96.15% and 94.93% respec-
tively. In summary, to solve the problems of apple tree organs classiﬁcation and yield esti-
mation in natural apple orchard, a novelty method based on multi-features fusion and SVM
was proposed and achieve good performance. Moreover, the proposed method could pro-
vide technical support for automatic apple picking, automatic pruning of fruit trees, and
automatic information acquisition and management in orchards.

Introduction
In China, apple trees are widely planted. However, the prun-
ing of apple trees, picking of apples, and real-time detection
and management of orchard are still mainly manual. The
classiﬁcation and recognition of apple tree organs are of great
signiﬁcance for the automatic management of orchards. The
automatic recognition of apple tree stems can be used for
automatic pruning of apple trees. The automatic recognition
of apple fruits can be used for automatic apple picking and
yield estimation. The automatic recognition of apple tree foli-
age can be used for automatic information acquisition and
management of orchards. So in recent years, many experts
and scholars have carried out corresponding studies.
With the development of computer technologies, 3D point
cloud was widely used in many ﬁelds. Due to 3D point cloud
provide both color and 3D position information. So the color
and shape features could be extracted from 3D point cloud.
And then, these features were used with supervised machine
learning method for fruit recognition, fruit positioning, fruit
tree organs classiﬁcation, phenotyping and yield estimation
in orchards.
For apples. Ji [1] et al. applied morphological skeleton fea-
tures and binocular stereo vision technology to apple branch
positioning. Nguyen [2] et al. applied a RGB-D camera to apple
fruit recognition and positioning. The distance and color ﬁlters
were used to remove foliages, branches, and tree trunks in the
3D point cloud. And then the Euclidean clustering algorithm
was used for apple fruit recognition. However, the proposed
method had limited correct recognition rate for partially
occluded apples. Karkee [3,4] et al. used a time of ﬂight of light
based three dimensional (TOF 3D) camera to reconstruct apple
trees in orchards, for the pruning of apple tree. Si [5] et al.
applied binocular stereo vision to apple fruit positioning. At
the same time, the binocular stereo vision could also be used
for the sweet pepper stem positioning [6], peach ﬂower posi-
tioning [7], litchi fruit positioning [8], pomegranate fruit posi-
tioning [9] and Guava positioning [10]. However, only within a
certain distance range, could the binocular camera have an
acceptable distance measurement accuracy. Gene´-Mola [11]
et al. used color (RGB), depth (D) and range-corrected intensity
signal (S) ﬁve channels information obtained from Kinect v2
RGB-D camera and Faster R-CNN for apple fruit detection.
However, a large training and test set were needed in this
study. Gongal [12] et al. proposed an over-the-row machine
vision system for apple fruit recognition and positioning. How-
ever, this system had a limited correct recognition rate. Tao [13]
et al. proposed a 3D apple recognition method based on Color-
Fast Point Feature Histograms (Color-FPFH) and Genetic Algo-
rithm based Support Vector Machine (GA-SVM). However, the
method was designed only for the local point cloud of apple
trees. Tsoulias [14] et al. proposed a method based on geomet-
ric and radiometric features using LiDAR Laser Scanner for
apple shape detection. And then the proposed method could
be used for apple fruit localization and apple fruit size estima-
tion. Zhang [15] et al. adapted TreeQSM to extract the informa-
tion of the fruit tree branch grades with. In this study both
Terrestrial Laser Scanning and Backpack-LiDAR were used.
However, no automation method was proposed for single tree
recognition. To solve this problem Zhou [16] proposed a spatial
clustering segmentation method based on K-dimensional tree
(KD tree) to recognize single tree point cloud. However, for tree
volume estimation, the proposed method had limited accuracy
with R2 = 0.8610.
As for other fruits. Feng [17] et al. designed a tomato pick-
ing system and applied structured light for the positioning of

Eizentals [20] et al. proposed a sweet pepper picking system.
The Lidar type laser range ﬁnder was used for sweet pepper
position measurement. However, the usage of manual thresh-

ing. And the nonlinear evaluator based on particle ﬁlter was

unknown fruit motion. Ramos [22] and Avendano [23] et al.
used color, curvatures, shape features, and SVM to classify
the nutritional structure of coffee branches. However, the pro-
posed method had limited Precision and Recall. For single
grapevine fruit recognition [24,25] and grapevine different tis-
sues classiﬁcation [26-28], the multi-features and classiﬁer
[26-29] were used. And then, the recognition and classiﬁca-
tion results were used for yield estimation [27] and pheno-
typic parameters acquisition [24,25,27-29].
He [30] et al. proposed a low cost multi-view stereo (MVS)
imaging system for 3D reconstruction of strawberry fruit to
obtain the phenotypic parameters of single strawberry fruit.
Xiong [31] et al. proposed a strawberry harvesting robot with
a cable-driven gripper. However, the proposed robot had lim-
ited picking rate of 53.6%. Nyarko [32] et al. used Convex Tem-
plate Instance (CTI) and k-nearest neighbor classiﬁer to
recognize the tomato, nectarine, pear and plum fruits. Wang
[33] et al. used Kinect RGB-D camera to detect the size of
mango on the tree. However, the device could not be used
in sunlight. Underwood [34] et al. used radar and vision sen-
sors for apricot tree canopy volume calculation, ﬂower and
fruit density evaluation, and fruit yield estimation. However,
the method had the limited accuracy of R2 = 0.77. Lin [10]
et al. used RGB-D camera and fully convolutional network to
classify guava and branches, and then the guava’s position
was evaluated. However, the method was proposed only for
the local point cloud of guava, and had limited the pose esti-
mation accuracy. Lin [35] et al. used Kinect V2 to obtain 3D
point loud of citrus. Bayes-classiﬁer and Density clustering
were used for the pre-classiﬁcation of citrus fruit. And then
a color, gradient, and geometry feature based Support Vector
Machine classiﬁer was used for precise classiﬁcation of Citrus
fruit. Kusumam [36] et al. used Viewpoint Angle (VA), View-
point Feature Histogram (VFH), Clustered Viewpoint Feature
Histogram features (CVFH), and Geometric features based
Support Vector Machine for recognition and size estimation
of broccoli fruit. However, there was no aggregation and
occlusion between the broccoli fruits. Tu [37] et al. used
RGB, dense scale invariant features transform (DSIFT) algo-
rithm, locality-constrained linear coding (LLC) features and
Support Vector Machine for maturity evaluation of passion
fruits detected by Faster-RCNN. For pomegranate tree organs
classiﬁcation and pomegranate fruit yield estimation, Zhang
[38] et al. proposed a method based on multi-features fusion
and Support Vector Machine. For multi-features fusion the
color and shape features were extracted in this study. Sun
[39] et al. proposed a method based on Support Vector
Machine and developed density-based clustering for cotton
boll counting and cotton boll volume obtaining. The experi-
mental results showed that the R2 values between ﬁber yield
and boll number was 0.87, and R2 values between ﬁber yield
and boll volume was 0.66.
The literature review showed that the 3D point cloud was
widely used for fruit recognition, organs classiﬁcation and
achieved some good results. However, for organs classiﬁca-
tion of 3D global apple tree point cloud, there are still some
problems of dense foliage, partial occlusion and clustering
of apple fruits in natural apple orchard. All of the problems
have brought challenges to organs classiﬁcation and yield
estimation of the apple trees. In this paper, a method based
on Color and Shape Multi-features Fusion and Support Vector
Machine (SVM) for 3D apple tree organs classiﬁcation and
yield estimation was proposed. Firstly, Red Green Blue (RGB),
Hue Saturation Value (HSV), Curvatures, Fast Point Feature
Histogram (FPFH) and Spin Image features were extracted.
And then the SVM based on linear kernel function was used
for apple tree organs classiﬁcation. The position weighted
smoothing algorithm was used for smoothing. Finally, the
agglomerative hierarchical clustering algorithm was used to
recognize single apple fruit for yield estimation. The proposed
method could provide technical support for automatic apple
picking, automatic pruning of fruit trees, and automatic infor-
mation acquisition and management in orchards.

2.2.3.
Apple organs classiﬁcation model SVM
For each point both in training and test set, a 196-dimensional
feature vectors composed with Red Green Blue (RGB), Hue Sat-
uration Value (HSV), Curvatures, Fast Point Feature Histogram
(FPFH) and Spin Image were extracted. And then the feature
vectors and their corresponding labels were sent to the linear
kernel function based SVM [42] for training. The parameter
optimization mode of SVM were set as automatic optimiza-
tion in this paper. 10 cross validation was used in training
step. The classiﬁcation error was obtained. When the classiﬁ-
cation error is less than a certain value, it means that the SVM
model being trained could be used for apple tree organs clas-
siﬁcation. For organs classiﬁcation, the feature vectors of the
global apple tree point cloud were sent to the trained SVM
model, and then the prediction label for each point could be
acquired. Finally, the global apple tree point cloud was classi-
ﬁed into fruits, branches and foliages.

Single apple fruit clustering
After smoothing and outlier removal of classiﬁed apple fruit

rithm [46] was used to recognize single apple fruit for yield
estimation. And the proposed algorithm could solve the prob-
lem of apple fruit misclassiﬁcation, caused by partial occlu-
sion and clustering of apple fruits.
