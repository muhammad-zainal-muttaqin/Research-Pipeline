Abstract—Palm oil is an essential world vegetable oil. The oil 
palm is reported to have the highest oil yield compared to other 
major world crops. Palm oil can be extracted from two main 
sources that are fresh fruit bunches and loose fruits. Although 
the main focus of the oil producer is to collect fruit bunches, the 
loose fruit has relatively higher oil content. However, the 
condition of scattered loose fruits at the farm wastes the 
farmers’ time and energy in the loose fruit collecting process, 
and it leads to the lower back pain and spine fatigue of the 
workers. The conventional methods for collecting loose fruit 
such as manual hand-picked or a roller-type fruit collector are 
still less efficient. Hence, this work proposes a system for oil 
palm loose fruits detection using Faster R-CNN, a deep learning 
algorithm and NVIDIA Jetson TX2 hardware. In this study, 500 
images of loose fruits were collected from an oil palm farm at 
Bukit Bangkong, Selangor during the harvesting process. The 
data were pre-processed using few techniques such as image 
resizing, cropping, data augmentation and data labelling. Faster 
R-CNN, a deep learning algorithm is used to train the model of 
the detection system by using 400 images from the acquired 
sample. The trained model was validated and tested with the 
remaining 100 images from the sample. The model performance 
showed that the loose fruit detection system is built successfully 
when it achieved accuracy for about 94%, 94% and 91% for 
intersection-of-union thresholds equal to 0.5, 0.7 and 0.9, 
respectively. The result showed that the developed system is able 
to detect oil palm loose fruits accurately and has the potential in 
contributing to the development of oil palm loose fruit automatic 
harvesting system. 
Keywords—Oil palm loose fruit; Faster R-CNN; Deep 
learning; Object detection; Jetson TX2 GPU 
 
I. INTRODUCTION 
 
Oil palm is one of the major contributors to the production 
of world vegetable oils. It is reported that oil palm can produce 
5 to 8 times more oil compared to other major crops such as 
soybean and canola within the same land size. In certain 
countries like Indonesia and Malaysia, oil palm plays a vital 
role in the economy. For example, the palm oil industry is one 
of the Malaysia’s leading industries in contributing to the 
country’s gross domestic product (GDP) [1].  There are two 
sources of oil from the palm which are fresh fruit bunches 
(FFB) and loose fruits. Although the main focus of the oil 
producer is to collect fruit bunches, the loose fruit has 
relatively higher oil content. The overall oil content for 
collecting all loose fruit after harvesting is around 48% while 
for FFB is around 22% only [2].  
The main challenges in collecting loose fruits are twofold: 
1) fruits are randomly scattered as shown in Fig. 1 probably 
obstructed on the ground, and 2) the lack of suitable tools for 
the workers to collect. As such, loose fruits are frequently 
abandoned on the farm’s ground. The abandoned loose fruits 
lead to oil palm farm management issue such as the growth of 
bushy and wild oil palm tree. Besides, it will indirectly 
increase the cost for weeding and rise the competition for 
nutrients among the good oil palm trees and wild oil palm 
trees. Moreover, collecting loose fruit will lead to back pain 
and spine fatigue due to frequently picking up loose fruits 
using conventional method which is by hand or raking [3].  
From the literature, there are significant innovations made 
to collect loose fruits such as roller-type pickers, vacuum 
collectors and relay collection machines [4]. However, it still 
requires manpower to handle and drive them. Hence, the 
concept of smart harvesting using autonomous machines 
should be implemented in the era of industry 4.0. Several 
works

On the other hand, an intelligent vision system also plays 
a part in an autonomous system. Several works have applied 
digital image processing techniques for various automated 
detection purposes, specifically in agriculture research, such 
as in [6], [7].  Thus, this work proposes an automatic oil palm 
loose fruit detection system using a graphical processing unit 
(GPU) hardware and deep learning software. The proposed 
work aims at providing an intelligent vision system to any 
loose fruits collector machine that intended to be made 
automatic and autonomous. With this system, the process of 
collecting loose fruit can be simplified automatically. The 
benefit of such automatic and autonomous system is that it 
will reduce the required number of workers, save time and 
revolutionise the conventional technology. It can also reduce 
the farmer’s losses due to abandoning those loose fruits.

II. METHODOLOGY 
Generally, there are 6 stages including software part and 
hardware part in order to conduct this research as shown in 
Fig. 2. The stages include data acquisition from palm oil farm, 
pre-processing sample data, training and validating pre-
processed sample data, testing the system with new sample 
data, analysing the performance of the system and the 
implementation of Rocker-Bogie vehicle with the object 
detection system.  
 
Fig. 1. Abandoned palm oil loose fruit. 
 
 
A. Data acquisition and pre-processing 
In this stage, 500 samples of oil palm loose fruits were 
collected from the oil palm plantation at Bukit Bangkong, 
Selangor during the harvesting period. The images of loose 
fruits were taken in various sizes and lighting conditions. The 
images were also taken with different backgrounds to avoid 
the singularities within the samples [8]. The sample images 
were pre-processed by following sub-stages in sequence: 
resize, data augmentation, and labelling. 
In order to reduce the computational training and testing 
time of the model, all sample images were resized to 640x480 
pixels. Then, the resized sample images were augmented to 
increase the amount of dataset. A few transformation 
techniques were implemented including flipping, rotating, 
padding, intensity, colour jittering and edge enhancement. 
Finally, each image was labelled properly with its name and 
bounding box using LabelImg software and was stored in the 
XML format. 
After the pre-processing stage, the images were splitted 
into training and testing dataset. Images were selected 
randomly and used for the model training and parameter 
optimisation with a ratio of 4:1. It means 80% of the images 
were selected as the training set and the remaining 20% of the 
images were used as the testing set.

D. Hardware Implementation 
 
A rocker-bogie type vehicle as shown in Fig. 5 was built 
to prototype the concept of autonomous harvesting machines. 
The rocker-bogie design allows the vehicle to travel easily on 
uneven surfaces in the oil palm farm and to across the 
obstacles such as stone and woods while the detection system 
is running. The vehicle is equipped with a Jetson TX2 
platform that allows the portable real-time object detection to 
be executed. Jetson TX2 is a GPU standalone unit that is 
developed based on NVIDIA Pascal family GPU with 256 
CUDA cores. The trained and tested detection model was 
transferred into the Jetson TX2 platform to perform the task. 
III. RESULTS AND DISCUSSION 
A. Training, Validation, Testing and Analysis 
 
 
The training was stopped at epoch-80000 when the loss is 
below a threshold of 0.05, in this case is 0.0496. Fig. 6 shows 
the loss performance of the detection model during the 
training stage. The graph shows that the training loss is 
decrease gradually from epoch to epoch, showing that the 
training achieved convergence. From the training, the best 
model with the lowest loss was chosen as the detection model. 
Next, the accuracy and the performance of the detection 
model was assessed based on IOU evaluation on mAP and 
confusion matrix. Training accuracy is perfect when mAP 
reaches a value around 1. In Table 1, mAPIoU=.50:.95 achieved 
an average accuracy value of 0.770. This means that the 
average overall accuracy of all IoU thresholds from 0.50 to 
0.95 can still achieve convincing results. Besides, both 
mAPIoU=.50 and mAPIoU=.75 achieved the value of 0.988 and 
0.916, respectively, which indicate that the area of the 
predicted bounding box overlapped most of the area of the 
ground-truth box. In other word, the trained model can detect 
the oil palm loose fruit as the object of interest accurately. The 
analysis also found that the scaled mAP with small object, 
medium object, and large object sizes are 0.264, 0.688 and 
0.837, respectively. In this case, the scaled mAP shows that 
the oil palm oil loose fruit is mostly categorised as large object 
by the model during training.

IV. CONCLUSION 
 
An oil palm loose fruits image detection system is 
proposed using GPU and deep learning approach. The image 
size and the amount of images with different background are 
the vital elements in deep learning due to its relation with the 
training loss and accuracy for loose fruit detection as well as 
the training and computational time. For the CNN model, the 
convolutional layer and pooling layer are used for feature
