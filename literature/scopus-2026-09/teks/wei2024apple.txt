ABSTRACT
In this research, we employ a deep learning approach to extract
features from an apple image dataset, enabling precise counting and
localization of apples. We address challenges like low resolution
and small targets by optimizing the network and implementing
small target optimization. The focus in another task is on building
a mathematical model using sYOLOv5 to assess apple ripeness
based on color and texture features. Additionally, we compute the
two-dimensional area and estimate mass by combining instance
segmentation and traditional image processing techniques. Lastly,
we train a fruit recognition model using sYOLOv5 on a harvested
fruit dataset.

INTRODUCTION
As the world’s largest producer and largest exporter of apples, China
produces apples of high and low quality, which play an important
role in the world apple market. China’s annual apple production
is about 35 million tons, of which China exports one-sixth of the
world’s exported apples [1-3].
However, apple picking in China is still mainly done by hand.
Due to the nature of fruit growing, when apples are ripe, a large
number of pickers are needed in the apple region within a few
days. At this point, the regional limitations of farmers, most of
whom grow only in their own parks, and the aging of agricultural
workers due to the departure of young laborers to work in their
home- towns, have become the two main problems hindering apple
picking in China. In order to get rid of the above dilemma, China has
been researching robots that can pick apples autonomously since
2011, and although some progress has been made, the popularity of
various apple-picking robots in the world is still far from ideal [4].
Most of the existing apple picking robots are unable to accurately
identify various types of obstacles such as obstructions, and do not
have the ability to make accurate judgments according to the actual
scene, which can easily damage the fruits, and even cause injuries
to the picking hand and robotic arm, and impair the quality of the
fruits. Therefore, it is of great economic and practical significance
to study how to improve the recognition and classification of fruit
images by picking robots.

IMAGE PREPROCESSING
For a dataset of already collected, extracting image features, build-
ing a model to count the number of apples, and plotting a histogram
of the distribution of all apples. We need to judge whether there are
apples in the image and count the number of apples in each image,
so based on the image classification model of deep learning Xi, For
example, ResNet, VGG and other networks are difficult to achieve
the expected results in this problem. This paper uses YOLOv5, a
single-stage object detection model based on deep Xi, to deal with
the problem [5].
Most apple images taken in natural environments are susceptible
to natural light, occlusion, and shadows, i.e., are more sensitive to
brightness. The three components of the RGB color space are all
closely related to brightness, and as soon as the brightness changes,
all three components will change with it. We want to detect ripe
apples in an orchard image, the color of ripe apples is often very
different from the surrounding color, so the HSV color space fits as
an input image. Therefore, we first convert the image from RGB
color space to HSV color space. After converting the images to HSV
color space, we also scaled all image sizes from 270 * 180 to 640 *
640 in order to facilitate subsequent image inputs to the network.
Once we input the images into the YOLOv5 network one by one,
the network extracts the feature information of each image and
then detects the apples in the image. The results as shown in figure
1.
The analysis results showed that a total of 44 images, such as
105 .jpg and 112 .jpg, were not detected. The observed images show
that in some images, there are targets that are not detected. The
targets in the image are dense and numerous, which may be that
the YOLOv5 network is not sensitive enough to small targets [5],
and the detection of small targets is not well optimized. Therefore,
this paper innovatively proposes a scheme to improve the flexibility
of small target detection in YOLOv5 network.

apples. Next, we will innovatively propose the sYOLOv5 network by
optimizing the network structure, so as to enhance the extraction of
apple texture features [6] for maturity analysis and determination.

CONCLUSION
In this paper, the apple data set is processed based on the Yolov5
model. The distribution characteristics of the data, such as the
number and location of apples in each picture in the data set, are
directly calculated from the returned results. When analyzing the
maturity of apples, the information of RGB three-channel is used
to assist the classification, and the apples are finally classified into
four categories of maturity and all the apples in the whole data set
are divided. When dealing with apple quality, we take into account
the relationship between apples and image imaging area for unsu-
pervised prediction of the relative quality of apples. Overall, our
work accomplishes the task of apple target detection in complex
situations, and such work will form a great help for robots to intelli-
gently collect apples. In addition to this, we have accomplished the
above task without additional information labeling in the data set,
so we can follow this method to research unsupervised prediction
methods that are more suitable for the task or add more labels to
the data set in the future to use supervised algorithms to compute
accurate quality and other information.
