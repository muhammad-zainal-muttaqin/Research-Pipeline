Abstract—Grading maturity oil palm fresh fruit bunches 
(FFB) is an essential issue in the agriculture sector because the 
quality of palm oil determines based on the maturity level. 
Recently, the production of high-quality palm oil has increased 
continually. Therefore, the implementation of computer vision 
in agriculture for grading the maturity of oil palm FFB is 
required to avoid subjectivity in determining the maturity 
level.  This study develops a classification method for grading 
the maturity level of FFB. Generally, this classification method 
performed using the color feature. In this study, the color 
feature is used to distinguish the maturity level of oil palm 
FFB. The mean value extracted as the color features in L*a*b 
color space is followed by implementing a machine learning 
method: Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA), in the 
classification process. The experiment used a dataset of 150 
images with three different classes: raw, under-ripe, and ripe. 
The dataset is applied in two stages: training and testing of 60 
images and 90 images, respectively. The performance 
evaluation of the method used successfully achieved an 
accuracy value of 98.89% using a testing dataset of 90 images. 
Keywords—oil palm fresh fruit bunches, color features, 
classification, computer vision, machine Learning 
I. INTRODUCTION 
Oil palm is a plantation crop with high economic value 
and one of the most widely used vegetable oil. Vegetable oil 
produced dividing into two main types of products, namely 
Crude Palm Oil (CPO) and Crude Palm Kernel Oil (CPKO). 
Both of them are widely used by consumers in the food 
industry even as a biodiesel substrate to reduce fossil energy 
use [1]. To obtain high-quality palm oil, the maturity level of 
oil palm FFB, when harvested, should be accurate [2]. The 
quality of oil palm can be determined based on its texture, 
shape, and color features [3]. Based on the previous 
explication, the classification method for grading the 
maturity of oil palm FFB needs to develop to produce high-
quality oil [4]. The classification of oil palm FFB is divided 
into three classes, i.e., raw, under-ripe, and ripe [1]. The 
correlation between the maturity level of oil palm fruit and 
the oil content as follow raw fruit has low oil content, under-
ripe fruit has less than optimal oil content, and ripe fruit has 
high oil content [2]. 
Currently, the implementation of computer vision in 
agriculture has been developed for various purposes. To 
complete these objectives requires two main processes, 
specifically segmentation and classification. In the previous 
study, the fruit area segmentation method generally used 
thresholding [5], [6], and edge detection [7]. Furthermore, 
several previous studies related to the classification of oil 
palm fruit maturity have been developed. The Blue-to-Red 
Fluorescence ratio index applied to the classification and 
regression Tree (C&RT) method [8], Multilayer Perceptron 
(MLP), and Principal Component Analysis (PCA) [9] 
designed a portable multi-band active optical sensor system 
to detect the ripeness of oil palm fruit [1]. In addition, an 
automatic machine applied to assess the maturity of oil palm 
FFB using the Stepwise Discriminate Analysis method [10], 
the study using the Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA) 
method for wetland classification using PoISAR imagery  
[11], features of mean and standard deviation for single oil 
pal fruit classification [12], detection of oil palm FFB 
maturity using the Hue Saturation and Intensity (HSI) color 
model with the Artificial Neural Network (ANN) method[3]. 
Another fruit maturity classification study used hyperspectral 
near-infrared (NIR) imaging technology using the LDA 
method [13]. 
The contribution of this study develops a classification 
method for grading the maturity of palm oil FFB. The 
classification consists of three classes, namely raw, under-
ripe, and ripe. This study performed the features extraction 
process in L*a*b color space so that four features were 
generated, namely the mean value of each color channel and 
the mean of gray color and classification using the LDA 
method. 
II. MATERIALS AND METHODS 
The method is evaluated using a local dataset of 150 
images, which is applied into two phases: training and 
testing. Each image is labeled according to the actual class 
based on the level of maturity (raw, under-ripe, and ripe). In 
the training phase, the dataset used was 60 images 
representing raw, under-ripe, and ripe classes, where each 
class consisted of 20 images. Meanwhile, in the testing 
phase, the total image used was 90 images consisting of 30 
images for each class of raw, under-ripe, and ripe. The 
dataset produced using a digital camera from a smartphone 
(Samsung A50) with the approximation distance between oil 
palm FFB and the camera is ± 10 cm. The resulting of the 
acquisition image is 4032 × 3024 pixels and saved in JPEG 
format. The use of digital camera technology on smartphones

C. Classification 
Classification is a process that aims to obtain the 
prediction class of data. This process consists of two main 
steps: training and testing—the objective of training to 
analyze the training data to get the classification rule. The 
second step is testing, wherein this step, the testing data uses 
to evaluate the performance of a method. Accuracy generally 
uses as the measuring tool in this step [19], [20]. 
In this study, the classification applied using the LDA 
algorithm. LDA is one of the popular algorithms of machine 
learning that works based on its experience by studying 
training data. LDA is used to identify variables that 
differentiate one class and another, which has unique features 
[21]. This algorithm is categorized as supervised learning 
due to it performs a training step to generate the required 
model parameters. The model is used to predict testing data 
and be classified in a particular class [22]. This algorithm 
aims to classify the objects into two classes or multiclass 
[23], [24].

IV. CONCLUSION 
The maturity grading method of oil palm FFB with three 
classes, including raw, under-ripe, and ripe, was developed. 
It applied based on L*a*b color space using mean features 
and performed the LDA algorithm. The evaluation of this 
method is carried out against a total of 150 images consisting 
of 60 images for training and 90 images for testing. The 
results of the performance evaluation succeeded in achieving 
an accuracy value of 98.88%. The method of maturity 
grading still has a vast opportunity to be developed. An 
example of development can be done using a combined 
variation of several color spaces or a deep learning method.
