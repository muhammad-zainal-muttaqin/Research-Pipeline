Abstract— This paper aim of this study is to assess the oil 
palm ripeness using a specific wavelength as a light source, 680 
nm, and simple image processing technique. This paper 
proposes a simple and low computation technique to estimate 
the oil palm FFB maturity using 680 nm LED as the light source 
and digital camera to capture the image. There are some 
parameters that can be used to detect oil palm FFB maturities, 
such as chlorophyll concentration and fruitlet detachment. In 
this research, the 680 nm image contrast is used to detect the 
chlorophyll due to it correlated with the chlorophyll absorbance. 
The accuracy of this approach technique is 64.94 %. The second 
approach is using texture analysis, skewness, to differentiate the 
detached and undetached fruitlets in oil palm FFB. The 
accuracy of this technique is 68.83 %. The last technique is 
combining image contrast and skewness. The result of this 
approach gives accuracy by 68.83 %. From the results, skewness 
and combination of contrast and skewness give the same 
accuracy, 68.83 %. It can be concluded that the estimation of oil 
palm FFB maturity can be done by using an only skewness value 
of the 680 nm image. The result is in-between another research 
results in oil palm FFB maturity grading. The proposed 
technique can be implemented using microcomputer. It will 
reduce the implementation cost and can be used to build 
portable systems. 
Keywords— 680 nm image, contrast, palm oil, ripeness, 
skewness 
I. INTRODUCTION 
Indonesia is the largest palm oil producer in the world. 
According to Indonesian Center for Estate Crops Research 
and Development, Ministry of Agriculture Republic of 
Indonesia; there are a lot of oil palm varieties in Indonesia. To 
improve productivity and efficiency, it is important to conduct 
assessment on the ripeness of fresh fruit bunches (FFBs) that 
is arrived in palm oil processing factory. Current method to 
assess the ripeness of FFBs is conducted manually using 
visual inspection by the expert grader. The parameters that are 
used by expert grader are the color of FFBs and the number of 
fruitlets detachment. This traditional method is subjective 
assessment that is depend on the human experience and their 
physical condition. Even though there is training to be a grader 
that is conducted locally by the palm oil processing factory, 
the main problem is that no certification process to become a 
certified oil palm grader. The second influence is physical 
condition; the graders should work in the outdoor area for 7 – 
8 hours and they have to assess around 800 FFBs from one 
dump truck in just 30 minutes. 
The most common oil palm variety that is planted in 
Indonesian plantation is nigrescens. Several studies to 
estimate and assess to assess the maturity level of the oil palm 
FFBs are conducted using several methods, such as computer 
vision based on the color of the FFBs and image entropy, 
hyperspectral imaging, visible light and near-infrared (NIR) 
spectroscopy. Several research in automatic oil palm ripeness 
grading use color based detection, especially to detect oil palm 
virescens variety, due to the ripeness of this oil palm can be 
detected by color changing. The color of unripe virescens is 
yellow and change to orange during its ripening, it is to 
identify the ripeness [1]. One of the main difficulties to assess 
the oil palm ripeness using color is that the nigrescens variety 
has different color during ripening. According to the expert 
(senior) grader, there are two type of nigrescens, that are black 
and red fruits. The color of the unripe black fruit is black and 
change to blackish red during its maturity. Meanwhile, the 
color of the unripe red fruit is red and change to reddish black 
during its maturity. There is no significant color changing 
throughout fruit ripening and to determine the ripeness of the 
fruit is based on the number of fruitlet detachment [2]. 
Another method is detecting the reflectance intensity using 
the light detection and ranging (LiDAR) scanning system to 
assess three levels of oil palm FFBs ripeness which is 
overripe, ripe, and under ripe [3]. In this systems, the 680 nm 
LED is used due to its correlation with oil palm lipid content. 
There are several parameters that are influence the oil palm 
maturity, such as the proportion of the chlorophyll and beta 
carotene in the oil palm. Unripe, under ripe, and overripe fruits 
have lower beta carotene content than the ripe one [4]. The 
unripe oil pam fruit has higher proportion of the chlorophyll 
and will gradually decrease during its ripeness [5]. One of the 
spectral characteristic of chlorophyll is it has absorbance in 
certain light wavelength, 670 nm [6]. The Spectral profile at 
three ripeness level (under ripe, ripe, and over ripe) of 
nigrescens fruits show that the most significant difference of 
the chlorophyll absorption is in around 675 nm [7].

The aim of this study is to assess the oil palm ripeness 
using specific wavelength as light source, 680 nm, and simple 
image processing technique. Even though LED with specific 
wavelength, 680 nm is used in this research due to its 
correlation with chlorophyll absorbance and in the literatures 
above, the specific wavelength to detect it is in 670 and 675 
nm; the intensity of this LED in 670-675 is around 80 % of the 
peak value. 
II. METHODS 
From the literature study, the maturity of oil palm FFB can 
be detected using specific wavelength, 680 nm. This research 
proposes a new method to assess oil palm FFB maturity using 
LED with this specific wavelength as light source and DSLR 
camera to capture the image. The output of the propose 
systems is an image with specific light spectrum, 680 nm. In 
this study, the image is captured in a closed room, to minimize 
noise ambient light. The proposed systems consist of four 680 
nm LEDs, the total power of this light source is 16 mW. The 
distance between the sensor / light source with the FFB are 
around 30 cm.  The illustration of the systems is shown in 
Figure 1. 
In total, there are 101 oil palm FFBs that are used in this 
study. Consists of 24 oil palm FFBs as training dataset and 77 
FFBs as testing dataset. The oil palms ripeness criterion for 
these datasets are determined by senior/chief grader of the 
palm oil processing plant with more than ten years of 
experience in the oil palm FFB grading. The training dataset 
consists of 16 unripe, 7 ripe, and 1 overripe FFBs. The testing 
dataset consists of 39 unripe and 38 ripe oil palm FFBs. In this 
research, the number of overripe FFB is only 1, due to rarity 
of the overripe FFBs in oil palm processing plant. 
Previous research that is utilized LiDAR, show that the 
reflectance intensity can be used to classify the oil palm 
ripeness maturity. In the image, it is common to use image 
contrast than intensity. Another research shows that the 
reflectance of the chlorophyll can be detected using specific 
wavelength spectrum, 680 nm. Due to these reasons, this 
research use contrast of the image with specific wavelength, 
680 nm. The hypothesis is that the ripe one has highest image 
contrast than other ones, due to the chlorophyll concentration 
of this FFB is high. The overripe fruit has lowest image 
contrast due to the chlorophyll concentration is low.

Another important parameter to assess the maturity level 
of oil palm FFB is the number of fruitlet detachment. Usually, 
this parameter is assessed visually by the grader. Another 
hypothesis of the research is this fruitlet detachment can be 
detected using texture analysis to differentiate the detached 
and undetached fruitlets in oil palm FFB. In this research, 
image contrast and skewness are used to estimate the maturity 
of the oil palm FFBs.

The input of the systems is a color image that is captured 
under light source in specific wavelength, 680 nm. The color 
image is converted to grayscale image. The next process is to 
measure the image contrast and skewness. The systems block

III. RESULTS 
The first approach to estimate oil palm FFB maturity is 
using contrast. The plot of the contrast training dataset is 
shown in Figure 4. From the plot, it can be seen that the range 
of contrast values of the unripe and ripe FFBs are wide. The 
data is classified using several classifiers in Matlab

Classification Learner App. The highest accuracy of this 
learning process is achieved using ensemble bagged trees, 
75%. The contrast testing dataset is classified using this 
classifier. The plot of the contrast testing dataset is shown in 
Figure 5. The accuracy of this classification process is 
64.94%.

As hypothesized, this study shows that the image contrast 
and combination of contrast-skewness in specific wavelength 
680 nm can be used to estimate the oil palm ripeness. Due to 
the accuracy of the skewness is the same as the combination 
of contrast-skewness, this research proposes the use of 
skewness of the 680 nm image to estimate oil palm FFB 
maturity. 
IV. CONCLUSIONS 
This research proposes a simple technique to estimate the 
oil palm maturity using LED in specific wavelength, 680 nm, 
as light source and digital cam-era to capture the image. This 
research explores the use of contrast and skewness of the 680 
nm image to estimate oil palm FFB maturity. The accuracy of 
the skewness is the same as the combination of contrast-
skewness, 68.83 %. Due to the result, this research proposes 
the use of skewness of the 680 nm image to estimate oil palm 
FFB maturity. The accuracy of the proposed systems is in 
between another research results in oil palm maturity 
detection. Nevertheless, the proposed systems introduced a 
simple oil palm grading technique using 680 nm LED and low 
computation to process the image (convert color to grayscale, 
contrast and skewness measurement). Due to these reasons, 
the 
proposed 
systems 
can 
be 
implemented 
using 
microcomputer such as Raspberry Pi or Beagle Board as 
computation resource to process the image. It will reduce the 
implementation cost. Another advantage is that the portable 
palm oil FFBs grading systems can be built using the proposed 
technique. 
The 
proposed 
systems 
required 
further 
investigation using large number of samples with different 
maturity, especially overripe FFB. The last one is to build 
training and testing datasets that the maturity level of oil palm 
FFB is determined using oil extraction rate parameter. For 
further development the system will be design like 
multispectral camera with more than one wavelength as its 
light source.
