Abstract—Even though Indonesia is the largest palm oil pro-
ducer in the world, but the productivity is very low compared to
other countries. The main research question is how to increase
productivity. In general, there are three ripeness criteria that are
used in Indonesia, i.e. unripe; ripe; and overripe. Several studies
have been done to improve the grading system. The common
method that can be used to assess the maturity level of FFB is
using a standard image processing technique. Using this image-
based assessment, several parameters can be obtained in more
detail, such as color; texture; and the number of fruitlets that
are detached from the FFB. Further research states that the FFB
image can be observed at several different wavelengths. This
study will focus on productivity improvement in the palm oil
mill through the development of an objective Fresh Fruit Bunch
(FFB) grading system. In this study, the multispectral image-
based is introduced to assess the level of FFB’s ripeness. There
are 19 LEDs with different wavelengths that are used as source
light, i.e. 615 nm, 635 nm, 615 nm, 660 nm, 680 nm, 710 nm,
730 nm, 750 nm, 765 nm, 780 nm, 810 nm, 830 nm, 850 nm, 870
nm, 890 nm, 910 nm, 940 nm, module 780 nm, module and LED
780 nm. Using these different source lights, the FFB is captured
using a camera to obtain the RGB image of the FFB. In this
research, HSV color space is introduced to assess the maturity
level of FFB. One of the main contributions of this study is that
the 940 nm wavelength gives the best result for determining the
maturity level of FFB.
Index Terms—HSV, multispectral image, ripeness grading,
palm oil FFB
I. INTRODUCTION
Every year, palm oil production is increasing due to the
higher demand from the consumer. Palm oil has the highest
yield compared to other vegetable oil, up to 5 to 10 times
higher than soybean and sunﬂower. Palm oil is obtained from
extracting oil from the Fresh Fruit Bunch (FFB). The quantity
and quality of palm oil are determined by the maturity level
of FFB. There are three types of palm oil, i.e. Nigrescens;
Virescens; and Oleifera [1]. In Indonesia, Nigrescens is com-
monly planted in palm oil plantation. In general, there are
three maturity levels of FFB, i.e. unripe; ripe; and overripe [2].
There are some parameters that can be observed to assess the
maturity level of FFB such as the color-changing of FFB and
the number of fruitlets that are detached from the bunch. This
study tries to address the main challenge of the maturity level
of FFB in Indonesia. The result of this study is expected to
improve Indonesian palm oil production and productivity. The
main challenge in assessing the maturity level of Nigrescens
FFB is the color of FFB is not changing during the maturity
process like Virescens. The common method to assess the
maturity level of Nigrescens is by calculating the number of
fruitlets that are detached from the FFB, which is very difﬁcult
to do.
There are several methods that have been developed to
assess the maturity level of palm oil FFB. One of the pro-
posed methods is utilizing the electromagnetic ﬁeld using a
microstrip ring resonator that operates in 2.2 and 3 GHz [3].
In recent studies, color analysis methods are introduced to
determine the maturity level of palm oil FFB [5], [6]. These
studies explore the possibility of utilization of different color
space to assess the palm oil FFB ripeness, such as the RGB;
HSV; I1I2I3; L*a*b*, XYZ, YCbCr, YIQ, and YUV. This
color analysis is can be used not only in the pre-processing
stage but also in determining the classiﬁcation features. Several
common classiﬁcation methods are already used to assess
the maturity level of palm oil FFB, such as artiﬁcial neural
networks (ANN) [1], [7], [8] and Support Vector Machine
(SVM) [7], and k-Nearest Neighbors (k-NN) [7]. Besides,
several studies also introduced the utilization of a multispectral
device or camera [5], [9]. Using this device, some FFB images
can be acquired using several speciﬁc wavelengths. The main
purpose of using multispectral image analysis is to observe
the image intensity in the several speciﬁc wavelengths in the
ultraviolet (UV), visible light, and infrared (IR). One of the
main advantages of this analysis is that more information
can be obtained, such as chemical compounds of the object.
In the assessment of the maturity level of palm oil FFB,
the hyperspectral camera is used to acquire the multispectral
images. Using these images, the amount of relative water
content in the palm oil FFB can be determined [5], [10].
In this study, we proposed the utilization of multispectral
images that are acquired from several LEDs in the various
speciﬁc wavelengths to assess the maturity level of Nigrescens
palm oil FFB. This study will explore the possibility of HSV
color space of multispectral images in the Nigrescens palm
oil grading. Through the development of Nigrescens palm oil
FFB multispectral image analysis in HSV, it is expected that
the proposed not only can improve productivity, but also the
efﬁciency in the palm oil industry.

II. METHODS
In this study, 24 palm oil FFBs are used, which consists of
14 unripe FFBs, 9 ripe FFBs, and 1 overripe FFB. Therefore,
in total there are 456 images that will be processed, which
consists of:
• Unripe FFB images: 14 (FFBs) × 19 (light source in
speciﬁc wavelength) = 266,
• Ripe FFB images: 9 (FFBs) × 19 = 171, and
• Overripe FFB images: 1 FFB × 19 = 19.

Even though the image acquisition process is conducted in
closed and dark conditions to minimize the inﬂuence from the
external light source, such as sun and lamplight, some images
still contain unwanted information, the background. To address
this issue, there are two proposed methods that will be used
in this study. The ﬁrst image processing method will process
images without pre-processing, this means that the image
background will also be processed. The second one is the pre-
processing will be applied to remove the image background.
There are three main steps in the pre-processing, the ﬁrst step
is grayscaling that convert RGB color image to grayscale. The
second one is thresholding. In this study, the minimum limit of
threshold value is set to 50. Therefore, the pixel that has value
≤50 will be set to 0. The last step is segmentation to get the
image mask that will be used to eliminate the background.
The block diagram of image background removal is shown
in Figure 2. The ﬁnal result of the background removal is the
palm oil FFB image without a background in RGB color space.
The next process is to convert the RGB image to HSV for both
palm oil FFB with or without background. The last process
of the proposed image processing method is calculating the
average value of the image pixel.

IV. CONCLUSIONS AND FURTHER DEVELOPMENT
Comparing to other 18 speciﬁc wavelengths as source light
to acquire multispectral images, the speciﬁc wavelength at 940
nm gives the best result in classifying the maturity level of
palm oil FFB using HSV color space in three classes, i.e.
unripe, ripe, and overripe. Furthermore, the performance of
the maturity level of palm oil FFB will improve if the class
is reduced into two classes, unripe and ripe. The result of
this study is in line with another study, speciﬁc wavelength
in about 940 nm can be used to detect the relative water
content in an object. Naturally, the ripe FFB has lower relative
water content compared to the unripe. It is conﬁrmed in the H
component graphics, the average pixel value ripe FFB is lower
than the unripe one. The 3D-plot gives more clearly the result
of this performance. In the speciﬁc wavelength at 940 nm,
the performance of the background removal process is quite
similar to the without background removal. Even though the
background removal has more process and computational load,
the system performance shows a similar result. Therefore, this
study recommends not to use the background removal process
in the proposed system due to the background of multispectral
images almost black, same as after removing background that
is set to black.
Further development will focus on increasing the number of
palm oil FFBs that are used as the dataset, especially for the
overripe FFB. Furthermore, the study will explore other color
spaces to improve the performance of the proposed method.
