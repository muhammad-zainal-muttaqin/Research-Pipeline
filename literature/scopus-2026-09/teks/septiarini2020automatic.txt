A B S T R A C T
Nowadays, computer vision-based automation systems in the ﬁeld of oil palm plantations are still being de-
veloped. The ripeness grading for quality control of oil palm fruit and the fruit picking robots are the im-
plementation examples of those systems. Such a system requires a pre-processing called segmentation.
Segmentation is a complex process and will be more challenging if the image of oil palm fruit is captured in the
natural background moreover with uneven brightness. In this work, the segmentation method of oil palm fruit is
proposed by applying the contour-based approach due to the oil palm fruit that has various shapes and colors.
This method implemented the Canny algorithm combining with several operations of morphology and re-
construction to remove the noise. The proposed method is evaluated using a local dataset consisting of 160
images with three kinds of ripeness grading (60 raw, 50 under-ripe, and 50 ripe). Overall, the average of seg-
mentation accuracy achieves of 90.13% with the error rates of false positive and false negative are 2.92% and
5.20%, respectively.
1. Introduction
Palm oil is one of the important plantation plants in the world due
to the ability to produce vegetable oil. Some derivative products from
both of them are edible products such as margarine, ice creams, bakery
fats, instant noodles, cocoa butter extenders, chocolate, cream biscuits,
ﬁlled milk, and sugar confectionery, as well as non-edible products such
as lubrication, cosmetic, soap, shampoo, detergent, textiles oils, and
biodiesel. Those products are daily necessity products, so the need for
palm oil will be increasing continually. Therefore, oil palm fruit pro-
duction along with the quality need to be improved. Nowadays, com-
puter vision-based automation systems are still being developed in the
plantation sector to improve the quality and production yield. Such
systems are a fruit picking equipment by a mobile platform for har-
vesting, a fruit detection system and a control system (Wang et al.,
2017).
In oil palm plantation, computer vision-based system has been de-
veloped such as to classify the ripeness grading of oil palm fruit
(Fadilah et al., 2012; Pamornnak et al., 2017; Shabdin et al., 2016),
detect palm disease based on the leaves (Yahya et al., 2018a) and the
stems (Khairunniza-Bejo and Vong, 2014; Yahya et al., 2018b). All
those systems require a segmentation process to reach the ﬁnal goal.
Segmentation is a complex and challenging process especially when it is
applied against the image with the natural (complex) background. This
process aims to classify the area of oil palm fruit and the background.
The complex background in the plantation area allows the oil palm fruit
to be mingled with other objects such as stems, leaves, soil, rocks or sky.
There are much of previous literature on segmentation has devel-
oped for other fruit images such as citrus (Lu, 2017; Maldonado and
Carlos, 2016; Ozturk et al., 2016; Thendral et al., 2014), apple
(Mizushima and Lu, 2013; Sun et al., 2018), tomato (Xiang, 2018), and
grape (Behroozi-Khazaei and Maleki, 2017; Luo et al., 2016; Pérez-
Zavala et al., 2018). Although the segmentation task is performed to
diﬀerent fruits, some of them implemented similar main methods.
Those methods may use as the reference to be applying to the image of
the oil palm fruit. The segmentation method of threshold-based applied
for citrus (Dorj et al., 2017; Lu, 2017; Maldonado and Carlos, 2016),
apple (Song and Yang, 2015), mango (Haﬀet al., 2013), dates palm (Al
Ohali, 2011), and in (Vasthi and Kusumaningrum, 2015) this method
performed for three kinds of fruit, they were tomatoes, apples, and
bananas. Likewise, the Otsu method which has been generally used to
handle the task of fruit segmentation (Avila et al., 2015; Lailee et al.,
2012; Li et al., 2011; Lv et al., 2019; Mizushima and Lu, 2013; Wei
et al., 2014).
Learning algorithm has been applied to segment the fruit as the
alternative approach. It used supervised and unsupervised learning. An

the color gradation edge detection approach has been applied for fruit
segmentation (Gongal et al., 2015) using Canny algorithm (Luo et al.,
2016; Thendral et al., 2014) and Sobel algorithm (Maldonado and
Carlos, 2016; Ozturk et al., 2016). The conversion process of color

results of fruit segmentation, such as converting RGB into HSV

nents. This process performed to make the fruit areas and background
separating more easily.
The objective of this work is to separate the oil palm fruit from
various kinds of background. This paper proposes an image segmenta-
tion method of oil palm fruit oil based on the contour using edge de-
tection and reconstruction operation to overcome the color gradation
that occurs on fruit and the complex detail in the background. This
proposed method shows that it is suitable and robust for the dataset
used in this work, especially if it will be continued for future work that
related to the ripeness grading classiﬁcation of the oil palm fruit.
2. Materials and methods
2.1. Dataset
The dataset is used to evaluate the performance of the proposed
method consisting three kinds of oil palm fruit images based on the
ripeness grading: raw (Fig. 1(a) and (b)), under-ripe (Fig. 1(c)), and ripe
(Fig. 1(d)). A total of 160 images (60 raw, 50 under-ripe, and 50 ripe)
acquired using smartphone digital camera (Samsung edge 7). In the
acquisition process, the capturing distance between the camera and the
oil palm fruit approximately 40–90 cm with two conditions: (1) outdoor
under natural illumination conditions (sunny days) and (2) indoor with
suﬃcient illumination. Those images captured in two types of back-
ground: simple or complex. Then, these are saved in red, green, blue
(RGB) color space and JPEG format with a resolution of 3264 × 2448
pixels. There are 52 and 108 of images with a simple and complex
background, respectively. The examples of images in the dataset as
shown in Fig. 1. Fig. 1 (a) and (b) present the simple background while
Fig. 1 (c) and (d) is the image with the complex one.
Additionally, the ground truth is formed to evaluate the perfor-
mance of the proposed method. The ground truth is created manually
using Adobe Photoshop CS6 as an image editing software. Fig. 1 (e)–(h)
shows the ground truth of the Fig. 1 (a)–(d).
2.2. The proposed method
The proposed method receives the oil palm fruit image as the input
data. There are three kinds of oil palm fruit based on the ripeness
grading (raw, under-ripe, and ripe) with various colors, shapes, and
backgrounds. This method consists of three main processes: (1) locali-
zation of oil palm fruit, (2) pre-processing, and (3) segmentation, where
each main process consists of several steps. Fig. 2 depicts the state
diagram of the proposed method including several examples of input
data and the process sequence with the resulting image of the main
process.
2.2.1. Localization of oil palm fruit
This process aims to form a sub-image that focuses on presenting the
oil palm fruit area. This sub-image is called the region of interest (ROI)
image. The ROI images that are formed have various size as shown in
Fig. 2(a). The size of them are smaller than the original image due to
the non-fruit area (background) that has been discarded. The forming of
the ROI image reduced the computational processing time and even
optimize the results of the following process. The localization process
initially applied to resize step to reduce the original image size to
640 × 480 pixels for computational eﬃciency (Lailee et al., 2012; Lu
et al., 2018; Zhuang et al., 2018). Hence the existence of oil palm fruit
in an image can be identiﬁed based on the color, therefore, the rough
area of oil palm fruit obtained by converting RGB color space into the
Lab. Lab color space based on "a" space is chosen because of its ap-
propriate to predict the potential area of fruit with the complex back-
ground (Thendral et al., 2014). The color space conversion of RGB to
Lab is deﬁned as follows (Jidong et al., 2016):

2.2.3. Segmentation
Segmentation is a challenging process that aims to separate the
areas of oil palm fruit and backgrounds in an image due to the fruits are
captured in the complex background or the ununiform illumination. Oil
palm fruit has various color which depends on the ripeness grading as
well as the shape. The fruit in the image with the complex background
may have a similar color with other objects. Therefore, in this research,
the segmentation implemented based on the contour using edge de-
tection. The edges occur because of the signiﬁcant diﬀerence between
the neighbor pixels (Garcia-Lamont et al., 2018).
Canny detection is an edge detection method that has been widely
applied in various cases to solve segmentation problems (Garcia-
Lamont et al., 2018), especially for fruit segmentation (Luo et al., 2016;
Thendral et al., 2014). In this work, Canny operation preceded with
Gaussian smoothing (Thendral et al., 2014). The result diﬀerence of
Canny detection from an ROI image (Fig. 4 (a)) between those not
applying the smoothing process and vice versa is shown in Fig. 4 (b)
and (c). Fig. 4(b) shows that the results of edge detection without
preceding by the smoothing process causes more details of background
occuring than with the applying one (Fig. 4(c)).

using three evaluation indices including segmentation accuracy (Sc)
(Sun et al., 2018), false positive error (FPe) and false negative error

the overlapping fruit area between obtained by the method and the
ground truth. FPe is used to evaluate the error rate of the background
area classiﬁed as the fruit area by the method, while FNe to evaluate the
error rate of the fruit area is classiﬁed as background by the method.

should have a high value of Sc. Conversely, it should have a low value

4. Conclusion
This works developed a segmentation method of oil palm fruit based
on the contour using Canny edge detection. Afterward, the morphology
operation continuing with reconstruction operation was applied to re-
move the noises. The proposed method is robust to segmented the
multi-colored oil palm fruit and eliminates more background areas
successfully. This method has been evaluated using a local dataset
consisting of 160 images with various background (simple and com-
plex) with three kinds of ripeness grading (raw, under-ripe and ripe).
The performance of the proposed method achieves Sc, FPe, and FNe of
90.13%, 2.92%, and 5.20%, respectively. Those values indicate the
error tends to produce under-segmentation result (the fruit area is de-
tected as the background) since the value of FPe is lower than FNe.
Incorrect segmentation occurs more frequently in the images containing
the multi-colored oil palm fruit, complex background, and non-uniform
of illumination conditions. However, for future work to the classiﬁca-
tion stage of ripeness grading, the ripeness grading of the fruit area
which detected correctly is still possible to be recognized.
