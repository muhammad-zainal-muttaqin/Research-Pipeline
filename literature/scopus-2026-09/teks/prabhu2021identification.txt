Abstract:
Identification of fruit and determining the 
location is a time consuming complex task. Many works have 
been proposed to determine the fruit. In our work we have 
proposed a framework that segments the fruit from the 
background , determines the location of the fruit and counts 
the fruit that helps in yield estimation. 
Similarity 
index 
is 
determined 
that 
measures 
the 
correlation between the segmented output and the ground 
truth image. The overlapping fruits are identified their 
contours are detected. Further fruit count algorithm is applied. 
The similarity measure ranges between 71.5 % - 92.4 % and 
highest accuracy of fruit count is observed for Apples and 
Tomato fruit images which is 97.5%. 
Keywords - Segmentation , MArker Controlled watershed , 
Hough Transform 
I.
INTRODUCTION 
Many fruit harvesting robots have been designed to 
overcome the labour cost and time consuming process of
manual fruit harvest. Transformation from labour intensive 
to technology driven facility is essential. Computer Vision 
system in precision agriculture have displayed promising 
prospects with increase in efficiency [1]. These systems 
guide the arm of the fruit harvesting robot, by sensing in the 
real working environments .The environment poses a
challenge such as densely arranged branches, occluded fruits, 
varied illumination, changing object appearances[2]. Hence 
the rate 
of success in identification of the fruit is essential during 
the harvesting. Many works have been carried on 
segmentation of fruits.[3] has used OTSU dynamic threshold 
segmentation method to recognise apples and achieved 86% 
accuracy for fruits occluded by branches. Edge detection and 
improved Hough transformation is also used. [4] have used a 
deep 
convolutional neural network (DCNN) for detecting in 
house mature and immature strawberries under natural 
lighting conditions with 88.03% average precision. [5] have 
used improved HSV and watershed algorithm achieving 81.6 
% accuracy.[6] have carried out works on identifying chillies 
by converting image to Lab, applying masked image , 
morphological operations, achieving 92.7% accuracy. [7] 
have used. Convolution Neural Network for segmentation of 
strawberry 
instances. 
Also, 
the 
ripeness 
level 
is 
determined.[8] recognise the green pepper based on the  
manifold ranking, Further morphological operations are 
applied on 500 images that have achieved 83.6% accuracy. 
II.
METHODOLOGY 
The proposed approach is divided into three main phases 
a) Segmentation b) Identification of fruit region c) Fruit 
Count. Segmentation approach is proposed to separate the 
various fruit images with complex background. The images 
also include uneven lighting conditions followed by 
occlusions , leaves and branches. The approach is designed 
to detect the mature fruits .The second phase includes the 
identification and separation of
fruits regions using 
watershed algorithm and the final stage is the fruit count 
using Circular hough transform which helps in yield 
estimation. The overall flow diagram of the proposed 
approach is as shown in Fig 1. 
Fig. 1. Flow diagram of proposed approach 
In Fig 1, At the initial stage the image is converted into 
L*a*b color space, followed by segmentation to remove 
background , the fruit regions are then identified and 
overlapping fruits are detected that is required for fruit count. 
245 sample data images are collected from the google source 
out of which 40 images are apple, 20 bell pepper, 40 cherry, 
15 chilly, 30 pomegranate, 30 raspberry, 30 strawberry and 
40 images of tomato, the images were taken with complex 
background, varied lighting environments. The images also 
include with illumination with mature and immature fruits 
and the approach is designed to detect only mature fruits. 
The sample data set collected for 8 fruits is shown in Fig. 2.

partition an image into multiple parts or segments, often into 
foreground and background with foreground labeled as 
region of interest and background as the non essential 
information of the image. Each region having similar pixel 
intensities. The workflow diagram of the proposed 
segmentation approach is shown in Fig. 3

The proposed segmentation approach is divided into 3
phases steps a) Conversion to Lab colour space b) Apply 
thresholding 3) RGB masking .In step a) the RGB images are 
converted into Lכ aכ b color space since Lab express a wider 
colour gamut than RGB and it has the capability to detect the 
fruit region with complex background appropriately. The 
color space conversion of RGB to Lab is defined as follows 
L=0.2126R + 0.7152G+0.0722B 
a=
1.479(0.2213R-0.339G+0.1177B)+128 
b=
0.6245(0.1949R+0.6057G-0.8006B)+128 
Known to be more perceptually linear than other color 
space , it extends its capability to separate luminosity from 
color, and includes all the colours that are beyond the human 
perception and enhance the colour micro variations. 
In step 2, the three colour channels are separated and min 
and max thresholds , Lmin , Lmax , amin , amax , bmin and 
bmax thresholds are determined based on histogram settings, 
which will help to convert the image from color to binary. 
Lmin= 0.000; Lmax= 100.000; 
amin = 19.685; amax = 74.845;  
bmin = -15.533; bmax= 67.881; 
In sterp 3) RGB masking is applied. Then the selected 
thresholds were applied on L, a, and b respectively. L,a,b and 
value mask is determined calculated and applied on each 
colour component. For L mask, L value is set at less and 
equal to low hue threshold and high hue threshold. While for 
a mask and b mask, a and b value was kept bigger and equal 
to the low threshold and high threshold respectively. Further, 
Lab masks were combined to detect the red parts of the 
image. Thus the background is removed and colour fruit 
region is extracted. 
Marker Controlled Watershed Algorithm Determining 
the exact location of the fruits especially when they are 
touching is essential. The fruit harvesting robot requires the 
location of the fruits. Normally on plants fruits are not
separated, they touch each other. Separating the touching 
fruits has always posed a challenge and the watershed 
transform is often applied to overcome this problem. 
However, watershed displays over segmentation and marker 
controlled method is applied. Watershed is applied directly 
on the gradient magnitude. The overall flow diagram of
MArker Controlled Watershed Algorithm is as shown in Fig. 
4. Morphological operations erosion is applied to remove the 
non fruit regions. This image is considered as the marker 
image. Next the mask image is defined , flood filling will be 
constrained to spread only to foreground pixels in the mask 
image and the original segmented image is the reconstruction 
mask. Thus, keeping original image as marker and eroded 
image as mask is opening by reconstruction. Regional 
maxima is used to obtain foreground markers. Futher the 
marker image is superimposed on the original image. In
order to highlight the shadowed objects, the edges have to be 
cleaned. Further the background markers are used and 
thresholding is applied. Further the watershed ridge lines are 
detected using the watershed transform. Next step is to 
determine the regional minima in desired locations. Finally 
watershed based segmentation is computed. For visualization 
purpose, label matrices are displayed such that outputs are 
converted to true colour images. 
Fig. 4. Overall procedure adopted fo r Marker Controlled Watershed 
Algorithm 
B. Fruit Count 
Flow diagram to count the fruits is shown in Fig 5.The 
phases included for this approach are segmented into 4 stages 
a) canny edge detection b) contour detection c) circular 
Hough transform d) counting fruits. The sharp changes in the 
intensity values are determined through canny edge .The 
next step is to determine the contours of the detected edges 
.For visibility , the cotours are white on black background. 
Further , the circular Hough transform is applied to detect the 
circular contours.

1)
Edge Detection 
Edge detection is basically used to reduce the information 
in the image. Canny edge detection is considered as the one 
of the popular approaches for edge detection. It involves 5
steps i) Smoothing - applied to reduce blur ii) Finding 
gradients- Edges are marked in areas such that the gradients 
of the image have large magnitudes.iii) Non maximum 
suppression - local maxima are marked as edges and all the 
ixels that less that it are removed. iv) thresholding - edge 
pixels that are created due to noise or with weak gradient is 
to be erased. This is solved using thresholding v) Edge 
tracking by hysteresis- Final edges are obtained by 
suppressing remaining edges that are connected to a very 
certain or strong edge. 
2)
Countours 
Contours are set of pixels connected to each other on the 
outline of the objects.By taking the output of the canny edge 
, it determines the boundaries of objects , make a hierarchy 
of the contours to keep the record of the holes inside the 
parent objects. 
3)
Hough Transform 
It is used to detect the circular patterns .IT is designed 
using voting process that transforms the edge points to 
parameter space.For each feature point the votes are 
calculated and stored in array. The presence of shape is 
detected if the array has highest number of votes.The circle is 
determined by the equation

B. Diced Index 
The Dice similarity coefficient, is used that compares the 
ground truth with the output of the segmented image. The 
ground truth are prepared using the photoshop software for 
all the fruit images. This image is binary image which is then
compared with the segmented output. Diced index values of 
5 sample images from 8 various fruit images are given in 
Table 1. 
The dice index is calculated for all the fruit images. It is 
found that for Apple, Pomogranate, Tomato and cherry have 
similarity index of 82.1 % , 80.2 % , 87.7 % and 82.4 %
accuracy. For Bell pepper , Chilli , Rasberry and Strawberry 
the dice index is 74.4 % , 70.3% , 73.2 % and 71.5 %
respectively. The dice co-efficient for circular fruits provide 
better accuracy since the structing element used in 
morphological operation is disk. However the other fruits have 
convex shape

watershed and finally counting the fruits detected.
