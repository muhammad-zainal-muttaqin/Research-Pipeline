Detection and counting of overlapped apples
based on convolutional neural networks
Mengyuan Gaoa,b, Shunagbao Mab, Yapeng Zhanga,b,∗and Yong Xueb
aHubei Key Laboratory of Digital Textile Equipment, Wuhan Textile University, Wuhan, China
bSchool of Mechanical Engineering and Automation, Wuhan Textile University, Wuhan, China
Abstract. Automatic identiﬁcation picking robot is an important research content of agricultural modernization development.
In order to overcome the difﬁculty of picking robots for accurate visual inspection and positioning of apples in a complex
orchard, a detection method based on an instance segmentation model is proposed. To reduce the number of model parameters
and improve the detection speed, the backbone feature extraction network is replaced from the Resnet101 network to the
lightweight GhostNet network. Spatial Pyramid Pooling (SPP) module is used to increase the receptive ﬁeld to enhance the
semantics of the output network. Compared with Resnet101, the parameter quantity of the model is reduced by 90.90%, the
detection speed is increased from 5 frames/s to 10 frames/s, and the detection speed is increased by 100%. The detection
result is that the accuracy rate is 91.67%, the recall rate is 97.82%, and the mAP value is 91.68%. To solve the repeated
detection of fruits due to the movement of the camera, the Deepsort algorithms was used to solve the multi-tracking problems.
Experiments show that the algorithm can effectively detect the edge position information and categories of apples in different
scenes. It can be an automated apple-picking robot. The vision system provides strong technical support.
Keywords: Instance segmentation, apple detection, GhostNet, Spatial Pyramid Pooling
1. Introduction
Apples are deeply loved by people because of their
richnutrition,minerals,vitaminsanddietaryﬁberand
Northern China is the main producing area of apples
[1]. However, due to the complexity of the picking
process apple picking consumes a lot of labor, and
accounts for about 40% of the whole apple produc-
tion workload [2–4]. Therefore, using apple picking
robot instead of traditional manual picking has great
practical signiﬁcance and a wide range of application
scenarios [5–7], which is conducive to promoting the
intelligent development of apple production and how
to quickly and accurately detect apples in orchards is
one of the key technologies of apple picking robot [8].
However, in the actual ﬁeld environment, the visual

In recent years, some progress has been made in
the research of Apple’s robot visual inspection and
recognition. Fan et al. [9] proposed a multi-feature
patch-based segmentation technique using k-means,
which can effectively segment apples in orchards in
real-time. Liu et al. [10] extracted candidate regions
using color space and detected and located apples in
candidate regions using hog features, which achieved
good detection results in terms of speed and accu-
racy. Li Da et al. [11] extracted the color features of
green apples using HSV color space and classiﬁed
them using support vector machine (SVM) classiﬁer,
which effectively realized the recognition and loca-
tion of green apples. Zhang et al. [12] used RGB color
space segment fruits and leaves from the background,
constructed a random forest classiﬁer to recognize

With the rise of artiﬁcial intelligence technology,
target detection technology based on deep learning
is gradually applied to the ﬁeld of apple detection.
Xing Zhang et al. [13] replaced VGG-16 network in
SSD model with Resnet-101 network, and realized
the detection of four different fruits using transfer

overlapping apples in orchards. Fu et al. [15] used the
YOLOv3 network to detect the apple target area in the
environment, and used the color space for threshold
segmentation to obtain the accurate location informa-
tion of the apple. Wu et al. [16] and Yang et al. [17]
used YOLOv4 and YOLOv5 to improve the detec-
tion speed and accuracy of the model. In addition, the
RCNN model [18] with two-level detection is supe-
rior to other target detection algorithms in accuracy.
Although the above methods have achieved good
real-time and robustness in orchard apple detection,
the large amount of model parameters leads to exces-
sive consumption of computing resources of apple
picking robot. Besides, only the rectangular frame
is used for apple detection and the closed detection
cannot effectively predict the edge position and shape
information of apple.
Although Apple Inspection has achieved good
inspection speed and accuracy, there are some prob-
lems worth considering, such as£º(i) ignore the
category of apples, just identify a single variety; (ii)
whenthepickingrobotismoving,itrepeatedlyrecog-
nizes the apples, which makes it difﬁcult to uniquely
identify each apple during the picking operation.
Therefore, aiming at the problems existing in the
above literature, this paper proposes a visual position-
ing and detection model of apple picking robot based
on instance segmentation network Mask RCNN [19].
The model proposed in this paper can accurately pre-
dict the edge and shape information of picking fruit.
In addition, based on the Mask RCNN, the backbone
network is replaced with a GhostNet network model
suitable for mobile terminals to improve the detec-
tion speed. Further, in order to enhance the semantic
information and make the model more robust to the
complex orchard environment, SPP network is used
for feature extraction. And the Deepsort algorithm
is used to track the detected fruits to prevent over-
counting detections.
The rest of the paper is organized as follows. In
Section 2 we present the general architecture of this
work: the data set acquisition, the CNN approaches
under evaluation, the fruit counting and tracking sys-
tem and data management. Results for fruit detection
on images, and multi-object tracking and counting for
frame sequences are presented in Section 3. Finally,
the discussion is presented in Section 4, and the Sum-
mary and Conclusions in Section 5.
2. Materials and methods
2.1. Data processing
2.1.1. Data acquisition
There are a large number of apple images on
the Internet, Python crawler software is utilized to
obtain batch acquisition to reduce the cost of manual
collection and improve the efﬁciency of the image
collection. To ensure the quality of the collected
images, manual removal of unclear, duplicate, sam-
ple data of a signal apple in the image. And different
test conditions were set and recorded, such as long-
distance and short-distance, light intensity at different
angles, occlusion of fruit leaves and branches, over-
lap between fruits, mixed fruits of different mature
fruits, and so on. The speciﬁcal details are shown in
Fig. 1.
2.1.2. Data augmentation
Because the size of the collected images is incon-
sistent, the neural network needs a ﬁxed size for
training, and the image size is uniformly adjusted
to 512 × 512 to facilitate the training of the model.
Mask RCNN uses a fully convolutional network to
review the mask of a sad image. When the target is
segmented, the image needs to be converted to an 8-
bit grayscale image, the label of the background is set
to 0, and the pixel value of target 1 is 1. The image
value of target 2 is set to 2, and the pixel value of
target n is set to n where n represents the nth target.

2.3. GhostNet-Mask RCNN model
2.3.1. Feature extractor
The
original
Mask

extraction network. This network can effectively
extract features and achieve good detection results
in actual detection. However, there are too many net-
work layers and the model parameters are huge. In the
extraction process, the amount of calculation param-
eters is large, it needs to occupy too much hardware
detection resources of the picking robot, which takes
a long time, and the detection speed is too slow.
Neural networks have powerful feature extrac-
tion capabilities. As the number of network layers
and parameters increase, the better the model effect,
but the higher the detection speed and hardware
requirements. In order to accelerate the speed of
model detection, many researchers have proposed a
lightweight network structure.
Due to the large workload of agricultural apple
picking,itisnecessarytohaveahighreal-timeperfor-
mance during apple positioning detection to quickly
carry out agricultural production work. Therefore, it
is necessary to improve the number of model param-
eters to increase the detection speed. Han et al. [22]
proposed a lightweight network GhostNet specially
used for embedded development in 2020 to improve
the detection speed, which used ghost module to
replace traditional convolutional architecture with
less calculation and faster inference speed. Found by
previous studies on neural networks£¬there is some
similar content in the feature map after convolution
which seems to be redundancy but it plays an impor-
tantroleinfeatureextraction.Therefore,thetwosteps
of GhostNet are as follows: (i) fewer convolutional
kernels are used to generate part of feature maps; (ii)
cheap linear operation which adapt the on the gener-
ated feature map is used to obtain another part of the
feature map, then the two feature maps are superim-
posed in dimension. To improve the performance of
Mask RCNN in accuracy and efﬁciency to adapt to
the model architecture, the backbone network Resnet
isreplacedwithGhostNet,andﬁvebranchoutputsare
used in the model of which four are used to detection
and classiﬁcation the apple and the last branch is used
to segment the apple in the background.
2.3.2. Semantic enhancement module
The Mask RCNN model performs the semantic
segmentation on the detected target in 14 × 14 rectan-
gular boxes, which could lead to the loss of semantic
information, and poor performance in accuracy
because of lower receptive ﬁeld. The SPP network

2.4. Multi-object tracking
Due to the reciprocating motion of the camera in
the detection of apples, repeated detection of some
fruits is caused, which makes the picking work dif-
ﬁcult. Many researchers have proposed solutions to
the problem of repeated detection of fruits [24–26].
The Multi-Object tracking algorithms based on deep
learning has strong robustness and rea-time per-
formance. Therefore, this paper uses the Deepsort
algorithms based on deep learning and Kaltman ﬁl-
ters to track the detection fruits of the Mask RCNN
model in real time.

3.3. Comparison with other models
To further verify the performance of the model,
YOLOv4, Faster RCNN and SSD models are used
for comparison.
As shown in Fig. 8, SSD and YOLOv4 are single-
stage target detection algorithms, which are due to
the two-stage Faster RCNN and Mask RCNN mod-
els in terms of speed. In terms of detection accuracy,
because the two-stage detection network adopts two
classiﬁcation and detection strategies, the detection
accuracy is better than single-stage target detec-
tion. For mutual occlusion between fruits and tree
branches, Mask RCNN can perform identiﬁcation
and detection compared with other detection mod-
els. It has strong robustness and is very suitable for
orchard fruit detection. In order to further evaluate the
performance of different models, the same evaluation
index is used to measure the performance of the four
models, as shown in Fig. 9. It can be seen that the map
and F1 scores of the Mask RCNN model are higher
than other detection models, the detection accuracy is
slightly lower than YOLOv4, and the Recall perfor-
mance is slightly lower than Faster RCNN. Overall,
Mask RCNN has the best performance as an Apple
detection model.
