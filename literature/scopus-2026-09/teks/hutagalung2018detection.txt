Abstract— In Agriculture, fruit farmers would be very helpful if 
they can monitor and estimate the yield before harvesting the 
fruits so that they can optimize and use the materials required 
more efficiently such as water consumption, fertilizers, and other 
agricultural chemical substances for every different location. 
This study proposed a method for detecting and counting the 
number of mangos in occluded conditions by evaluating the color 
filter and identifying the specific characteristics of fruit such as 
the homogeneity of fruit surface. This study fully made use of the 
information extracted from the created blobs after conducting 
histogram filtering such as blob weighting, evaluating the blob 
gradient topography and performing a hierarchical clustering. 
This method had a lower efficiency cost and did not need to 
determine the number of clusters to be searched.  The function of 
this method was also improved by providing the information of 
the position and the number of fruits in the result images. This 
information could be used to make a precise detection. The 
images used in this experiment were 150 mango images divided 
into 30 training images and 120 testing images. The results of the 
experiments showed this method was able to detect mango, 
precision and false rates up to: 97.53%, 99.28%, and 0.72%, 
respectively. In general, the result of this study presented the 
total number of fruit detected by system of 646 images as the 
True Positive conditions from the total of 705 fruits, with an 
overall ratio of Recall, Precision, False rate of 91.63%; 97.88%, 
2.12% respectively.   
Keywords—mango detection and counting; fruit occlusion detection; 
distance transform. 
I. 
INTRODUCTION 
A number of researches [1]  have been conducted to support 
the farmers and agricultural industries in identifying, 
localizing, predicting yield of the fruit trees and developing 
automatic fruit harvesting systems. However, numerous 
researches of fruits identification being conducted by 
[2][3][4][5] were faced with a very challenging issues of 
unpredictable outdoor conditions that still need to be 
addressed, such as: varying illumination, fruits occlusion, 
fruits overlapping and color similarity of fruits with its 
background. In conjunction, green mango detection within 
green canopy and occluded forms have become a very difficult 
issues that previous researchers [6][7] were still trying to 
overcome (Fig. 1).   
In this research, a technique is proposed to detect and counting 
green mangoes in the green leaves canopy under occluded 
conditions.

IV. CONCLUSION   
 
This study proposed a method for detecting and counting 
mango fruit in occluded conditions by evaluating the color 
filter and the local property of fruit that can be extracted such 
as the homogeneity of fruit surface. To address the issue of 
green mango fruit’s detection in the green leaves canopy, our 
method performed histogram intensity thresholding on each 
RGB color channel and morphological operations for 
segmenting fruits object from its background. In order to 
address fruit counting under occluded condition issue, we 
proposed a distance weighting mechanism to the blobs created 
after histogram filtering and performing a hierarchical 
clustering, that has a lower efficiency cost and does not 
require us to determine the number of clusters to be searched. 
This study also improved the functionality of the method by 
adding the information of the fruit position and showed 
automatically the number of fruits which was not discussed in 
previous work. This information was able to be used to track 
the precision of the detection and counting.  
The image used in this experiment is 150 images of mango 
that divided into 30 training images and 120 testing images. In 
the case of clear appearance of mango, experimental results 
show that mango detection rate, counting rate and false rate 
are up to: 97.53%, 99.28%, and 0.72%, respectively. By 
overall, using the proposed method, the total number of fruit 
detected by system was 646 images as True Positive 
conditions from the total of 705 fruits, with overall ratio of 
detection rate, counting rate, false rate: 91.63%; 97.88%, 
2.12% respectively. 
V. FUTURE WORKS 
This method has a several limitation in detecting and 
counting fruit object that cannot be done in over-exposure 
lighting conditions since the local property of fruit such as 
non-reflective fruit surface cannot be extracted, and it cannot 
be applied in night times’ conditions.  
This study requires the selection of an optimum value of 
several thresholds that generated empirically from the pre-
experiment and examining the distribution of fruits, in which 
the first threshold plays important role in affecting the 
capability of the next thresholds. Therefore, to improve 
detection capabilities, more studies need to be done in more 
depth on the basic method of color features, shape features and 
texture features that requires no static parameter settings. 
Furthermore, by involving human visual in developing both 
ground truth dataset and human verifications, it causes the 
counting rate becomes time consuming and in-efficient. 
Further studies are needed to develop ground truth validation 
which is expected to improve the precision of the system 
performance. Since our method only using color space 
features and does not requires a model (unsupervised), 
detection of another object by this method tends to be an 
interesting work to be explored.
