Using K-Means Clustering Algorithm

Abstract. The maturity level of Fresh Fruit Bunches (FFB) palm oil affects Oil Extraction Rate (OER). With ripe 
conditions, the FFB of palm oil will produce a high OER, while unripe FFB palm oil will produce less oil. This study aims 
to compare the results of three methods of measuring distance on K-Means Clustering to detect the maturity level of oil 
palm FFB based on fruit colour using K-Means Clustering. K-Means Clustering is a grouping method based on the closest 
mean value (cluster centroid) from n objects to k clusters. K-Means Clustering is relatively easy to apply because the 
calculation process is simple. Still, the accuracy depends on the chosen distance measurement method because the results 
of the clustering process will produce different clusters if the distance measure used is different. This study compares three 
measuring data distances to the cluster's centre: Euclidean, Manhattan, and Chebyshev Distance. The optimality testing of 
three measurement methods using the Silhouette Coefficient Technique. This method is applied to classify the maturity 
level of Oil Palm FFB based on RGB images. The data used in this study were 120 images of oil palm FFB from four 
maturity levels, namely unripe, half-ripe, ripe, and overripe—the initial cluster centre for RGB determined by calculating 
the colour index. Colour feature extraction was obtained based on the mean value of each colour index. As a result, the 
Chebyshev Distance-Silhouette Coefficient is the most optimal for use in the case of grouping the maturity of Oil Palm 
FFB with K-Means Clustering. 
INTRODUCTION 
The estimated ripeness of palm fruit is an essential component in managing the palm oil harvest, as it will lead to 
the profitability and selling power of the product [1]. The OER of FFB of palm oil depends mainly on its maturity [2]. 
With ripe conditions, the FFB of palm oil will produce a high OER, while unripe FFB palm oil will produce less oil. 
This research will present the classification method between FFB of palm oil into unripe, half-ripe, ripe, and overripe. 
The process of detecting the ripeness of palm oil will have difficulty if manually [1]. Farmers have difficulty reaching 
the fruit to detect ripeness with the eyes when the palm tree has been tall. It needs a system that can detect the maturity 
level of palm oil FFB based on colour [3]. RGB data from images are processed using artificial intelligence algorithms 
to cluster the maturity level of palm oil. The K-Means Clustering method is one of the artificial intelligence algorithms 
to classify FFB palm oil maturity levels.  
The K-Means Clustering algorithm clusters the maturity level of FFB palm oil based on images. Data in RGB 
numbers will be categorised into four categories: unripe, half-ripe, ripe, and overripe. Previous research has addressed 
the use of K-Means Clustering for the classification of image data, i.e. for grouping tomato leaf types [4], and flower 
classification [5], and also classification of blueberries [6]. [6] The advantages of the K-Means Clustering include 
grouping large objects and object outliers very quickly, thus speeding up the grouping process. The disadvantage of 
K-Means Clustering is susceptible to the random generation of starting points and allows a group to have no members. 
Therefore, this study will compare three methods of measuring the distance of the data to the centre of the cluster, 
namely the Euclidean distance, Manhattan distance, and Chebyshev distance methods. Distance measurement is used

METHOD 
The research method describes the Clustering of the k-means method and oil palm data. It includes the image 
feature extraction and the clustering processes into four maturity levels of oil palm FFB. The oil palm data is from oil 
palm plantations in East Kalimantan and North Kalimantan 
Palm Oil 
The palm oil, Elaeis guineensis Jacq, is categorised as monocots and comes from moist tropical forests in Central 
and West Africa, as in Figure 1. As an industrial crop for vegetable oil products, the plant is widespread in East-South 
Asia, especially Indonesia and Malaysia. Both countries provide a large supply of palm oil for use in the international 
market. A critical factor in the success of palm oil production is the selection of good crops to increase genetic value 
and eliminate unwanted alleles. Materials from today's plants are three times more productive than 30 years ago. 
The colour difference of palm fruit's exocarp (outer layer) is caused by pigmentation classified into three types: 
nigrescens, virescens, and albescens. Palm fruit is found naturally in some forests. The Nigrescens type is the most 
common of the three. Although found as a small population, the characteristics of virescens are reported to be 
dominant. Palm oil producers, including Indonesia and Malaysia, used nigrescens and later virescens. Other plants 
with different exocarp colours are reported in dates [7].

The characteristic extraction is done to get RGB (Red, Green, Blue) values contained in the image. RGB values 
themselves serve as parameters in K-Means Clustering's processes. The K-Means Clustering carries out the process 
of detecting the maturity of palm oil TBS. There are four maturity levels: unripe, half-ripe, ripe, and overripe. Before 
entering into the calculation, the first step is determining the centroid or centre of the cluster.  
In this study, the distance calculation between the data and centroid is measured using three methods. They are 
Euclidean Distance, Manhattan Distance, and Chebyshev Distance. The clustering process is done after finding each 
distance of data with the centroid cluster. The following process tests the clustering result using Silhouette Coefficient. 
It is used to determine the best distance measurement method.

CONCLUSION 
Based on the research and discussions that have been done, it was concluded that Clustering the maturity level of 
palm fruit gets results that can recognise the level of ripeness of palm fruit based on colour. The data used amounted 
to 120 with 80 training image data and 40 test image data. Based on the test results, the accuracy produced by the 
application in detecting palm fruit maturity is 90% with the amount of 36 accurate data and 10% with the amount of 
4 inaccurate data. Silhouette Coefficient testing using the K-Means Clustering on each distance measure follows 
Euclidean Distance Silhouette Coefficient value is 0.232149, Manhattan Distance Silhouette Coefficient value is 
0.240016, Chebyshev Distance Silhouette Coefficient value is 0.242821. The most optimal distance measure to use in 
the case of palm oil FFB maturity grouping is Chebyshev Distance. 
For further research, other clustering and distance measurement methods can be used to cluster the FFB palm oil. 
The clustering method for detecting the ripeness level of FFB palm oil can be applied to hardware in combination 
with the Internet of Things (IoT).
