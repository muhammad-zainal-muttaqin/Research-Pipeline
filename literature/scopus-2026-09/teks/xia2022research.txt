Abstract
It is beneficial for growers and breeders to 
optimize the planting process, to make better decision on the 
production process, and to improve the fruit quality by 
predicting and estimating the yield of Xanthoceras sorbifolium 
Bunge fruit accurately. Due to the lack of automatic counting of 
Xanthoceras sorbifolium Bunge, this paper proposed a counting 
method based on deep learning, thus predicting and estimating 
the fruit yield of Xanthoceras sorbifolium Bunge quickly and 
effectively, and promoting the development of Xanthoceras 
sorbifolium 
Bunge 
planting 
industry. 
Experiments 
were 
conducted with deep learning models including SSD, YOLOv2, 
YOLOv2-tiny, YOLOv3, YOLOv3-tiny, YOLOv4,YOLOv5s and 
YOLOv5x , respectively, and the optimal model was selected. In 
view of the similar color of Xanthoceras sorbifolium Bunge fruit 
and leaves, in order to improve the accuracy of detection and 
counting, first of all, the experiment was to improve the contrast 
of the original data set twice. Secondly, the data set of green 
Malus pumila Mill and Pyrus spp cultivars with similar fruit type 
was mixed with the data set of Xanthoceras sorbifolium Bunge 
for data augmentation.  In order to further study the influence of 
different flight modes of UAV on the prediction results, a 
comparative test was conducted on three flight modes. There is 
the optimal performance of YOLOv3, with the IOU 98.84%, and 
there is the highest counting accuracy of YOLOV3-tiny, up to 
98.05%. Moreover, the YOLOv3-tiny model is suitable for the 
counting statistics of Xanthoceras sorbifolium Bunge data not 
only in large area collected by UAV, but also in local area 
collected by daily mobile phone, so that it is suitable for 
predicting and estimating the yield of Xanthoceras sorbifolium 
Bunge. Appropriately increasing the image contrast can improve 
the accuracy of the detection and counting of Xanthoceras 
sorbifolium Bunge fruit, but if the contrast increases too much, 
the detection accuracy will decrease. The accuracy of detection 
and counting can also be improved by training with mixed fruit 
data sets of similar color and fruit shape. The best value of 
recognition and counting accuracy can be achieved by the two 
modes of lateral flight and vertical flight, respectively. The 
identification and counting method based on deep learning can 
realize the automatic, accurate and efficient estimation of the 
fruit yield, and overcome the shortcomings of artificial fruit 
counting, such as missing number, repeated counting and heavy 
workload. 
Keywords
Xanthoceras sorbifolium Bunge, deep learning, 
fruit recognition and counting, UAV, contrast, mixed data set 
I. INTRODUCTION  
As a kind of woody oil tree in China, Xanthoceras 
sorbifolium Bunge has great value in economy, medicine, 
ecology and ornamental gardening. However, the low fruiting 
rate of Xanthoceras sorbifolia Bunge leads to the limitation of 
industrial development. Accurately predicting and estimating 
the yield of Xanthoceras sorbifolium Bunge can assist growers 
in optimizing the process of planting management, and making 
better decisions on labor allocation, fruit storage, harvest time 
and oil extraction during harvest. In addition, rapid and 
accurate prediction of Xanthoceras sorbifolium Bunge yield 
can help breeders improve fruit tree quality [1]. 
At present, the yield estimation of Xanthoceras sorbifolia 
Bunge fruit mainly depends on manual counting [2]. Due to the 
sunlight, shading of branches, adjacent fruits, and similar color 
with leaves in the field environment, there are some problems 
in manual counting estimation of Xanthoceras sorbifolium 
Bunge, such as missing data, repeated counting and heavy 
workload. Therefore, a fast and effective method is urgently 
needed to identify and count Xanthoceras sorbifolia Bunge. At 
present, there has been no research on automatic counting of 
Xanthoceras sorbifolium Bunge fruit at home and abroad. 
Fruit identification is the basis for achieving counting. 
Automatic fruit recognition methods are mainly divided into 
two categories: the first is directly based on fruit color [3-4], 
texture [5], brightness, contrast [6], shape [7], three-

However, there has been no research on recognition and 
counting of Xanthoceras sorbifolia fruit with deep learning 
method at present. The fruit and leaves of Xanthoceras 
sorbifolium Bunge are similar in color, so that it is a great 
challenge for whether the deep learning method can detect 
small targets with similar colors quickly and accurately. In 
addition, Xanthoceras sorbifolium Bunge mainly grows in 
mountains and hills, and the tree height is about 2-5 meters, 
which is not suitable for large-scale equipment operations. 
Although using UAV to collect video data can effectively 
overcome the influence of terrain factors, there are still outliers 
caused by fruit tracking and positioning errors in video data, 
leading to a challenge to count fruits from video data [44-45]. 
In the early stage, the research team used an unmanned vehicle 
to carry a Raspberry Pi to collect and study leaves, stems, 
flowers and fruits of crops [46]. On this basis, in order to 
realize the rapid and accurate identification and counting of the 
fruit in the field, the UAV is used to collect the information of 
the fruit in the field, and then the optimal method for its 
identification and counting is studied based on deep learning in 
this paper.

2) Data preprocessing 
a) Convert video in MP4 format to pictures at 100-
frame interval
The video data captured by the UAV is MP4 format, so that 
it is necessaryto be converted into a picture format for model 
analysis and processing. The Argparse package in OpenCV 
was used to convert the video collected by the UAV into 
pictures by frame. Due to the influencing factors such as UAV 
flying speed and  wind force, the pictures were taken at 
intervals of 50 frames, 100 frames and 150 frames for 
comparison. After experiments, the pictures in the dataset 
generated at 100-frame interval can have the highest 
availability rate, thus better reducing the number of invalid 
images. On this basis, considering the planting distance of 
Xanthoceras sorbifolia and the flight speed of UAV, the 
pictures were intercepted finally at 100-frame interval.
b) Denoise and illuminate the pictures, and delete the 
pictures that cannot meet the requirements.
The original data obtained from video cutting was screened, 
51 unavailable pictures were eliminated, and then the 
remaining 1,000 pictures were trained in the models.
Considering the weather, the pictures were
uniformly 
illuminated to eliminate errors caused by different light 
intensities.
c) Data augmentation and data simulation.
It is easy to produce over-fitting with too little data when 
using convolution neural network for training, so that 
augmentation of the original dataset is required. The 
amplification operations mainly include rotation, cropping and 
flipping for improving the over-fitting of data, enhancing the 
universality of models and reducing overfitting. But the 
traditional data augmentation method is limited to obfuscating 
existing information and cannot generate new information, 
with the inputs in the network model still highly correlated. 
Data simulation technology combines image synthesis 
technology on the basis of traditional data augmentation 
technology, so that when there is no real training data, the data 
simulation technology can better improve the prediction 
accuracy. Because the structure and characteristics of the 
simulated object are predetermined, a large number of 
annotated training pictures can be generated at low cost 
without consuming a lot of time and manpower intervention 
[47].
Therefore, this paper adopts data simulation technology on 
the basis of data augmentation. The information of fruits, 
surrounding objects, environment and tree canopy was 
classified and saved by cropping pictures. Based on the saved 
information, the fruit pictures were randomly distributed on the 
canopy pictures by controlling the factors, such as background 
color, quantity, brightness and environment. Thus, the 
Xanthoceras sorbifolia pictures can be simulated by this kind 
of random composite picture, which greatly expands the 
dataset and prevents over-fitting. An example result of 
composite simulation picture is shown in Figure 2.
Fig. 2. Example of composite simulation picture.

d) Data annotation and file configuration
Deep learning cannot directly train the clipped pictures. 
The pictures clipped by framein the video collected by the 
UAV were marked, and uniformly named with six digits, such 
as 000000, 000001and 000002. The labelImg tool was used to 
label the fruits in the form of a labeling box, as shown in 
Figure 3. Then the Xanthoceras sorbifolium Bunge dataset was 
created for the next step of deep learning model training. 
Considering the similarity between fruit color and leaf color, 
the fruits with more than 80% occlusion or located at the edge 
of pictures with less than 50% exposed area were not be 
labeled.
Fig. 3. Fruit labeling.
e) Divide training set and test set
The converted data was divided into a dataset, including a 
total of 1500 pictures. Then the picture data was divided into 5 
parts on average according to the method of 5-fold cross-
validation. During the training process, one of them was taken 
as the verification set in turn, and the remaining four were 
taken as the training set and the test set. Moreover, the final 
evaluation value was the average value of five training results.
3) Configuration environment and framework
The processing platform was a desktop computer in linux 
environment, with the Intel i7-8700 CPU, the 15.6 GB memory, 
the 250.9 GB disk space, the Python language 3.8, and the 
Spyder software. Darknet was used as the open source deep 
learning framework. As a light and open source deep learning 
framework with few dependencies and good portability, it can 
support both GPU and CPU computing methods.
4) Model training parameter settings
The main problem in target detection refers to that 
convolution layer is not obvious enough to the feature 
extraction, so that it is possible to lose part of the information 
of the target image. In order to effectively solve this problem, 
according to the characteristics of inconsistent fruit sizes in 
field, enlarging image input scale and multi-scale training 
methods were adopted to detect Xanthoceras sorbifolia fruit in 
the field. Limited by hardware and GPU, the final size of the 
input image of Xanthoceras sorbifolium Bunge was finally 
selected as 608*608, and then the scale was randomly changed 
after every 10 batches of training for subsequent training. After 
multi-scale training, the model can have strong robustness and 
adapt to different scales of Xanthoceras sorbifolia Bunge in the 
field, resulting in a better image detection. The relevant 
parameters in the configuration file was modified.  In the 
training phase, the batch size was set to 32, meaning that the 
number of pictures in each iteration training was 32. In order to 
reduce the memory pressure, each batch was further divided 
and sent to the network for training twice, with the 
subdivisions set to 16. Due to the number of classes as 1, 
classes was set to 1, and then filter was set to 18, initial 
learning rate 0.001, momentum term 0.9, attenuation 
coefficient 0.0005, and max_batches round number 10000.
III. RESULTS AND ANALYSIS
A. Evaluation Method
In this paper, the detection performance of the models was 
evaluated by IOU (Intersection Over Union), precision P 
(Precision), recall rate R (Recall), and average precision AP 
(Average Precision). The definitions of P and R are showed in 
Equation 1 and Equation 2:
P =TP/(TP + FP)                                       (1)
R = TP/(TP + FN) 
                             (2)
Where, TP (True Positives), FP (False Positives), and FN 
(False Negatives) represent the number of correctly detected 
fruits (True Positives), the number of incorrectly detected fruits 
(False Positives) and the number of undetected fruits (False 
Negatives), respectively.

In order to facilitate the researchers to predict the yield of 
the fruit, an online identification and counting system of the 
fruit has been developed in this paper, with the website: 
http://www.deepbiology.cn/DeepFruit.
The system back-end is developed with Python language, 
and the front-end is constructed with HTML+CSS+Javascript. 
The Docker application container engine is used to deploy the 
back-end on the Tencent ECs. The server adopts a 64-bit 
CentOS 7 system.
The system consists of four modules: home-page module, 
system introduction module, online prediction module and 
communication module. Among them, the online prediction 
module is the main functional module of the system, including 
three parts: model selection, detection image input and final 
detection result output. The system can provide YOLOv2, 
YOLOv2-tiny, 
YOLOv3,

and

V. DISCUSSION
Xanthoceras sorbifolium Bunge is an important oil crop. In 
view of the research gap on automatic counting of Xanthoceras 
sorbifolia fruit at home and abroad, this paper has done some 
exploratory research on automatic recognition and counting of 
Xanthoceras sorbifolia fruit based on deep learning. Using 
various models to recognize and count Xanthoceras sorbifolia 
Bunge can ensure both the accuracy of recognition and 
counting results and the efficiency of recognition and counting, 
with high practicability.
Simulated data, a cost-effective technology, has gradually 
attracted the attention of researchers in recent years. It has 
proved that a deep learning method based entirely on simulated 
data can accurately calculate the number of fruits in real plant 
images [30]. The use of simulated training data has also been 
shown to improve the accuracy of Arabidopsis leaf counting 
[48-49]. Based on simulated data, the possibility to achieve 
efficient soil-root segmentation in real images has been 
validated
[50].
In some
cases, it was proved that a 
convolutional neural network trained with a larger number of 
composite images is superior to a CNN trained with a smaller 
number of real-world images, and the simulated plant images 
can improve the accuracy of maize leaf counting [47]. With 
simulated data, high-quality results were also achieved in rose 
leaf segmentation and counting tasks [51]. Therefore, based on 
the data collected by the UAV, this paper uses not only data 
enhancement technology but also data simulation technology in 
the data processing stage, so as to better prevent overfitting.
The detection results can be affected by images with dim 
light or similar foreground and background colors [52-53],
which is still an urgent problem to be solved in target detection 
by computer vision. In order to facilitate detection, Yang [54]
used a contrast-enhancing method to process images by 
stretching the contrast of adjacent pixels and expanding the 
difference between the foreground and background. Chen et al. 
[55]
adopted the adaptive local algorithm of contrast 
enhancement (ACE) to process aerial images, in order to 
reduce the noise and noise and important feature blurriness 
caused by weather and other factors. The experiment has 
proved that this strategy is effective. Therefore, this paper 
carried out experiments to improve the contrast according to 
the characteristics of similar color between Xanthoceras 
sorbifolia fruit and leaves. The experimental results show that 
the method of improving contrast is effective in increasing the 
counting accuracy of the models, but only by properly 
improving contrast can the accuracy of model detection be 
improved. This conclusion is innovative to some extent.
In this paper, the datasets of Xanthoceras sorbifolia Bunge 
and other fruits with similar colors or fruit types are 
innovatively mixed for recognition and counting experiments. 
According to the experimental results, it is very possible to 
improve the accuracy of detection and counting by using the 
datasets with similar colors and fruit types for mixed training.
The research results are of great significance to the 
automatic yield estimation of Xanthoceras sorbifolium Bunge. 
Since the recognition and counting of almost covered fruits 
have not been studied in this paper, it will be further studied in 
the future.
VI. CONCLUSION
This paper studies the fruit counting method of 
Xanthoceras sorbifolium Bunge based on deep learning. The 
data is collected by UAV, and then preprocessed according to 
data enhancement and simulation technology. With a variety of 
deep learning models for testing, the IOU value of detection 
and recognition can reach 98.84%, and the counting accuracy 
796

98.05%. In order to improve the recognition and counting 
accuracy of Xanthoceras sorbifolium Bunge, the contrast 
improvement experiment, the experiment based on the mixed 
data set, and the experiment of the influence of different UAV 
flight modes on the counting results were carried out, 
respectively. Compared with the artificial fruit counting 
method, there are significant advantages of the method 
proposed in this paper, including small error, less workload and 
efficient fruit counting process, so that it can exert a positive 
effect on the healthy development of the fruit planting industry.
