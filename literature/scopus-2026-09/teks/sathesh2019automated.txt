Abstract— The yield estimation in agricultural field plays a 
vital role for the better utilization of resources and to enhance 
total yield per unit area within the time. The yield estimation of 
fruit is currently computed manually which leads to labour 
extensive and also expensive. It also takes much time for yield 
estimation. This manual sampling may also results in imprecise 
yield calculation. This makes the demand in machine vision 
based systems to address the above mentioned problem in 
detecting the yield estimation and thus reduces the error in 
counting the number of fruits on each tree. The multilayer CNN 
proposed here is used to classify the fruit from the tree image and 
also gives better result even in case of diminished images. 
Overlapped fruits also counted separately to give better result. 
Keywords— Convolutional neural network; Zero Component 
Analysis; Tensorflow. 
I. INTRODUCTION 
In the field of agriculture, counting the number of fruits 
yielded in a particular is a difficult and time consuming process 
while doing it in a manual way. Accurate yield estimation 
allows us to predict the quantity earlier so that it is possible to 
do some earlier precaution steps before the harvesting period. 
This tends to the development of automatic yield estimation 
system which predicts the number of fruits automatically. 
 
In recent years deep learning plays a major role in the 
computer based vision research activities and taken enormous 
advancement in technology wise. Various vision-based 
activities are adopted for recognizing, classifying and counting 
the objects which leads to provide greater accuracy of result. 
Trained samples of images are required to perform these tasks 
at a greater level of accuracy. To achieve accuracy, large 
amount of training samples are required. These training 
samples need to be labelled for our process which is cost 
consuming process in terms of both time and money. 
 
There are numerous applications [1][11][12] of deep 
learning in this real world and some of them are (1) 
Colorization of Black and White Images, (2) Adding Sounds 
To Silent Movies, (3) Object Classification in Photographs, (4) 
Automatic Machine Translation, (5) Automatic Handwriting  
 
Generation, (6) Image Caption Generation, (7) Character Text 
Generation, (8) Automatic Game Playing and lot more 
 
There are two objectives that need to be focused mainly for 
the good classification scheme. First, the dataset used should be 
high-quality which could eliminate the noisy background. So 
that classification does not lead to incorrect labeling one. 
Secondly, the deep neural network is trained to identify wide 
range of fruits (objects) from images. The deep learning 
algorithm works on the given input dataset and tries to 
minimize the difference between its classification/prediction 
and the expected output. 
 
There are some issues may occur during the fruits counting 
by using computer vision algorithms. Some of them are varied 
degree of fruits under shadow, overlap amongst fruits, and 
scale variation, illumination variance foliage occlusion. To 
address these problems, we propose a novel deep learning 
model in which it predicts the number of objects explicitly by 
just passing the entire image. Here the unnecessary steps of 
object localization and detection are eliminated. 
 
The proposed CNN [8] network consists of many 
convolution layers and pooling layers which helps us to 
retrieve the various features in different scales. Various stages 
of proposed model involves Image preprocessing and Labeling, 
Training and Experimentation and finally comes to the 
Evaluation Measures Initially, by means of ZCA whitening 
force unit variance and image patches pixel-level correlations 
are removed [7]. Then we train the Convolution Neural 
Network to classify an image into either fruit or non - fruit. In 
successive steps, we apply watershed transform method for 
yield estimation - counting the overlapped fruits and test the 
performance or evaluation measures afterwards. 
 
The overall work is interpreted as follows: First discuss the 
related work based on yield estimation. Second, the proposed 
model with CNN is discussed; then Implementation and 
Experimental result are highlighted. Finally we conclude our 
work and how the future work can be proceeded is highlighted.

II. RELATED WORK 
Kim et al. [4] developed a method to predict and follow 
moving object in the view of counting the number of people in 
motion by means of single fixed camera.  
 
Lempitsky et al. [5] proposed a novel supervised learning 
model for counting the visual objects which optimizes the 
MESA-distance loss during the learning.  
 
Giuffrida et al. [6] proposed a supervised regression model 
which does unsupervised learning to count the leaf. It is a 
learning-based approach to count the leaves and applied in 
rosette plants. 
 
Pragati Ninawe et al. [1] introduced fruit recognition 
technique which analyzes four features such as texture, size, 
color and shape based method to improve accuracy of 
recognition. It employs nearest neighbor classification 
algorithm to classify and recognize the fruit images based on 
the nearest training fruit example.  
 
Hossam M. Zawbaa et al. [2] applies K-Nearest 
Neighborhood (K-NN) algorithm for classification phase and 
employs Support Vector Machine (SVM) algorithm to support 
for variety of fruits. Totally 178 fruit images of dataset have 
been used to carried out their proposed model. 
 
Maryam Rahnemoonfar et al. [3] proposed a simulation-
based learning method where the training is done on simulated 
data but testing is on real data. They employs a modified 
version of Inception-ResNet Convolutional Neural Networks 
(CNN) and for counting the fruits. 
 
Y. Song et al. [10] framed an algorithm to count the fruits 
by taking the multiple views of tree with fruits so that hidden 
fruits, overlapped fruits are counted without missing them. In 
case of boundary of vertical images there may be chance of 
counting the single fruit twice. This conflict also rectified in 
their proposed work.

B. Classification 
CNN compares the images piece by piece. The piece that it 
looks for is called features. The preprocessed image is given 
as input to convolution layer. A convolutional layer is a 
collection of neurons which is made up of kernels. The kernels 
are smaller in size which have same depth as the input. A 
small region of the input from the kernels are connected with 
neurons, called the receptive field. For example, an image of 
size 128 x 128 which has 16,384 pixels and if 64 neurons 
present in the first layer, then it would result in 20, 97,152 
parameters. The kernels with the width and height of the input 
are used to mine high level features and produce an activation 
map which is 2 dimensional. There are four layers such as 
convolutional layer which succeeded by Relu layer, Pooling 
layer and then full layer.  
 
1. Convolution layer: 
This is the first layer and filters the given input image 
which has 64 kernels of size 4x4.The features are extracted in 
the form of matrices. The Relu layer applies the activation 
function max (0, x).The increases the nonlinear properties 
rather than reducing the size. Then max pooling is applied, the 
output of Relu is given as an input to the next convolutional 
layer which has 32 kernels of size 3x3. The last convolutional 
layer is of size 1x1 and has 128 kernels which is followed by a 
layer(fully connected) which contains 512 neurons   
 
2. Relu Layer: 
The extracted features are given as input to Rectified linear 
unit layer which removes all negative values obtained from the 
convolutional layer feature output. It transforms a function 
only the input is above certain threshold. The output is zero 
for less than zero input values. 
 
3. Maxpooling Layer: 
 The 
Maxpooling 
layer

to 
minimize 
the 
dimensionality and allows to assume that features in the sub-
regions are binned. It also reduces amount of high calculations 
done in the network. Its main advantage is control overfitting. 
The pooling layer which uses filters of size 2 x 2 with a stride 
2. It also reduces its input to its one by fourth from its original 
image size.  
 
4. Fully Connected layer: 
   Fully connected layer combines each and every neuron 
in one layer to the every neuron in another layer. It obtains 
from the output from the previous layer. The classified image 
will return either as a fruit or not. Finally, the proposed CNN 
layer counts the number of apple fruits in the given input 
image after the final layer.

VI. CONCLUSION AND FUTURE ENHANCEMENT 
Thus proposed model is used to classify the fruits from 
the trees and predicts the yield of the tree. The system works 
well in all type of real images with different lighting, 
illumination, shading, color and background. This automated 
system employs 3-layer CNN model classification and count 
the numbers of fruits (yield) with an average accuracy rate of 
93.4% (Fig. 3) . Manual yield estimation is cost expensive and 
consumes much time whereas the proposed model is cost 
effective with less in effort.  
 
Still there is room for enhancing the proposed model. The 
developed system is for counting the apple fruits specifically 
red apple. In future it can be enhanced to classify all types of 
apples and also make it applicable for all type of fruits. Since 
it has been developed as stand alone application, it can also be 
extended as Android application in future for the benefit of 
user.
