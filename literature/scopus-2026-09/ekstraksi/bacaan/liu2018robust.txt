KEY: liu2018robust (Liu, Pi, Xing et al. 2018 — "Robust fruit counting" line; source text lines 146–148 name the contribution as integrating FCN segmentation with SfM tracking)

CROP / SETTING / SCALE: Orange and apple orchards. Orange dataset: 1280×960 images, daytime, steady camera moved at walking speed. Apple dataset: 1920×1200 images, night, flash lighting, camera on a vehicle at about 1 m/s (lines 616–627). Monocular RGB only (lines 185–188). Count evaluated against ground truth per tree (Fig. 7, lines 550–574).

ACQUISITION: Video along a row (monocular camera, sides of row not stated in the extracted lines read). Orange: Bluefox USB 2 camera, 10 Hz, steady camera at walking speed, daytime (lines 616–626). Apple: PointGrey USB 3 camera, 6 Hz, night with flash, vehicle at about 1 m/s (lines 619–627). Camera distance from canopy: not stated in the lines read.

OBSERVATION / DETECTOR: Fully convolutional network (FCN) for two-class segmentation (fruit / not fruit), same structure as the authors' earlier FCN [2], implemented in TensorFlow (lines 219–245, 632). Detection accuracy as a per-frame metric: not stated in the lines read. Detection is a segmentation mask; the fruit boxes are used for tracking.

ASSOCIATION METHOD: Tracking of fruit between frames with Kalman-corrected KLT (Kanade–Lucas–Tomasi) optical flow, with Hungarian assignment (lines 453–458). Cost between a predicted and a detected fruit = centre distance / (a_i + a_j) + (1 − λ) times a term from bounding-box size and overlap (lines 453–458). Incremental structure from motion (SfM) with COLMAP; SIFT features restricted to within 25 px of fruit boxes (lines 501–542). After tracking, fruit tracks are localised in 3D (lines 583–590). Counting rule: a track is counted if its age is greater than one third of the number of overlapping frames (lines 480–490). Appearance features beyond SIFT: not stated. Learned matching: not stated.

ASSUMPTIONS STATED OR IMPLIED BY AUTHORS: Not every fruit is tracked in every frame it appears in, because of occlusion and illumination variation (lines 583–585). Double-counted tracks are close in 3D space after localisation (lines 588–590). Estimated fruit size is large for false positives, so size outliers can be removed (lines 596–597). Relative size outlier range 0.5–4 (lines 591–600, 673–683). Depth threshold 1.5 × mean depth (lines 684–685). Orange scene has more outliers and double counts than apple (lines 713–715).

DOUBLE-COUNT SOURCES ADDRESSED: (a) same fruit in consecutive frames: addressed by KLT tracking with Kalman correction and Hungarian assignment across frames (lines 453–490). (b) track lost and restarted: partly addressed. Tracks with low age are discarded (age threshold, lines 480–490). Fruit not tracked in all frames are noted as a limitation (lines 583–585). (c) same fruit from different views: addressed by 3D localisation of fruit tracks through incremental SfM; tracks close in 3D are treated as double counts and removed (lines 583–590). Other stated sources: none in the lines read.

CLASS / MATURITY HANDLING: none. The method has one class (fruit) against background. No maturity or ripeness classes are reported.

COUNT REFERENCE AND METRIC LEVEL: Ground-truth count per tree for orange (3456 fruit) and apple (7949 fruit) (Fig. 7, lines 550–574). Metrics: L1 loss (sum of absolute count errors across trees), error mean (percent), and error standard deviation (percent) (Fig. 7). Identity metrics (MOTA, IDF1, HOTA, ID switches): not stated. Detection metrics: not stated in the lines read.

KEY QUANTITATIVE RESULTS:
- Orange, uncorrected model (before 3D step): count 4049 vs ground truth 3456; L1 593; error mean 17.2% (Fig. 7) / 17.1% (text, line 647); standard deviation 26.3% (Fig. 7, line 646–648).
- Orange, corrected with 3D reconstruction step: count 3449 vs ground truth 3456; L1 203; error mean −0.2%; standard deviation 7.8% (Fig. 7).
- Apple, uncorrected: count 8622 vs ground truth 7949; L1 673; error mean 8.5%; standard deviation 4.7% (Fig. 7).
- Apple, corrected: count 8215 vs ground truth 7949; L1 322; error mean 3.3%; standard deviation 4.1% (Fig. 7).
- Per-tree examples (orange): 57 counted before correction vs 2 after, ground truth 206 (lines 663–665); 101 before vs 9 after, ground truth 78 (lines 674–675).

FAILURE CONDITIONS / ERROR SOURCES REPORTED:
- Occlusion and illumination variation leave some fruit untracked in some frames (lines 583–585).
- Double counts from the same fruit seen across the track (lines 588–590), removed by 3D localisation.
- False positives with large estimated size (lines 596–597).
- More outliers and double-counted fruit for oranges than for apples (lines 713–715).

COMPARISONS ON THE SAME DATA: Uncorrected vs corrected model on the same orange and apple ground truth (Fig. 7). No comparison with another counting method on shared data is reported in the lines read.
