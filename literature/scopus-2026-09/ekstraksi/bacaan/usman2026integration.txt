ACQUISITION: Still photographs only. Devices: iPhone 15, Samsung Galaxy A56, Canon EOS 80D (L456-460). Of the images, 7,000 are field photos from three plantations and 3,000 come from Roboflow (L456-460). Capture distance, angles, lighting protocol, video, frame rate and number of visits: not stated in the parts read. Field capture is not described in enough detail to reproduce.

OBSERVATION / DETECTOR: No detector. The task is whole-image classification with ResNet18, ResNet34 and ResNet50 backbones (L1481-1483 and Table 8). Input 224 x 224. No bounding box, localisation, instance or object-detection step appears in the text read (L1-3000). The abstract's "real-time ripeness detection" (L2877-2878) refers to classification of a whole photo. Failure cases of the kind asked for (small, occluded or frond-covered bunches) are not analysed. Confusion between neighbouring classes is shown only in the Fig. 5 confusion-matrix paragraph, which is duplicated verbatim in the text (L1809-1887).

ASSOCIATION METHOD: None. No tracking, no identity across images or views, no counting rule. Each photo is classified on its own. The search (GA, PSO, SA, NSGA-II-style Pareto archive, CBAM, SADA) tunes hyperparameters and architecture, not association.
