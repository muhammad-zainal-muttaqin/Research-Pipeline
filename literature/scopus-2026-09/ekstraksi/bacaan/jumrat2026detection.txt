Source: "Detection the Ripeness of Oil Palm Fresh Fruit Bunch Using Pretrained and Improved Models of YOLOv8", Journal of Advances in Information Technology, Vol. 17, No. 6, 2026, doi 10.12720/jait.17.6.1028-1040.

CROP / SETTING / SCALE:
- Oil palm fresh fruit bunches (FFB) at a palm oil processing facility or plantation context; images from Thaksin Palm Company (L446-447, L542-543).
- 427 cleaned images in three ripeness classes: raw 146, half-ripe 127, ripe 154 (Table II, L473-483).
- Scale of the bunch-level view is not stated. No field-level or tree-level scale is described.

ACQUISITION:
- "All images were collected at Thaksin Palm Company under real industrial conditions." (L542-543)
- "The images were captured using a standard RGB camera at varying distances and angles to reflect practical inspection scenarios." (L543-545)
- Camera model, resolution, frame rate, and capture distance in metres: not stated.
- Lighting: natural lighting, "including variations in illumination and background clutter" (L546-548).
- Single still images. No video, no multiple views of the same bunch, no revisits.

OBSERVATION / DETECTOR:
- Detector: YOLOv8 (Ultralytics YOLOv8 family), not a newly designed architecture. Variants n, s, m, l, x pretrained on COCO (80 classes) with 640-pixel input (Table IV, L580-604).
- Fine-tuned variant: YOLOv8x with 10, 13, 16 or 19 blocks frozen (labelled YOLOv8x10, x13, x16, x19 in Table VI). The "improved" model is YOLOv8x19 (19 frozen blocks).
- Authors' definition: "the term 'improved YOLOv8' in this study refers to an optimized fine-tuning strategy rather than a modification of the original network architecture." (L1092-1094)
- Detects bounding boxes of bunches and labels each box with one of three ripeness classes: raw, half, ripe (L459-463). Labelled in Roboflow, YOLOv8 format.
- Reported accuracy (Table VI, as printed; split not named in the caption, L789-795):
  - YOLOv8x19: P 0.966, R 0.968, F1 0.967, mAP50 0.988, mAP50-95 0.773, 16.2 ms, 130.39 MB.
  - YOLOv8x10: P 0.956, R 0.964, F1 0.96, mAP50 0.989, mAP50-95 0.763, 16.3 ms, 130.39 MB.
  - YOLOv8x: P 0.918, R 0.964, F1 0.94, mAP50 0.975, mAP50-95 0.761, 29.3 ms, 136.7 MB.
  - YOLOv8n: P 0.827, R 0.93, F1 0.876, mAP50 0.956, mAP50-95 0.709, 2.9 ms, 5.96 MB.
- Per-class confusion matrix (Fig. 7): raw and ripe 100% correct; half 89% correct (L1059-1063).
- Per-image prediction accuracy shown in Fig. 8 on a "testing set of 45 images": 87% raw, 89% half, 88% ripe, 80% and 81% for the multi-class image (L1080-1088).

ASSOCIATION METHOD:
- None. Each image is processed on its own. The paper does not track, link, or match detections across images or frames.

ASSUMPTIONS STATED OR IMPLIED BY AUTHORS:
- Stated: a "carefully curated" three-class dataset is enough for "robust performance even with a relatively small dataset" (L1275-1277 and L1160-1164).
- Implied: the three-class industrial grading scheme is the target taxonomy (L1295-1297 says overripe or abnormal bunches were not considered).
- Implied: images with one bunch or a few bunches are the operating case; failures are said to occur "especially when multiple bunches appeared in a single image" (L1118-1121).

DOUBLE-COUNT SOURCES ADDRESSED:
- (a) same fruit in consecutive frames: not addressed; no video used.
- (b) track lost and restarted: not addressed; no tracker.
- (c) same fruit from both sides or different passes: not addressed; not stated.

CLASS / MATURITY HANDLING:
- Classes: raw, half-ripe ("half"), ripe. Three classes (L450-451).
- Class is attached per detection box at the image level by the detector. No class is attached to a unique physical bunch; no bunch identity exists.
- Disagreement between frames: not applicable (single images).
- Confusion: "Most misclassifications occurred between the 'half-ripe' and 'ripe' classes, as indicated by the confusion matrix." (L1113-1115)

COUNT REFERENCE AND METRIC LEVEL:
- No count reference. No hand count, harvest count, or packhouse count is compared.
- Metrics: precision, recall, F1, mAP50, mAP50-95, inference time (ms), model size (MB). No counting error, MOTA, IDF1, HOTA, or ID switch metrics.
- Ground truth: manual bounding-box annotation by trained personnel, reviewed for consistency (L485-495).

KEY QUANTITATIVE RESULTS:
- Dataset: 427 images (Table II). Augmented split 891 train, 84 val, 46 test, total 1,021 (Table III, L511-528).
- Split: stated 7:2:1 (L499). Augmentation (horizontal flip p=0.5; grayscale on 25% of train; Gaussian noise variance 0.01; Gaussian blur 5x5 p=0.3) applied to train only (L530-539).
- Table VI: see OBSERVATION / DETECTOR.
- Fig. 8 per-image accuracies: 87%, 89%, 88%, 80%, 81% (L1080-1088).
- Timing (L1089-1091): inference 32.8 ms, preprocessing 1.8 ms, postprocessing 1.3 ms, total 35.9 ms, "averaging 32 ms per image".
- Model size (Table VI): YOLOv8x 136.7 MB; x10, x13, x16, x19 each 130.39 MB.
- Table VII (literature comparison, not same data): Lai et al. [26] YOLOv4, ripe only, 1 class, mAP50 0.879, 21 FPS; Gunawan et al. [30] YOLOv8m, 6 classes, mAP50 0.927, "no layer freezing"; Naftali and Hugo [3] YOLOv8s-DW, 5 classes.

FAILURE CONDITIONS / ERROR SOURCES REPORTED:
- Misclassification between half-ripe and ripe due to "visual similarities in color and texture" under "varying illumination conditions" (L1114-1117).
- Partial occlusion of bunches and background clutter caused incorrect predictions, especially with multiple bunches in one image (L1118-1121).
- "RGB-based visual features alone may be insufficient to fully discriminate subtle ripeness transitions." (L1122-1124)
- Small dataset limits exposure to "rare or ambiguous visual patterns" (L1130-1132), and robustness with "overlapping fruit bunches or non-uniform lighting" (L1136-1138).
- Qualitative inspection: sensitivity to "non-uniform illumination, partial occlusion, and complex backgrounds" with more misclassifications under these conditions (L1306-1312).
- Small bunches, bunches embedded in or covered by fronds: not discussed.

COMPARISONS ON THE SAME DATA:
- None. Table VII compares against published results on other datasets (Lai et al. 1 class; Gunawan et al. 7,171 images). The authors state that "differences in evaluation protocols and deployment targets further contribute to variations in reported metrics" (L1171-1173). The paper does not run any comparison model on its own data beyond the YOLOv8 variants.

AUTHORS' STATED LIMITATIONS / FUTURE WORK:
- Limitations: small dataset, "may limit generalization to highly diverse field conditions" (L1291-1294); only three classes (L1294-1297); YOLOv8x still needs "relatively high computational resources" and "may constrain deployment on low-end edge devices" (L1297-1305); sensitivity to illumination, occlusion, background (L1306-1312).
- Future work: expand dataset to more environmental conditions and ripeness categories (L1377-1382); lightweight YOLOv8 variants and compression (L1379-1382); Flask service, backend, database, dashboard, and graphs of "quality and quantity of ripe oil palm bunches" (L1383-1395); multispectral or hyperspectral imaging (L1396-1403); attention mechanisms or "temporal information from video sequences" (L1406-1409).
- No counting or video tracking is proposed as current work.

INCONSISTENCIES INSIDE THE PAPER:

- Inference time for YOLOv8x19: Table VI gives 16.2 ms (L867); text gives "inference time of 32.8 ms" and "total detection time of 35.9 ms, averaging 32 ms per image" (L1089-1091); the conclusion gives 16.2 ms (L1343-1344).
- Model size for YOLOv8x19: Table VI gives 130.39 MB (L868); the conclusion gives "a model size of 136.7 MB" (L1344-1345). In Table VI, 136.7 MB is the YOLOv8x row (L836).
- Table VI does not name the split on which metrics are reported (caption at L789).
