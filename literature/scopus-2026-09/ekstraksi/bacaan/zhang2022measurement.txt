KEY: zhang2022measurement
Zhang Xianjie, Wang Xiaochan, Sun Guoxiang et al. 2022. "Measurement of tomato fruits quantity at different ripening stages based on color point cloud images" (Chinese title: 基于彩色点云图像的不同成熟阶段番茄果实数量的测定方法). J. South China Agric. Univ. 43(2): 105-112. DOI 10.7671/j.issn.1001-411X.202105021. Language: Chinese text with English abstract and English captions/table headers. Lines cited below are line numbers in the text file.

CROP / SETTING / SCALE:
Greenhouse tomato (cultivars 'Qimei' and 'Taotaifen'), hanging-string (吊蔓式) planting, plant height 145–165 cm, plant spacing 40 cm, row spacing 120 cm (lines 103–108). Images collected at the Jiangsu Academy of Agricultural Sciences greenhouse on 17 Dec 2020 (14:00–17:00) and 23 Dec 2020 (14:30–17:00) under different light conditions (lines 115–117). Scale: 252 sets of plant image data (lines 115–117); 252 plant point clouds generated, 246 synthesised after registration (lines 689–691). Counting unit: tomato fruits per ripening stage, within a set near-distance range (lines 90–94). The 4 rows counted in section 2.3 (lines 738–740).

ACQUISITION:
- Camera: Microsoft Kinect V2.0 RGB-D, colour 1920×1080, depth 512×424 (lines 118–123).
- Platform: Shenzhen WZS DashGo B1 ROS mobile robot; the platform stops at fixed points and captures colour and depth images (lines 118–121). Section 2.3 repeats "定点停靠" (fixed-point stop) for the 4-row count (lines 738–740).
- Views: two viewpoints per plant; the two point clouds are registered into one (abstract lines 15–17; lines 128–130).
- Frame rate, camera-to-plant distance, sides of row sampled, and total number of stops per row: not stated in the text read.

OBSERVATION / DETECTOR:
- Each plant is reconstructed as a colour point cloud from RGB and depth (lines 128–130). Depth is used to keep only the nearby plant (lines 92–94; conclusion lines 814–819).
- Detector: PointRCNN, a two-stage 3D object detector (lines 241–254, 315–324), implemented in PyTorch 1.3.1 with OpenPCDet 0.3.0 (lines 325–326). Hardware: Intel Gold 6226, 256 GB RAM, RTX 2080Ti, Ubuntu 18.04 (lines 326–328).
- Labels: manual 3D box annotation in CloudCompare, converted to KITTI format (lines 328–330). Label classes: tomato and 2 other classes (lines 331–332).
- Training: 196 of the 246 synthesised plant point clouds for training; the other 50 for testing (lines 689–697). Settings: 20,000 epochs, batch 3 per GPU, 2 GPUs, learning rate 0.01, div_factor 10, Adam one-cycle optimiser; transfer learning (lines 333–342).
- Detection accuracy as reported (50 test plant point clouds): reference count 277, detected 268, correct 231, false 37, missed 46; precision 86.19%, recall 83.39% (lines 719–722; abstract lines 20–21).
- Box position: mean centre relative error 36.83% over 50 randomly selected detections (lines 723–727; Table 2, line 800–801).

ASSOCIATION METHOD:
- Association is across two viewpoints by point cloud registration: RANSAC coarse registration (voxel 0.02, distance threshold 0.03) followed by point-to-plane ICP fine registration (distance threshold 0.001) (lines 222–239).
- Depth-based z-threshold keeps near-row points; statistical outlier removal is applied (lines 158–175).
- Counting is per detected 3D box in the single merged point cloud of a plant (lines 698–705; 2.3 lines 737–811). No tracking across time, no SfM or SLAM, no identity model.
- How points or fruits seen in both viewpoints are deduplicated after registration: not stated in the text read.

ASSUMPTIONS STATED OR IMPLIED BY AUTHORS:
- Stated: a 2D RGB image cannot give fruit distance, so it cannot tell whether a fruit belongs to the counted plant (lines 74–78).
- Stated: removing distant fruit reduces interference with counting near fruit (lines 92–94; conclusion lines 814–819).
- Stated: two-view synthesis adds feature information and improves recognition (lines 94–95).
- Implied: the colour of a fruit in the point cloud is informative enough for the SVM trained on RGB-image fruit samples (lines 462–468; 729–735). The SVM was trained on 320 image samples of fruit, not on point clouds (lines 475–481, 540–544).

DOUBLE-COUNT SOURCES ADDRESSED:
(a) Same fruit in consecutive frames: not applicable; each plant is a single merged point cloud (lines 128–130).
(b) Track lost and restarted: not applicable; no tracking.
(c) Same fruit from two viewpoints: the two plant point clouds are merged by registration (abstract lines 15–17; lines 128–130). The paper states the aim as reducing the influence of occlusion and overlap (conclusion lines 815–817). Explicit handling of a fruit visible in both views (merge by overlap or by box matching) is not stated.
- Distant fruit from other plants is removed by depth truncation rather than deduplicated (lines 92–94; 815–819).

CLASS / MATURITY HANDLING:
- Four ripeness stages judged by internal flesh colour: mature-green (绿熟, green flesh about 90% or more), breaker (微熟, 10–40% red), ripe (成熟, 40–90% red), fully ripe (完熟, 90% or more red) (lines 469–474).
- Class training: 320 RGB image samples, 80 per stage; R, G, B, S, V channel mean and variance (10 colour features); feature matrix chosen by SVM accuracy (lines 475–539). Split 60% training, 40% test (lines 540–541).
- Class per fruit: each detected box's point cloud is classified by the trained SVM (lines 729–735). Output: 112 green, 72 breaker, 55 ripe, 29 fully ripe across the 50 test plant point clouds (lines 734–735; 268 total, equal to the detected count).
- Frames disagree: not applicable (one point cloud per plant). Ties: not applicable.
- Ripeness accuracy: 94.27% on the training split, 96.09% on the test split (lines 543–544; abstract lines 21–22).
- The ripeness classifier was not evaluated against the test-set point-cloud fruits with a reference class label; section 2.2 gives only predicted counts (lines 729–735).

COUNT REFERENCE AND METRIC LEVEL:
- Reference: manual 3D box annotation of the synthesised point clouds in CloudCompare (lines 328–330; 692–694). The 50 test plant point clouds have 277 annotated fruit (line 721).
- Metrics: precision AD = NC/ND and recall AR = NC/NR (Eq. 4–5, lines 554–571); mean centre relative error CR (Eq. 6, lines 672–684).
- Identity metrics (MOTA, IDF1, HOTA, ID switches): not applicable; not used.
- Count per ripeness stage for the 4-row measurement: predicted only, no reference count given (lines 809–811).

KEY QUANTITATIVE RESULTS:
- Detection, 50 test point clouds: reference 277, detected 268, correct 231, false 37, missed 46; precision 86.19%, recall 83.39% (lines 719–722; abstract lines 20–21).
- Position: mean centre relative error 36.83% (Table 2, lines 723–727, 800–801). Per-cloud values in Table 2 range from 9.93% (cloud 4) to 59.72% (cloud 1) (lines 760–799).
- Ripeness SVM: training accuracy 94.27%, test accuracy 96.09% (lines 543–544; 823–825).
- Predicted ripeness counts in 50 test plant point clouds: green 112, breaker 72, ripe 55, fully ripe 29 (lines 734–735).
- 4-row measurement: green 448, breaker 393, ripe 294, fully ripe 281, total 1,416 (lines 809–811). Sum is 1,416.

FAILURE CONDITIONS / ERROR SOURCES REPORTED:
- Occlusion and overlap of fruit; unripe fruit colour close to leaf colour; complex background (lines 65–68).
- Distant fruit from other plants in the view (lines 92–94).
- Position error varies widely across clouds: 59.72% at cloud 1 and 48.49% at cloud 48 versus 9.93% at cloud 4 (Table 2, lines 760–799).
- Missed detections 46 and false detections 37 out of 277 reference fruit (lines 719–722).
- The paper does not report failures by light condition, ripeness stage, or occlusion level.

COMPARISONS ON THE SAME DATA:
- Mehta et al. (ref 19): pseudo-stereo from multi-view 2D RGB; the authors say multi-camera placement reduces practicality (lines 79–82). No numeric comparison.
- Li Han et al. (ref 20): RGB contour plus depth, improved SOM-K-means; the authors say it applies only to ripe red tomatoes (lines 82–86). No numeric comparison.
- No comparison on the same data is reported.

AUTHORS' STATED LIMITATIONS / FUTURE WORK:
- A limitations section is not present in the text read. The conclusion (lines 813–830) states the method, the accuracy numbers and says the data support yield evaluation (abstract lines 22–23, 56–58). It names no limitation.
- Future work: not stated.

INCONSISTENCIES INSIDE THE PAPER:
- Plant point clouds: 252 generated (line 690), 246 synthesised after registration (line 691); the 252 sets are in the abstract-level scale statement (lines 115–117). Which 246 are used, and what happened to the other 6, is not stated.
- Accuracy claim versus position error: the abstract says the method "can accurately identify the number of tomato fruits at different ripening stages" (abstract lines 56–58) while mean centre relative error is 36.83% (line 727) and per-cloud errors reach 59.72% (line 763).
- Ripeness classifier training domain: the SVM is trained on RGB image samples of fruit (lines 475–481, 540–544) but applied to RGB values of point clouds in section 2.2 (lines 729–735). Whether these match is not reported.
- Count claim: the title and abstract describe counting at different ripening stages (abstract lines 31–32). Section 2.3 gives only predicted counts (1,416) with no ground-truth count per stage (lines 809–811).
