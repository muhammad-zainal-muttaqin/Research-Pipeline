KEY: mollineda2025estimation

CROP / SETTING / SCALE: Orange trees (Citrus sinensis) in commercial orchard rows, real uncontrolled conditions (sunlight, shade). Dataset: Ambimetrics Orange Counting Dataset, 5 row videos in the Methods section (see INCONSISTENCIES). Tree spacing about 3 m (Sec. 3). Scale of output: visible oranges per tree, used as a sample of fruit load.

ACQUISITION: Video along the row from a farm vehicle (tractor). Intel RealSense D435 camera, 30 FPS, RGB 480x640. Vehicle speed stated as 2-5 km/h in Methods and 3-5 km/h in Discussion (see INCONSISTENCIES). Cameras on the far side of the vehicle relative to the trees (Sec. 3). Each tree is described by a tree-centred window of frames; the Discussion states the row segment per tree would be described by at least 30 frames at the stated speed. Field of view stated in Discussion as "about 2 ms wide by 2.5 ms high" (unit as printed in the text; see INCONSISTENCIES).

OBSERVATION / DETECTOR: No detector is used for the main counting. The counting method is direct regression of the number of visible oranges from images. Three regressors: EfficientNetB0 regressor, FPN-linear and FPN-map (two Feature Pyramid Network based models). Only block 7 is retrained (as stated in Sec. 3). Input size of the regressors: not verified in this pass. Accuracy of the regressors: see KEY QUANTITATIVE RESULTS. Detectors used only for validation in Study II: YOLOv5 and YOLOv7 (exact versions/weights not verified in this pass). Detection accuracy of YOLOv5/YOLOv7 against the annotations is not reported as AP in the parts read; agreement is reported as correlations (Table 7).

ASSOCIATION METHOD: No tracker, no motion model, no 3D reconstruction, no SfM/SLAM, no identity matching across frames. Sequences of 2n+1 frames centred on a manually chosen key frame, with n=5, giving 11 frames (sizes 1, 3, 7, 11 compared). Each frame gets a count estimate from the regressor. Final tree estimate = sample median of the per-frame estimates (Eq. 2). Tree label for training = mean of the frame annotations (Eq. 3). Counting rule: per-frame count of visible oranges, aggregated by median over the sequence; no unique-fruit ID.

ASSUMPTIONS STATED OR IMPLIED BY AUTHORS: The number of visible fruits per tree is positively and significantly correlated with the total, unknown fruit load for most trees (Sec. 6). Results depend on the specific acquisition configuration and may change with vehicle speed, frame rate and FOV (Discussion). A significant reduction in FOV is expected not to impair relative yield distribution as long as the image covers a significant portion of the canopy (Discussion). Only oranges with about 50% or more of the surface visible were annotated (Sec. 3).

DOUBLE-COUNT SOURCES ADDRESSED:
(a) Same fruit in consecutive frames: not handled by linking. The paper states that YOLOv5 overcounts repeated oranges (paraphrase; verbatim sentence not captured in this pass). The regression approach gives a per-frame count and aggregates by median; the paper does not state that it removes repeated fruit explicitly.
(b) Track lost and restarted: not applicable (no tracker). Not stated.
(c) Same fruit from both sides or different passes: not stated. Cameras are on one side of the vehicle (Sec. 3).

CLASS / MATURITY HANDLING: None. Single class (visible orange). No maturity or ripeness classes in the parts read.

COUNT REFERENCE AND METRIC LEVEL: Reference is manual annotation of visible oranges in individual frames, made with makesense.ai at about 45-60 s per frame (Sec. 3). Tree reference = mean of frame annotations. Metrics: MedAE (median absolute error), MedAPE (median absolute percentage error), MAD (median absolute deviation), Wilcoxon signed-rank test (p-values, Table 6). Study II: Pearson and Spearman correlations between counts from the regressors/annotations and YOLOv5/YOLOv7 counts (Table 7). No harvest, packhouse or yield reference: the paper states yield annotations from past harvests are not yet available (Sec. 6). No identity metrics (MOTA, IDF1, HOTA, ID switches).

KEY QUANTITATIVE RESULTS:
- Experiments: 960 runs = 3 models x 4 sequence sizes x 4 folds x 20 random hyperparameter sets; fourfold cross-validation by row (Sec. 4).
- Trees: 99 trees in Table 2; 77 trees x 11 frames = 847 annotated frames (Sec. 3.2).
- Table 4, MedAE 1-frame -> 11-frame: EfficientNetB0 3.20 -> 2.61 (MedAPE 0.186 -> 0.151); FPN-linear 2.79 -> 2.57 (7-frame value 2.42 as printed in Table 4 notes; MedAPE 0.147 -> 0.163); FPN-map 3.10 -> 2.38 (3-frame) and 2.31 (11-frame), MedAPE 0.217 -> 0.151. Exact column assignment for the intermediate values not re-verified.
- Sec. 4.2 text: reductions of 22% (EfficientNetB0) and 25% (FPN-map).
- Discussion: MedAPE of 13.7% for FPN-map with 7-frame sequences, called "a promising rate in a complex scenario".
- Table 5, MAD for 3, 7, 11 frames: EfficientNetB0 0.21 / 0.47 / 0.54; FPN-linear 0.19 / 0.43 / 0.48; FPN-map 0.17 / 0.39 / 0.57.
- Table 6, Wilcoxon p-values for 3, 7, 11 frames: EfficientNetB0 0.014 / 0.023 / 0.005; FPN-map 0.012 / 0.023 / 0.019; FPN-linear 0.975 / 0.951 / 0.635 (not significant).
- Table 7, Study II correlations (Pearson / Spearman) as printed in the extracted text: YOLOv5-Annot 0.82/0.81, 0.88/0.86, 0.90/0.89, 0.91/0.90; YOLOv7-Annot 0.81/0.80, 0.85/0.84, 0.87/0.87, 0.87/0.88; YOLOv5-YOLOv7 0.87/0.88, 0.90/0.89, 0.91/0.90, 0.91/0.91. The four column groups are labelled only partly in the extracted text (3-, 7-, 11-frame labels visible); the first group's label was not visible in this pass.
- Table 1 (this work): "Absolute error: 2.3; Relative error: 0.14; multiple video partitions" (as summarised in the table row read earlier).

FAILURE CONDITIONS / ERROR SOURCES REPORTED:
- YOLOv5 overcounts repeated oranges (paraphrase, Sec. 4 or 5; verbatim not captured).
- YOLOv7 misses shaded oranges (paraphrase).
- Sunlight flare in row 5 led to exclusion of that row (22 trees) from experiments (Sec. 3.1).
- Oranges with less than about 50% visible surface are not annotated (Sec. 3).
- Frame-level estimates vary; median over sequence reduces error for EfficientNetB0 and FPN-map but not significantly for FPN-linear (Table 6).

COMPARISONS ON THE SAME DATA: Single-frame vs 3/7/11-frame sequences for the three regressors on the same video set (Tables 4-6). Regressor counts vs YOLOv5 and YOLOv7 counts on the same tree sequences (Table 7, Study II). YOLOv5 vs YOLOv7 agreement also reported (Table 7).

AUTHORS' STATED LIMITATIONS / FUTURE WORK: Results apply to one acquisition configuration (frame rate, FOV, speed); effects of these parameters left to future work (Discussion). Hybrid strategies with manual correction of detector outputs could be explored (Discussion). Yield annotations (total weight in kg) from past harvests are needed for the production goal (Sec. 6). Solutions that process whole video sequences could be evaluated (Sec. 6).
