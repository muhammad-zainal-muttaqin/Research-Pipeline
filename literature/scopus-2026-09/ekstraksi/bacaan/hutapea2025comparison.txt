KEY: hutapea2025comparison

CROP / SETTING / SCALE: Oil palm fresh fruit bunches (FFB), ripeness classes. Data collected during one visit to a PTPN 4 plantation in Medan, North Sumatra (L179-180, L21-22). 250 images collected; 80 selected; 185 after augmentation (L197-200). Plantation size, tree count and number of bunches per image: not stated.

ACQUISITION: Discrete single images only. Camera: iPhone 12 (L184-185). Two image types: close-ups of individual fruits and group photos of mature fruit clusters taken at the harvest site (L185-187). Training images of "every fruit and every cluster of ripe fruits" taken "from several perspectives" (L187-188). Not stated: video, frame rate, camera-to-bunch distance, capture angles, platform, number of sides of a row, number of tree visits.

OBSERVATION / DETECTOR: YOLOv8s (Ultralytics) and YOLOv10s (developed by researchers at Tsinghua University; built on YOLOv8s) (L204-208). Model size "s" for both (L182). Input image size: not stated. Training: 25 and 50 epochs, same dataset for both models (L211-213, L248). Pretrained weights and hardware: not stated. Detects and classifies FFB into ripeness classes. Reason given for YOLO: "suitable for faster object detection and its accuracy [21]" (L202-203); YOLOv8 is described as one that "has improved capabilities, especially in identifying small objects [17]" (L83-84; the text does not name the model it is compared with). Reported accuracy: mAP50 0.995 (YOLOv8s) and 0.985 / 0.977 (YOLOv10s); see KEY QUANTITATIVE RESULTS.

ASSOCIATION METHOD: None. Single-image detection and classification. No tracker, motion model, appearance features, 3D reconstruction, SfM, SLAM, learned matching, statistical correction, or counting rule.

ASSUMPTIONS STATED OR IMPLIED BY AUTHORS:
- YOLO's speed and accuracy transfer to FFB ripeness (L202-203).
- YOLOv8's small-object capability carries over (L83-84).
- Lower GFLOPS means lower energy consumption (L276-278); energy was not measured.
- Augmentation (flip, rotate) "to enhance the model's reliability" (L283-284); not tested.
- Folder labels assigned before annotation are usable: "While this categorization was not strictly necessary, it facilitated the upload and annotation process." (L194-195)

DOUBLE-COUNT SOURCES ADDRESSED:
(a) Same fruit in consecutive frames: not applicable; not addressed.
(b) Track lost and restarted: not applicable; not addressed.
(c) Same fruit from both sides or different passes: not addressed. The "several perspectives" (L187-188) are training images, not linked observations.

CLASS / MATURITY HANDLING: Three classes: "ripe", "rotten" and "unripe" (abstract L22-23; conclusion L281-282). Each image was placed in a class folder by hand before upload to Roboflow; "images annotated according to their pre-assigned categories" (L195-196). The text does not define the classes or the criteria for "rotten". No per-object class rule. No class disagreement analysis (single images only). No half-ripe class.

COUNT REFERENCE AND METRIC LEVEL: No count reference (no hand count, harvest or packhouse count). Metrics: mAP50 and mAP50-95 (Table 2, L217-247); GFLOPS (Table 2); precision and recall shown only as graphs (Figures 3-4, L255-260), with no numeric values in the text other than precision "of 1" for YOLOv8s (L258-260). No counting, identity, MOTA, IDF1, HOTA or ID-switch metrics.

KEY QUANTITATIVE RESULTS (Table 2, L217-247, and abstract L25-28):
- YOLOv8s, 50 epochs: mAP50 0.995; mAP50-95 0.829; GFLOPS 28.4.
- YOLOv8s, 25 epochs: mAP50 0.995; mAP50-95 0.817; GFLOPS 28.4.
- YOLOv10s, 50 epochs: mAP50 0.985; mAP50-95 0.657; GFLOPS 24.5.
- YOLOv10s, 25 epochs: mAP50 0.977; mAP50-95 0.646; GFLOPS 24.5.
- YOLOv10s "require 25% fewer parameters" than YOLOv9-C (L209 citing [20]; L296 in conclusion).
- Split (train/val/test) and whether the mAP values are on validation or test images: not stated. Text says "Based on the training data results" (L248-249).
- Inference time: not stated.
