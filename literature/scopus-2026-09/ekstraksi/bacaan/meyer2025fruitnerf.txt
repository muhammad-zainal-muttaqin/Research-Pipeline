KEY
meyer2025fruitnerf. Meyer, Ardelean, Weyrich, Stamminger, "FruitNeRF++: A Generalized Multi-Fruit Counting Method Utilizing Contrastive Learning and Neural Radiance Fields," arXiv:2505.19863v1 (26 May 2025). Source text: literature/scopus-2026-09/teks/meyer2025fruitnerf.txt, lines 1-1000 read; references (l.1001 onward) skipped.

CROP / SETTING / SCALE
- Fruit types: apple, plum, lemon, pear, peach, mango (synthetic trees, l.262-265), and a real apple orchard (FUJI, l.272-273).
- Synthetic: 6 tree models, 300 images each rendered from the upper hemisphere (l.265-267). Table II caption: synthetic data at 1024x1024 px, 300 frames (notes, l.559-563).
- FUJI: 582 images from 12 trees at a commercial apple orchard (l.272-273). Training images downsampled to 1296 px x 864 px (l.277-278).
- Abstract frames the target as counting fruits "from unstructured input photographs of orchards" (l.6-8).
- Fruit size or cluster type is not specified beyond the list above in the text read.

ACQUISITION
- Synthetic: RGB and semantic images rendered with BlenderNeRF, with extrinsic and intrinsic camera parameters extracted (l.265-268). Ground-truth instance masks rendered in addition (l.268-270).
- FUJI: camera poses recovered with COLMAP applied to each side of the tree, then "manually registered both sides" (l.273-275, verified quote in QUOTES).
- Input is unordered RGB photographs: "Both our synthetic and real-world datasets consist of sets of unordered RGB images." (l.262-263).
- Poses are a known weak point: noisy poses are cited for the FUJI gap (l.833-836) and in Limitations (l.845-847).

OBSERVATION / DETECTOR
- Instance and semantic masks come from foundation models, not a trained custom model: "To achieve a unified fruit counting approach we utilize Grounded-SAM [37] and Detic [41] to predict precise instance segmentation masks without training a custom model." (l.280-282).
- Grounded-SAM = Grounding DINO (text-conditioned boxes) + SAM with SAM-HQ weights (l.288-295).
- Grounded-SAM prompt: fruit name, except mango and plum where "apple" was added (l.285-287).
- Detic uses the LVIS vocabulary; fruit-related LVIS classes are selected and filtered in post-processing (l.296-302, l.349-351).
- Table I IoU on synthetic data, Grounded-SAM vs Detic: mean 0.561 vs 0.49 (l.352-354). Per fruit (Grounded-SAM / Detic): apple 0.659 / 0.632; pear 0.519 / 0.468; plum 0.382 / 0.144 (plum+apple prompt); lemon 0.556 / 0.514; peach 0.662 / 0.654; mango 0.588 / 0.528 (mango+apple prompt) (l.315-345).
- Per-image masks are the input to the neural instance field (rendered per-image; notes, l.486-489).

ASSOCIATION METHOD
- Identity is encoded per fruit as an instance embedding in a neural instance field, trained with a contrastive loss (l.361-364 section list; notes, chunk 3a).
- Loss: photometric + semantic (BCE, Eq. 5) + contrastive, with weights set to 1; gradients blocked through the density field (notes, l.468-472).
- Contrastive term: InfoNCE adapted from MoCo (Eq. 6), then a prototype-based multi-positive loss (Eq. 7) (notes, l.417-467).
- Negatives: hard local negatives from the L-1 nearest 2D masks; global negatives across G groups (notes, l.498-505).
- Training is cascaded; the instance field is trained with the other fields frozen (notes, l.506-529).
- Clustering: point cloud sampled from the fields (notes, l.530-551). k-Means spatial partitioning into S partitions (l.705-710); HDBSCAN per partition with d_kl = lambda_c d_c + lambda_e d_e (Eq. 9, l.711-718).
- The clustering uses no fruit shape template, which is the change from FruitNeRF (l.69-72, l.113-116, l.857-864).

ASSUMPTIONS STATED OR IMPLIED BY AUTHORS
- Stated: foundation-model masks generalize to fruit "without the need for fine-tuning" (l.282-284).
- Stated: results depend on "a good coverage of the tree" (l.843-844).
- Stated: inaccurate poses cause "the embeddings of one fruit to contaminate the neighbouring features" (l.845-847).
- Implied: one fruit should map to one instance identity across views; the text says this fails under occlusion (l.848-850).
- Implied: clustered fruits are rare in the synthetic data, so Euclidean separation dominates (l.823-826).
- Implied: fruit-agnostic clustering needs no shape prior (l.863-864).

DOUBLE-COUNT SOURCES ADDRESSED
- Occlusion splitting: "both approaches face the issue that when a fruit is partially occluded by an obstacle, such as a leaf or branch, it may be detected as two separate instances." (l.356-358). "This leads to the feature space of a fruit being partially separated and incorrectly counted as two separate instances in the clustering phase." (l.358-360).
- Limitations repeat this: a leaf or branch "visually separates a fruit; this causes the feature embeddings of the two halves to be pulled apart." (l.848-850).
- Contrastive learning is the proposed remedy: instance embeddings are pulled together per fruit and pushed apart across fruits (notes, l.417-467). The paper does not quantify how often this fixes the split.
- Cross-view duplicates: handled implicitly by the shared instance field, not by an explicit cross-view matching step (notes, l.530-551). The text read does not report a measured double-count rate.

CLASS / MATURITY HANDLING
- No maturity classes. Classes are fruit types only; the method is "fully agnostic to the type of fruit" (l.862-863).
- Per-fruit prompts for Grounded-SAM (Table I "Prompt" column) and a shared LVIS vocabulary for Detic (Table I caption, l.306-313).
- Clustered growth habits are excluded: "challenges arise for fruits that grow in clusters, such as bananas, berries, or dates." (l.851-853).
- Generalization was also tested on household objects: 99 of 100 objects detected in Messy Rooms (l.865-867). This is a single preliminary experiment; no table or metric is given in the text read.

COUNT REFERENCE AND METRIC LEVEL
- Count level: per tree (Table II). The Table II caption says a count is obtained when a fruit centre is close to the GT centre (notes, l.559-563). The exact distance threshold is not stated in the text read.
- Metrics: Precision, Recall and F1 per fruit, computed from the count (Table II columns).
- Table II has three FruitNeRF++ mask sources (GT mask, SAM mask, Detic mask) and one FruitNeRF baseline column marked * with values taken from FruitNeRF [6] (Table II caption).
- FUJI: F1 only (l.827-830). The text read does not state how FUJI ground truth was defined. Not verified in the parts read.

KEY QUANTITATIVE RESULTS
- Table II F1, FruitNeRF* baseline (from [6]): apple 1, plum 0.885, lemon 0.978, pear 0.971, peach 0.987, mango 0.873 (notes, l.577-696).
- Table II F1, FruitNeRF++ GT masks: apple 0.996, plum 0.859, lemon 0.978, pear 0.996, peach 0.996, mango 0.713 (notes; mango R 0.557, P 0.988).
- FruitNeRF++ SAM masks: apple 0.996, plum 0.509, lemon 0.967, pear 0.967, peach 0.993, mango 0.561 (notes).
- FruitNeRF++ Detic masks: apple 0.998, plum 0.311, lemon 0.907, pear 0.965, peach 0.98, mango 0.438 (notes).
- Six-fruit averages: text reports GT 0.925, Grounded-SAM 0.832, Detic 0.776 (l.733-735, notes). My arithmetic from the per-fruit F1 values: GT 0.923, SAM 0.832, Detic 0.767 (mine; see INCONSISTENCIES).
- FUJI (Grounded-SAM masks, D=32, tau=0.35, lambda_e=5, 600k points, S=40): F1 0.765 (l.827-830).
- Parameter sweeps (Fig. 5, synthetic): D from 1 to 256; apples reach 1 at D=16 (notes, l.736-744). Temperature feasible region 0.1-0.3, best near 0.2 (notes, l.748-759).

FAILURE CONDITIONS / ERROR SOURCES REPORTED
- Mango: F1 0.713 with GT masks and recall 0.557; the text attributes poor mango results to "the large amount of fruits and high occlusions inside the tree" (notes, l.746-747).
- Plum with SAM and Detic: recall 0.341 and 0.184 (notes, l.601-696). Plum masks are the weakest Table I entries (IoU 0.382 Grounded-SAM, 0.144 Detic with plum+apple).
- Occlusion by leaves or branches splits instances (l.356-360, l.848-850).
- Noisy or inaccurate poses contaminate embeddings (l.845-847).
- Poor tree coverage (l.843-844).
- Fruits in clusters (bananas, berries, dates) are not handled (l.851-855).
- Apple clustering uses mainly Euclidean separation; embedding contribution is minor there (l.823-826).

COMPARISONS ON THE SAME DATA
- FruitNeRF (fruit-specific, cascaded clustering with templates): F1 values in Table II are from the FruitNeRF paper [6], not re-run (Table II caption).
- FUJI: FruitNeRF++ F1 0.765 vs Gené-Mola et al. [2] F1 0.881 (l.832-834). The paper attributes the gap to "the noisy poses and manual registration of both sides" (l.834-836). The two are not confirmed to share the same ground truth or protocol in the parts read.
- Gené-Mola et al. [2] and Häni et al. [5] are described as fruit-specific (apples), using instance masks and point-cloud clustering (l.102-111).
- Liu et al. [4] (mango, sequence tracking, triangulation) is described in related work only; no numeric comparison is given (l.96-101).

AUTHORS' STATED LIMITATIONS / FUTURE WORK
- Compute: "Training on larger scenes, like the Fuji dataset, takes about 8 hours on an Nvidia A5000 (24GB VRAM), making it unsuitable for real-time use." (l.838-840). "The slow convergence of the instance field accounts for more than half of the training time." (l.841-842).
- Data quality and coverage (l.843-847).
- Occlusion splits (l.848-850).
- Clustered fruits need new work: "Further research is needed to efficiently estimate cluster sizes and predict accurate cluster counts." (l.853-855).
- Future: incremental scene building with SLAM [50], Gaussian Splatting [51] or PAgNeRF [7] for real-time use; temporal information to "simplify fruit ID association" (l.875-881).
- Future: class-agnostic counting from a class name (Xu et al. [52]), currently 2D only (l.869-874).

INCONSISTENCIES INSIDE THE PAPER
1. Abstract says FruitNeRF++ "compares favorably to other state-of-the-art methods" (l.24-25). On FUJI, the reported F1 0.765 is below Gené-Mola et al. [2] at 0.881 (l.827-834).
2. Section V says the same parameters were used for all experiments (D=32, tau=0.2, lambda_c=1, lambda_e=1) (notes, l.729-731). The FUJI result uses tau=0.35, lambda_e=5, S=40 (l.827-830), and Section IV-E gives lambda_e=5 (notes, l.724-725). The D sweep uses lambda_e=0 (l.738-740; Fig. 5 caption l.819).
3. Table I caption says "For Grounded-SAM we used each fruit as a text prompt" (l.306-307). Body text says mango and plum prompts also included apple (l.285-287), and Table I rows show "plum, apple" and "mango, apple" (l.332-333, l.344-345). The caption omits this.
4. Six-fruit mean for GT masks: text 0.925 (l.733-734), my arithmetic 0.923. Detic: text 0.776 (l.735), my arithmetic 0.767. My arithmetic is from the per-fruit Table II values in the notes file; checked against the text only for SAM (0.832, matches).
5. Fig. 5 legend includes "Fuji-SAM" (l.818) but the caption does not explain it (l.819-822). Not confirmed against l.736-765 in this read.
