KEY: ogundele2025real. Ogundele et al. 2025, "Real-Time Strawberry Ripeness Classification and Counting: An Optimized YOLOv8s Framework with Class-Aware Multi-Object Tracking", Agriculture 2025, 15, 1906. Full text read in full; reference list skipped.

CROP / SETTING / SCALE: Strawberry (Fragaria x ananassa), three Korean cultivars (Seolhyang, Keumsil, Hongee) per the limitations section. Single greenhouse, varied conditions (dense clusters, leaf occlusion, overlapping fruits, camera angle and orientation changes). Dataset: 2190 original images, 4250 after augmentation, 13,228 labelled instances (Category I 6133, II 1675, III 5420). 37 videos collected; 20 used for counting evaluation ("20 distinct streams captured under greenhouse conditions", Sec 3.5, L1104-1111). Open field not tested (limitations).

ACQUISITION: Still images with a Sony Cyber-shot DSC-RX100 VII (1.0-type CMOS, ~20.1 MP, Table 2, L301-310). Video with an iPhone 13 Pro Max (Sec 2.2, L285-290). Camera mounted on a mobile platform at 0.8 m, distances 30-80 cm, captured in morning, noon and evening (L285-290). Video is taken along a row by a manually guided platform; the authors attribute tracker failures partly to "irregular, non-linear motion of the manually guided data collection platform" (Sec 3.6). Frame rate: not stated. Number of sides of the row / passes: not stated.

OBSERVATION / DETECTOR: YOLOv8s modified in three ways (Sec 2.3, L374-484): (1) C2f blocks replaced by C3x (1x3 and 3x1 convolutions; Table 4 params C2f 11,126,745 vs C3x 8,607,705); (2) an extra detection layer on the 160x160 feature map for small targets; (3) CIoU replaced by WIoU v3 (Tong et al.). Input size: not stated in the text read. Per-frame detection results: mAP@0.5 92.5%, recall 89.6%, precision 87.0%, F1 88.3% (Table 6, Sec 3.1). Per-class AP (Table 7/8): Category I 93.0, II 86.3, III 98.0. Detection is for three ripeness categories; bounding boxes annotated manually in Label Studio 1.16.0.

ASSOCIATION METHOD: ByteTrack (Zhang et al. 2022), with the Kalman state vector augmented by a class variable (L597-617). Motivation: dense small fruit with occlusion and ID switching caused by camera motion (L586-589). BYTE logic as described: high-confidence detections are matched first to Kalman tracklets, then low-confidence detections to unmatched tracklets (L592-596). Matching is done per class so that Category III and I are not swapped (L615-617). Counting rule: a virtual line (ROI 500x1080 px inside a 1920x1080 frame, L618-621). "unique track-IDs are assigned to individual strawberries as they cross the virtual counting line (ROI)" (Sec 3.5). A registry of IDs prevents recount: "If the ID was already registered, it was ignored, thereby preventing recounting due to occlusion or erratic movement." (L622-632). No appearance features or re-identification are used by the proposed tracker; no 3D, SfM or SLAM.

ASSUMPTIONS STATED OR IMPLIED BY AUTHORS: Each fruit crosses the virtual line once, so a unique ID at the line equals one fruit: "to ensure each fruit is counted only once" (Introduction, L147-148). Counting all detections in a video "can lead to errors" (L169-171). Camera motion causes ID switches (L586-589). Stable camera motion was used as a selection criterion for ground-truth videos: "The selection criteria prioritized videos exhibiting more stable camera motion to ensure a reliable basis for ground-truth." (L355-356). The stated assumption that fruits are sampled at a line crossing without return passes is not discussed.

DOUBLE-COUNT SOURCES ADDRESSED: (a) same fruit in consecutive frames: handled by tracking (ByteTrack) and by the unique-ID registry at the line. (b) track lost and restarted: handled by ByteTrack's low-confidence association, and the ID registry suppresses a second count of the same ID; re-identification of a restarted track with a new ID is not addressed. (c) same fruit from both sides or different passes: not addressed; the videos are single passes per platform trajectory in the text read. Authors do report errors where a fruit at the frame edge or in occluded clusters is counted wrongly (Sec 3.5, per-video discussion).

CLASS / MATURITY HANDLING: Three categories by surface redness: I = predominantly greenish/whitish; II = approximately 75% or less of surface red; III = fully red surface (Table 3, L324-336). Protocol "similar to that of our previous study [34]" (L313-316). Class is a state variable in the Kalman tracker: "To maintain distinct counts for each ripeness category, we integrated class information directly into the tracking state." (L597-600). Disagreement across frames: the text says matching is per class (L615-617); what happens when a single track changes class across frames is not described beyond that. Category II is the most ambiguous class (see Sec 3.5, "inherent visual ambiguity"). Misclassification "propagate[s] into the tracking and counting pipeline, impacting class-specific tallies even when the total fruit count remains relatively stable." (Sec 3.5).

COUNT REFERENCE AND METRIC LEVEL: Ground truth is a manual count per video, made by two independent operators after data collection (not per frame); "minor discrepancies seldom occurred in highly occluded scenes or under low-light conditions"; inter-operator difference "an average of approximately 2 fruits per video" (L357-361). Counting metrics: R2, MAE, RMSE, MAPE per class and in total (Table 10). Identity metrics (MOTA, IDF1, HOTA, ID switches): not reported. Detection metrics: mAP@0.5, precision, recall, F1 on the test split.

KEY QUANTITATIVE RESULTS:
- Split: train/val/test 8:1:1 on the 4250-image augmented set (L369-372).
- Detection (Table 6): mAP@50 92.5% (proposed), 92.1% (YOLOv8s), 92.6% (YOLOv8m); P 87.0, R 89.6, F1 88.3 (proposed).
- Ablation (Table 8, AP I / AP II / AP III / mAP50 / params M): A YOLOv8s 92.5/86.1/97.9/92.1/11.1; B C3x only 92.1/85.3/96.7/91.4/8.6; C C3x + head 92.5/86.6/97.8/92.3/8.05; D C3x + head + WIoU 93.0/86.3/98.0/92.5/8.0. Single run per configuration; no seeds stated.
- Efficiency (Table 9, params M / GFLOPS / size MB / inference ms): YOLOv8s 11.1/28.4/21.4/8.9; YOLOv8m 25.8/78.7/49.6/17.2; YOLOv8n 3.0/10.1/9.94/6.8; YOLOv3-tiny 12.1/18.9/23.2/5.6; YOLOv5s-p6 15.3/24.2/29.4/10.6; YOLOv6s 16.3/44.0/31.3/11.0; proposed 8.0/27.1/15.7/9.7. Text: 27.9% fewer parameters than YOLOv8s; 9.7 ms per image, about 103 FPS (L1078-1096).
- Counting (Table 10): Category III R2 0.950, MAE 2.65, RMSE 3.37, MAPE 11.63; Category II R2 0.752, MAE 2.70, RMSE 3.16, MAPE 16.25; Category I R2 0.910, MAE 4.20, RMSE 5.07, MAPE 9.62; Total R2 0.914, MAE 8.55, RMSE 9.63, MAPE 9.52.
- Per-video accuracy range "84.51% to 95.24%" (Sec 3.5); lowest video 19 (84.5%, under-count), highest video 4 (89.4%, over-count) per text. Per-video values are shown only in a figure, not a table.
- Tracker comparison on the same detector (Figure 17, values not in text except DeepSORT): ByteTrack proposed; DeepSORT R2 0.842; SORT, Norfair and MOTPY: "fragmented trajectories and a high number of undercounts" (Sec 3.6).

FAILURE CONDITIONS / ERROR SOURCES REPORTED:
- Occlusion by leaves and fruit clusters; errors at frame edges (L780-789).
- Category II: "inherent visual ambiguity" (mottled red, white and green) and class imbalance (L917-932).
- Category I: "green-on-green" problem, citing [48,49] (L910-916).
- Cluster density, leaf occlusion and lighting cause per-video error (Sec 3.5).
- DeepSORT over-counts from Re-ID feature matching errors (Sec 3.6).
- SORT, Norfair, MOTPY: fragmented tracks and undercounts, attributed to occlusion and platform motion; Kalman prediction "insufficient" (Sec 3.6).

COMPARISONS ON THE SAME DATA:
- Same YOLOv8s detector with ByteTrack, SORT, DeepSORT, Norfair [50] and motpy [51] (Figure 17, Sec 3.6). Only DeepSORT R2 (0.842) is given in text.
- Detector variants on the same test split (Table 6, Table 9): proposed mAP50 92.5 vs YOLOv3-tiny 90.1, YOLOv5s-p6 91.0, YOLOv6s 90.5, YOLOv8n 90.5, YOLOv8s 92.1, YOLOv8m 92.6. Text gains: 2.4, 1.5, 2.0, 2.0, 0.4 points (L796-799; checked against Table 6, matches).
- Cross-study: Li et al. [30] Faster R-CNN mAP 87.3%, counting accuracy 99.1% on ripe fruit; Zhou et al. [31] YOLOv5s + multiple mapping, relative counting error 6.7%. Authors say direct comparison is "inherently challenging" (Sec 3.6).

AUTHORS' STATED LIMITATIONS / FUTURE WORK: single greenhouse; three cultivars; open field not tested; three-level ripeness scale simplifies continuous maturation; no yield in weight (kg) estimated, and size estimation with depth sensing is proposed as future work (Sec 4, L1243-1271).

INCONSISTENCIES INSIDE THE PAPER:
- Conclusion (L1273-1289): "achieved an mAP@0.5 of 92.5%, outperforming the baseline YOLOv8s and other common variants while being 27.9% more lightweight". Table 6 shows YOLOv8m at 92.6%, above the proposed 92.5%. Section 3.1 (L798-799) itself states YOLOv8m achieved "the highest detection accuracy of 92.6% mAP". The conclusion's "outperforming ... other common variants" is therefore not true for YOLOv8m on mAP50.
- Recall claim (L801-802): "Our model achieved a recall of 89.6%, a substantial increase of 3.4% over the baseline YOLOv8s and other models." The 3.4 is percentage points against YOLOv8s (86.2). Against YOLOv8m (89.3) the gap is 0.3 points and against YOLOv8n (88.7) it is 0.9 points, so "and other models" overstates the margin. Also "increase of 3.4%" mixes points and percent.
- Abstract vs body: mAP 92.5, R2 0.914, MAPE 9.52, R2 0.950 for ripe fruit all match Table 10 and Sec 3.5. No mismatch found.
- Sec 3.5 text (L1133-1135) contains a typo, "This result correlation represents"; not a numerical inconsistency.
- Dataset count: 2190 original images vs 4250 after augmentation; split is stated on the 4250 set (L362-372). The text does not say whether augmented copies of the same frame could appear in both train and test.
