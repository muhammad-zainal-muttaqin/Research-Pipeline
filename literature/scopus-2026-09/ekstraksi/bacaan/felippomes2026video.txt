KEY: felippomes2026video (Felippo Mes et al., 2026, video-based apple counting with RGB-D sensors on a fruiting wall; full text at literature/scopus-2026-09/teks/felippomes2026video.txt, reference list skipped)

CROP / SETTING / SCALE: Apple (fruiting wall, one orchard). IRTA Mollerussa orchard. 420 trees, row spacing 3.6 m, tree spacing 1 m. Scanning on 6 Sep 2021 and 28 Sep 2021; harvest 5 Oct 2021. Single orchard, homogeneous architecture (Section 4.4). Counting unit: stretches of row 2 (stretches 4-6 used in the dataset, lines 286-288). Number of fruits in total: not stated in the read text.

ACQUISITION: Video along the row, carried on a platform at 2 km/h. Scanned east and west sides of the row (two sides). Sensors at 162 cm height, placed 1.25, 1.75 and 2.25 m from the row axis. Two RGB-D cameras: Azure Kinect and ZED 2 (Table 1, both at 30 fps). Scanning distance is one of the tested variables (225 cm is the reported setting in Table 4; the abstract states longer scanning distance improves accuracy). Frame count: 1723 frames from row 2 stretches 4-6, split 1295 / 203 / 225 (train / validation / test). Repeated scans on two dates (06/09 and 28/09) are used for calibration and evaluation.

OBSERVATION / DETECTOR: YOLOv5x with COCO pretraining; SGD, learning rate 0.01, batch size 4; hardware RTX 2080 Ti (Section 2). Detector F1 0.834 and mAP@0.5 0.871 for YOLOv5; YOLO26 F1 0.819 and mAP@0.5 0.865 (Section 4.1). Input size: not stated in the read text. Detection targets: apples. Raw per-frame counts are then calibrated by a regression fitted per sensor, scanning distance, date and side (Section 2.4).

ASSOCIATION METHOD: Multi-object tracker assigns an ID to each apple across successive frames (Section 2.2). Compared trackers: ByteTrack (retained), SORT, DeepSORT (Table 3). BoT-SORT was tested and gave about +4% MOTA, +3% IDF1 and +6% HOTA over ByteTrack at about 5x processing time; ByteTrack was kept (Section 4.1). No 3D reconstruction, SfM or SLAM described in the read text. No object-level fusion or cross-view matching between east and west scans (Section 2.4, Section 4.2). Counting rule: per-stretch count of tracked IDs, then regression calibration of the raw count against manual count.

ASSUMPTIONS STATED OR IMPLIED BY AUTHORS: Fruits hidden from one angle may be visible from the other (Introduction). Overlapping perspectives create duplicate detections (Introduction). The calibration coefficients are specific to the orchard and acquisition setup, namely sensor, scanning distance, date and side (Section 2.4). Raw counts are "a proxy of fruit load rather than an exact count" (Section 4.3). Enhancement is for stretch-level yield estimation, not instance-level accuracy (Section 5).

DOUBLE-COUNT SOURCES ADDRESSED: (a) same fruit in consecutive frames: addressed by the tracker and ID persistence (Section 2.2). (b) track lost and restarted: not stated as a separate problem in the read text; tracker comparison (ByteTrack, SORT, DeepSORT, BoT-SORT) is the only related treatment (Section 4.1). (c) same fruit from both sides of the row or different passes: not addressed. The paper states that no object-level fusion or cross-view matching was performed, so fruit visible from both viewpoints "may be counted twice in the intermediate observations" (Section 2.4). Dual-side improvement is attributed to complementary canopy visibility, not to de-duplication (Section 4.2). Count is interpreted at stretch level only (Section 5).

CLASS / MATURITY HANDLING: none. Single class (apple). Maturity or ripeness classes not stated.

COUNT REFERENCE AND METRIC LEVEL: Count reference: manual count per stretch (for calibration and regression, Section 2.4 and 3.2). Count error: MAE, MAPE and RMSE per stretch (Table 4). Regression R2 on raw and calibrated counts (Section 3.2). Identity metrics for tracking: MOTA, IDF1, HOTA (Table 3), evaluated on the same 1723-frame set used for annotation (see inconsistencies). Detection metrics: F1, mAP@0.5 (Section 4.1). ID switches: not stated.

KEY QUANTITATIVE RESULTS:
- Table 3, ByteTrack: MOTA 0.6817, IDF1 0.8369, HOTA 0.6894, 15.4 ms per image.
- Table 3, SORT: MOTA 0.6401, IDF1 0.8091, HOTA 0.6804, 15.3 ms.
- Table 3, DeepSORT: MOTA 0.5735, IDF1 0.7646, HOTA 0.6824, 128.0 ms.
- Section 4.1: BoT-SORT about +4% MOTA, +3% IDF1, +6% HOTA over ByteTrack at about 5x processing time.
- Section 4.1: YOLO26 F1 0.819 vs YOLOv5 F1 0.834; mAP@0.5 0.865 vs 0.871.
- Table 4, Azure Kinect at 225 cm, 28/09: MAE 35.07, MAPE 7.48%, RMSE 46.98.
- Table 4, ZED 2 at 225 cm, 28/09: MAE 31.71, MAPE 6.91%, RMSE 43.04.
- Section 3.2, raw-count R2: Azure Kinect 0.702; ZED 2 0.744.
- Section 3.2, post-correction R2 at 225 cm: Azure Kinect 0.686 (06/09) and 0.682 (28/09); ZED 2 0.519 (06/09) and 0.733 (28/09).
- Section 3.3: side effect significant only for RMSE (p = 0.024). Adjusted RMSE 46.73 (both sides), 59.36 (west), 58.69 (east).

FAILURE CONDITIONS / ERROR SOURCES REPORTED:
- Duplicate detections from overlapping views (Introduction, Section 2.4).
- Single orchard and one architecture; generalisation not tested (Section 4.4).
- Scanning distance and sensor change the calibration; close partial views are worse than full-tree views (Abstract).
- Raw counts are a load proxy, not exact counts (Section 4.3).

COMPARISONS ON THE SAME DATA: Azure Kinect vs ZED 2 on the same stretches (Table 4, Section 3.2). Trackers ByteTrack, SORT, DeepSORT, BoT-SORT on the same dataset (Table 3, Section 4.1). Single side vs dual side compared on the same orchard; dual-side gives higher accuracy, attributed to visibility (Section 4.2).

AUTHORS' STATED LIMITATIONS / FUTURE WORK: Evaluation in one orchard and one architecture; "further studies evaluating the proposed system under a wider range of orchard conditions ... would be valuable" (Section 4.4). No cross-view identity matching implemented (Section 4.2, Section 5). Calibration is setup-specific (Section 2.4).

INCONSISTENCIES INSIDE THE PAPER:
- Stretches 4-6 of row 2 are excluded from counting (lines 286-288), but the evaluation uses row 2 stretches 5 and 6 (lines 316-318 and 597-599).
- The 1723-frame set is used both for annotation and for tracker evaluation (Section 2.3 and Section 4.1).
- The abstract gives MAPE 6.91% and R2 0.733 without the sensor, distance or date in the summary; those values belong to ZED 2 at 225 cm on 28/09 (Table 4, Section 3.2). The Conclusion (lines 1342-1344) repeats them without the sensor or date.
