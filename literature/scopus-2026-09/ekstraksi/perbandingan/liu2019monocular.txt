C1 TYPE: ACQUISITION
C1 ARMS: Mono Final (monocular camera only, full pipeline) vs Suite Multi-view (camera + 3D LiDAR + GPS/INS/RTK, all sequence images, system from [3]); both on the same 18 trees and the same image sequences; the benchmark uses the same Faster R-CNN detector (lines 742-744)
C1 RESULTS: Per-tree count error mean: Mono Final 27.8, Suite Multi-view 19.8; per-tree count error std dev: Mono Final 29.6, Suite Multi-view 22.9 (Figure 10, lines 801-806). Linear regression of estimated vs field count: slope 0.86 (Mono Final) vs 0.97 (Suite Multi-view); R2 0.78 vs 0.88 (lines 822-824). Best arm: Suite Multi-view on error mean, std dev, slope and R2.
C1 WHERE: Figure 10 (lines 801-806); text lines 816-828; Figure 9 (lines 793-797)
C1 QUOTE: "For the monocular camera system, the slope is 0.86 compared to 0.97 for the sensor suite system, and the R2 value is 0.78 compared to 0.88." (lines 822-824)
C1 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: The difference is concentrated in trees with high counts. The authors attribute it to occlusion and to the third double-counting source: with two point clouds from opposite sides, the trunk-centroid rule rejects fruit that lies behind the trunk from one side and is not visible from the other, which causes more undercounting (lines 829-843). They propose a better point-cloud merge as a fix (lines 844-848).
C1 CONDITIONS: Mango, 18 trees chosen from all 10 rows; UGV with Prosilica GT3300C camera, 3296 x 2472 px at 5 Hz (lines 758-760); the sensor-suite benchmark uses the same detector and RTK poses (lines 742-745). Mono Final uses only 2D images.

C4 RESULTS: Reconstruction time: about 5 minutes (semantic SfM) vs about 10 hours (SIFT SfM), on a 4-core i7 CPU; 2D tracking time excluded (lines 856-860).
