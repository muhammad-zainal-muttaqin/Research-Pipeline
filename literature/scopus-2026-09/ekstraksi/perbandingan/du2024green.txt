C1 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: Appearance matching "effectively alleviates the two primary types of false associations stemming from the motion feature mutations of green pepper fruit, leading to a reduction in the number of ID switches" (lines 1143-1147). Track optimization alleviates incorrect target-to-track matching by using track context (lines 1149-1154). Lower light is said to cause correlation errors attributed to changes in motion features (lines 817-820).
C1 CONDITIONS: Radio-controlled car at 0.2 m/s with a phone camera (lines 511-518). Source video 1920x1080, 30 fps. CS_YOLOv5s runs at 17 FPS, below the 30 FPS source rate; a 15 FPS real-time setting would need a halved car speed (lines 1126-1137). This was not tested and is stated as future work.
