C1 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: For TrackRCNN, the authors attribute negative scores to "too many ID switches of the instances" (lines 744-746). For Grounded-SAM2 mode A, they attribute the best result to "the favorable side-view perspective and moderate proximity to the canopy, which facilitates clearer object contours and minimizes severe occlusions" (lines 941-943). For Grounded-SAM2 mode D, they say it "detected significantly fewer apple instances" so its high MOTSP has limited practical value (lines 944-948; line 749-750). For mode C, the authors state only that performance "degrades markedly" and that Grounded-SAM2 "still delivers superior precision compared to AppleMOTS in every mode, suggesting greater robustness to variations in flight dynamics" (lines 751-754). Reason for the mode C drop is not stated.
