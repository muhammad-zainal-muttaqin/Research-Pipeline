CROP AND DATA: orange orchard; crop-row videos recorded with an Intel RealSense camera (30 FPS, pyrealsense2) mounted on a farm vehicle (lines 589-601, 600-603 region; speed 3-5 km/h per line 1257-1258). Five row videos with 20, 20, 19, 18 and 22 trees (Table 2, lines 625-640; total 99). Row 5 (22 trees) excluded from experiments because of sunlight flare (lines 572-580), leaving 77 trees; 847 frames annotated (77 trees x 11 frames, lines 640-650). Reference type: manual annotation of visible oranges (at least 50% of surface visible) on each frame and on the tree sequence (lines 653-665). Tree label = average of the frame annotations (Eq. 3, lines 768-790). Not a hand count on the tree and not a harvest.
SAME-DATA COMPARISON: yes
C1 TYPE: ACQUISITION
C1 ARMS: one frame per tree (1-frame) versus tree-centric sequences of 3, 7 and 11 frames, each summarised by the sample median (S = median, lines 740-765). All arms use the same 77 trees and the same fourfold row-based cross-validation (lines 888-894). The arms differ only in how many frames of the tree are used. Evaluated for three regressors: EfficientNetB0, FPN-linear, FPN-map.
