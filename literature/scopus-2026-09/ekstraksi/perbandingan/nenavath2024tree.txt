C1 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: Four-side counting is "too far from the actual result", eight-sided counting is "too close to the reality of the tree fruit count" (lines 689-693). The authors conclude "employing the 8-side model is preferable to the 4-side model when counting the fruit on the tree" (lines 692-694). The double-count reason for eight sides: at the corners, mangoes "will counted twice" when only eight exact directional images are used, so the eight-sided model with Eq. (5) is used to avoid double counting (lines 753-766). Why the 4-side arm undercounts is not stated.
