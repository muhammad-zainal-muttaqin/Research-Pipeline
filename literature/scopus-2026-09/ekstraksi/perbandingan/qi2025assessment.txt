C1 TYPE: TRACKER
C1 ARMS: Four trackers on the same test videos, each run with two detectors (YOLOv8 and RT-DETR), at 30 fps (Table 3, lines 702-734; text lines 644-647): SORT (Kalman plus IoU, no appearance); DeepSort (appearance ReID); ByteTrack (two-stage, low-confidence boxes used, no ReID); BotSort (ReID plus camera motion compensation).   - YOLOv8: SORT MOTA 80.1 %, Prel 94.0 %; DeepSort MOTA 82.1 %, Prel 91.1 %; ByteTrack MOTA 81.4 %, Prel 94.4 %; BotSort MOTA 82.0 %, Prel 93.5 %. Best MOTA: DeepSort. Best Prel: ByteTrack.

C1 QUOTE: "Given the previous sensitivity analyses, we can rationally conclude that the combined utilization of RT-DETR and ByteTrack leads to the most accurate and robust counting algorithm." (lines 668-671)
C1 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: ReID trackers: "The trackers that have a ReID module, such as DeepSort and BotSort, ensure a higher MOTA score, indicating the potential of the ReID module to reduce ID switches." (lines 652-654). ReID can fail: "batches with analogous postures and ripening stages may confuse the appearance-based identification module" (lines 655-657). Motion-only trackers: SORT and ByteTrack "can effectively distinguish bunches of various fruits regardless of appearance similarities" (lines 657-661). BotSort: "the addition of a motion compensation feature in BotSort contributes to its superior identification accuracy" (lines 662-664). DeepSort with YOLOv8 has higher Prel than with RT-DETR, which the authors attribute to more false detections producing more IDs (lines 647-652).
C1 CONDITIONS: Fixed camera on platform, 30 fps (lines 644-646); fixed track in greenhouse, platform speed 0.3 m/s; similar appearance of bunches (lines 655-657, 784-786).
