CROP AND DATA: Potted kumquat (ornamental kumquat, 四季观赏型金桔); test data: nine potted kumquat plants, 149 fruits in total, RGB-D (Kinect 2.0) point clouds captured at six angles at 60-degree intervals on a motorised rotating turntable; reference type: hand count of the actual fruits on the plants ("真实果实数", 149 fruits in total, Table 1). (Lines 42-45, 104-141, 498-506.)
SAME-DATA COMPARISON: yes

C1 TYPE: MECHANISM
C1 ARMS: (A) Method of reference [13] ("文献[13]方法", MENDEZ et al. 2019): colour filtering to remove background and green fruit plus an improved ISODATA algorithm for clustering, applied by the authors themselves to the registered complete point cloud of the same plants, with parameters modified to fit this dataset. (B) Proposed method ("本文方法"): multi-angle point-cloud registration (RANSAC cylinder fitting to obtain the rotation axis, initial registration by rotation about that axis, point-to-plane ICP for fine registration), Euclidean clustering, and RANSAC spherical fitting to recognise and count fruits, independent of colour information. The two arms differ in the mechanism that turns the merged observations of the whole plant into fruit counts: colour-based clustering versus colour-independent 3D sphere fitting. (Lines 614-625, 320-334, 386-412.)
C1 RESULTS: Table 2, arms and values exactly as printed: reference [13] method - recall (R) 97.10%, precision (P) 61.47%, F1 58.05%, MAPE 44.97%, R2 0.73; proposed method - recall (R) 85.91%, precision (P) 79.01%, F1 82.32%, MAPE 16.02%, R2 0.97. On the printed values, the reference [13] arm is best on recall (97.10% vs 85.91%); the proposed arm is best on precision (79.01% vs 61.47%), F1 (82.32% vs 58.05%), MAPE (lower is better, 16.02% vs 44.97%), and R2 (0.97 vs 0.73). The proposed arm's recall, precision, F1, MAPE and R2 on these data are also reported at lines 536-540 and 629-639. (Lines 557-580, 536-540, 629-639.)
C1 WHERE: Table 2 (表2 果实识别与计数结果对比 / Tab.2 Comparison of fruit identification and counting results), source lines 557-580; statement that reference [13]'s method was applied to this paper's own data, lines 614-615; description of that arm, lines 616-624.
C1 QUOTE: "将文献[13]方法应用于本文的数据中，和本文方法对比结果如表2所示。" (lines 614-615)
C1 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: The authors say the colour filter of reference [13] removes many green fruits and leaves only a few yellow ones, so its precision is high but many green fruits cannot be recognised; reference [13]'s algorithm suits large, well-separated fruit and works poorly on this dataset's small, clustered fruit, so its recall, F1 and R2 are lower than the proposed method's (lines 628-636). They also state that the proposed method relies on 3D spherical contour features rather than colour, so it recognises immature green fruit and clustered or occluded fruit (lines 53-55, 548-551, 622-625).
C1 CONDITIONS: Kinect 2.0 RGB-D camera (colour 1920x1080, depth 512x424, best range 0.8-2.0 m); plant on a turntable of 1.0 m circumference about 1.0 m from the camera; six views at 60-degree intervals; fruit diameter 1.0-3.5 cm; plant height 55-75 cm, width 30-50 cm; fruit immature and green, often occluded by leaves or clustered (lines 104-141, 521-522, 546-551). Processing time about 90 s per plant (about 60 s of preprocessing) on an Intel Core i7-8550U at 1.80 GHz x4 with 8 GB RAM (lines 584-590).
