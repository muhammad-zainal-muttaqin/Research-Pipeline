- Excluded: leaf removal treatments (one side, two sides, control). Each treatment is a different set of vines with a different canopy condition, evaluated separately (Table 1, lines 409-473). A canopy condition is not an acquisition arm of the kind listed in the rule, and no arm changes how fruit is linked or counted. Same logic as the pruned vs unpruned exclusion in mendez2019field.
- Borderline, not recorded: detected point count vs projected area of detected points as the yield proxy, same data (Table 1 columns; lines 490-501, 507-509). Projected area reached R2 above 0.75 in three two-side or control rows (Gr-19 two sides 0.81, Gr-19+Ga-20 two sides 0.77, Gr-19 control 0.82), and the point count reached at most 0.66 (Gr-19 control, lines 495-497). This compares two estimators on one data set; it does not change how observations are linked or counted across images, so it was judged outside the rule. A reviewer may choose to record it as COUNTING-RULE.
