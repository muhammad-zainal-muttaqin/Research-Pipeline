C2 WHERE: Table VII (lines 609-619); text lines 551-563 (BoT-SORT) and 564-575 (ByteTrack); Figures 8 and 9 (captions lines 562-563 and 573-575).
C2 QUOTE: "The combination of RF-DETR and ByteTrack (Figure 9) produced the weakest performance overall, recording the highest errors across all metrics, with an MAE of 29.28 and an MRE of 22.12%." (lines 564-567)
C2 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: The authors attribute the overcounting to detector false positives "which are then propagated and reinforced by the tracker" (lines 556-558) for RF-DETR with BoT-SORT. For RF-DETR with ByteTrack they write that the errors "likely stem from compounded issues, including overcounting at the detection stage and unstable tracking behavior due to frequent ID switches or fragmented trajectories" (lines 569-572). The explanation for the tracker gap is therefore partly detector-side and is not isolated by the design.
C2 CONDITIONS: Same as C1 (videos with motion blur and occlusion, lines 330-333). The authors state that overcounting is more pronounced in high-density scenes (lines 552-553, 562-563). Frame rate, platform, speed: not stated.
