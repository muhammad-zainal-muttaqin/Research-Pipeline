KEY: nuske2014modeling
CROP AND DATA: grapevine (Vitis vinifera/hybrid, Chardonnay, Riesling, Traminette); test data: Chardonnay 2011 vineyard dataset (individual vine and row predictions, overall yield of the vines in the dataset; vine count not stated in the text read, lines 466-521); laboratory dataset 56 Thompson Seedless clusters (lines 358-372); reference type: harvest weight (vine and row yield) and cluster weight (lab, Table 2)
SAME-DATA COMPARISON: yes
C1 TYPE: MECHANISM
C1 ARMS: Chardonnay 2011 yield prediction with two calibration sources applied to the same image berry counts. Arm A: calibration from destructive hand samples taken at the time of imaging. Arm B: calibration from prior harvest data (2010 Traminette and 2010 Riesling datasets). Extrapolation of the hand samples alone is reported as a third reference estimate (not an arm of the method).
C1 RESULTS: Overall prediction error (Fig. 4b, text values): hand calibration 3% (best); 2010 Traminette calibration 4%; 2010 Riesling calibration -4.5% (text: "an under-prediction of overall weight by 4.5%"); hand-sample extrapolation -13% (least accurate). Average absolute per-vine error: "around 18% for all approaches" (lines 516-517); the text at lines 495-496 gives 17 to 19% across calibration approaches, with hand calibration "slightly more accurate". Row error: between 7% and 8% (lines 497-498), per-arm values not stated in text. Per-arm values for the per-vine and row bars are not printed in the text; only the ranges above are stated. Hand calibration is best on all three text-stated metrics it is reported on.
C1 WHERE: Fig. 4 (lines 507-520); Section 5.4 text (lines 494-502, 516-520); Fig. 3 (lines 481-484) describes the prior-harvest calibration.
C1 QUOTE: "The hand calibration was most accurate at 3% error and using calibration from the 2010 Traminette dataset had 4% error." (lines 499-500)
C1 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: "The calibration from hand samples is slightly more accurate than the calibration from prior harvest data." (lines 512-514). The authors attribute the remaining per-vine error to distribution: "the individual vine errors are well distributed and average out" (lines 517-518). The cause of the hand-sample advantage is not stated.
C1 CONDITIONS: Prior-harvest calibration predicted Chardonnay 2011 yield 12 weeks before harvest (lines 484, 491); the text also says the system was evaluated "at both 10 days from harvest and 90 days" (lines 523-524). Visible berry count used as the image measure for these results (lines 474-476). Hand-sample calibration done at the time of imaging (lines 467-469).
NOTES: (1) Table 2 (lines 358-372) reports lab image measures against weight (total berry count r2 0.95, visible berry count 0.88, ellipsoid 0.85, convex hull 0.92).
