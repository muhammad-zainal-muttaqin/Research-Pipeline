C1 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: The appearance features of DeepSORT did not improve tracking, which the authors attribute to "the inherent similarity in appearance among apples, which may limit the effectiveness of the Re-ID network" (lines 349-354; also lines 443-447). ByteTrack's second association step is described as making it "more robust in the presence of objects partially occluded" (lines 265-269), but this is a method description and not an explanation of the measured gap. The authors note this contrasts with Villacrés et al. (2023), who found DeepSORT better than SORT (lines 354-357).
C1 CONDITIONS: camera moved along the row at 0.556 m/s; sensor 2.25 m from the row axis; 30 fps video; two dates (about four weeks and one week before harvest, lines 220-224); fruit colour changes between dates (bicoloured to fully red); detector YOLOv5x fine-tuned, F1 0.834 and mAP 0.871 on the detector (lines 332-335). Occlusion level is not quantified.
