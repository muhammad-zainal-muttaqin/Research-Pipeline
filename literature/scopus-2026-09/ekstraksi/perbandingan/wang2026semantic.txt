C1 QUOTE: "However, its performance is constrained by its reliance on the DBSCAN clustering algorithm and predefined object templates: the initialisation process significantly impacts clustering performance, and the morphological variability of real-world fruits makes precise counting with predefined templates difficult." (lines 940-945)
C1 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: The authors attribute FruitNeRF's weaker results to its DBSCAN clustering, whose results depend on the initialisation, and to predefined templates that cannot fit the morphological variability of real fruits (lines 940-945). The proposed method is credited with adaptive point-cloud clustering plus geometric features, giving "stronger robustness to variation in fruit type, morphology, and scene complexity" (lines 972-974).
C1 CONDITIONS: Three datasets; synthetic set with eight fruit types; Fuji-SfM described as "complex agricultural scenarios" (line 926-927). Wilcoxon signed-rank test on MAE is reported (lines 976-979) but its statistics were not read in detail. The proposed method also uses a semantic NeRF reconstruction, which FruitNeRF does not, so the difference is not isolated to the clustering step.
