KEY: sun2020three
CROP AND DATA: cotton (field, ground-based mobile platform with five Fuji X-A10 cameras on a tractor, 1.5 fps; boll counts from 3D point clouds). Test data: 30 plots (SVT field and NAM field, 15 plots each; SVT sampled Nov 22 2017 per line 807, NAM Dec 14 2017 per line 808; lines 210-264, 283-285). Reference type: manual harvesting of bolls per plot (lines 802-804, 1098-1101 acknowledges hand harvesting).
SAME-DATA COMPARISON: no
C-entries: none qualify under the inclusion rule.
NOTES: The only same-plot arm pair the authors report is VCCS versus CRGS over-segmentation (lines 764-770, 786-790, 802-824; Fig. 8a-d; Table 2 at lines 957-998). The two arms differ only in the over-segmentation step that precedes the same SVM classifier and the same DBSCAN clustering. That difference is a segmentation module, not one of TRACKER, COMPONENT, MECHANISM, COUNTING-RULE or ACQUISITION, so it is excluded under the detector-module rule. For reference, the authors state that "similar boll counting performance was obtained using both VCCS- and CRGS-generated region datasets" (lines 822-824). Reported values for that pair, for the record only: boll-number correlation with fiber yield, SVT R2 0.91 (VCCS) vs 0.87 (CRGS), MAPE 7.99 vs 8.15, RMSE 77.09 vs 93.57; NAM R2 0.90 vs 0.90, MAPE 19.29 vs 17.75, RMSE 143.84 vs 141.01 (Table 2, lines 969-983). Boll-volume correlation: SVT R2 0.66 vs 0.66, MAPE 17.10 vs 17.59, RMSE 152.05 vs 151.70; NAM R2 0.84 vs 0.85, MAPE 37.86 vs 34.36, RMSE 179.94 vs 173.25 (lines 984-998). Table 2 measures yield correlation, not count accuracy, so it is not a counting-rule or association comparison. The paper reports the best per-plot boll count accuracy as "~95% (MAPE = 5.08%) and RMSE of 16.87" (lines 804-805) without naming the arm, and the 30-plot average as "~90%" (line 804) without a per-arm value. Multi-boll cluster splitting by volume ratio (lines 597-709) is described as the method, with error analysis at lines 825-871 and Fig. 9; no same-plot arm with and without splitting is reported. The 3D SfM reconstruction is the proposed method; no same-data 2D or no-3D arm is reported.
