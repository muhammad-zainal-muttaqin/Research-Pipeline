KEY: zaenker2021viewpoint
CROP AND DATA: capsicum (sweet pepper). Test data: two simulated scenarios (Scenario 1 static pole, Scenario 2 ceiling-hung arm), each with four simulated plants and seven fruits per plant with fruit meshes; 20 trials per arm per scenario (lines 495-509, 533). Reference type: simulated ground truth (fruit bounding boxes and octree of fruit meshes, lines 495-499). The real glasshouse run has no ground truth and no numerical analysis (lines 660-663), so it is not usable here.
SAME-DATA COMPARISON: yes (with a caveat: the arms are viewpoint-planning strategies, not association methods; see NOTES)
C1 TYPE: ACQUISITION
C1 ARMS: Scenario 1 (simulated, static pole). "Ours" = ROI-targeted sampling switched with exploration sampling (Sec. V-D). "Explo" = pure exploration sampling at frontiers of occupied cells without ROIs, similar to Monica et al. (lines 524-529). Both use the same fruit clustering (26-neighbourhood) and the same planning time of 3 minutes (lines 471-482, 523). The arms differ in which viewpoints are sampled, hence in the views acquired. Utility suffixes UP (proximity count) and UU (unobserved voxels) are shown as separate rows in the table; both are included below.

C2 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: Lines 613-619: "A reason why the simpler metric achieves better results could be the computation time." The authors add that the distance to the nearest ROI must be computed at each step for UP, so the cost is higher in the larger environment, and the simpler UU metric lets more poses be reached within the planning time (lines 614-619). Lines 623-626 state that the sampling approach outperforms exploration sampling in this scenario.
C2 CONDITIONS: Simulated only. Same statistical test and planning time as C1. The larger environment is the stated reason for the UU advantage (line 616-619).
NOTES: (1) Classification caveat: the arms are viewpoint-selection strategies. They change which views are acquired, while the fruit-linking step (clustering of ROI voxels, lines 471-482) is the same for every arm. Recorded as ACQUISITION because the number and position of views differ between arms; if the protocol is read as allowing only association or counting differences, both C1 and C2 should be excluded. (2) Text versus table, Scenario 1: lines 539-541 say Ours-UP is best overall, but Table I gives Ours-UU the highest number of detected ROIs (13.8 vs 13.2) and Explo-UP the lowest center distance (2.23 vs 2.41). (3) The bold significance marking referred to in the Table I caption (line 597) is not present in the extracted text, so significance could not be checked against the table. (4) The UP vs UU utility comparison (lines 528-531) is a scoring-metric comparison inside the planner, not an association mechanism; it is not recorded as a separate comparison. (5) The real glasshouse experiment is a single qualitative trial without ground truth (lines 660-665) and is not recorded.
