C2 TYPE: MECHANISM
C2 ARMS: YOLOv8+NeRF (detections lifted to a shared 3D NeRF coordinate space, then clustered into fruit, DBSCAN in the method description) vs YOLOv8+BoT-SORT and YOLOv8+ByteTrack (2D detection with trajectory tracking on the same rendered orbit videos). C2 RESULTS (Table II, as printed): YOLOv8+NeRF: MAE 1.5; MSE 2.5; MAPE 9.375%; Accuracy 90.625%. Best on all four metrics. Compared with the best 2D arm (ByteTrack, C1): MAE 3.6 vs 1.5; MSE 14 vs 2.5; MAPE 22.5% vs 9.375%; Accuracy 77.5% vs 90.625%.
