KEY: song2014automatic
CROP AND DATA: Sweet pepper (Capsicum) in greenhouse, 3 m high plants; 148 recombinant inbred lines, four camera heights, image every 5 cm along aisles (Section 2, Conclusions lines 846-850). Test data: 10 experimental plots for visual identification (Fig. 8); validation trial of 435 plots with at least 12 images at each of 4 camera heights (lines 600-602); over 28,000 images in total (line 804). Reference: manual fruit count per plot (K) from physical counting and harvest (Section 2; lines 569-575 refer to visually identified fruits as ground truth for recognition).
SAME-DATA COMPARISON: no

NOT A COMPARISON (recorded for the reviewer)
- Lines 569-583 and Fig. 8 (correlation 94.6%, stage 2 alone with visually identified fruits, versus 74.2% for the full automatic pipeline, Fig. 9, lines 806-807 and 856-859): the two arms differ in the fruit-recognition stage (manual identification versus BoW detector), and the aggregation rule is the same. This is a detector-only difference, so it is excluded.
- Table 2 (lines 603-641): correlation for a range of BoW weight thresholds W (0 to 1000). W is a detection-stage threshold, so this is a detector-module parameter sweep and is excluded.
- Table 3 (lines 750-768; 100 simulations of the full dataset of 435 plots, bK bias for S2 limits 75, 90, 95, 99, 99.5, 99.9%): this varies the probability level of the same-fruit acceptance test in the multi-view algorithm (COUNTING-RULE-like). It is excluded for two reasons: the data are simulations generated from the model, not observations from images, so criterion (a) does not hold; and the table reports bias only, with no arm evaluated on the real images. Reviewer may decide whether to count it. Printed bias values: 75% 0.83; 90% 0.24; 95% 0.00; 99% 0.19; 99.5% 0.21; 99.9% 0.27.
- Lines 649-657 (summing K over four heights, and views from two aisles treated separately): describes the counting procedure, not a comparison with an alternative arm.
- Discussion lines 712-744 mentions earlier template-correlation and colour-threshold attempts, and a 3D approach (Song et al., 2011). No numbers are reported for these on the same data, so they are not comparisons.

NOTES:
- The abstract and Conclusions give 94.6% (stage 2 with visually identified fruits, 10 plots, Fig. 8) and 74.2% (full pipeline, 435 plots, Fig. 9). These are on different plot sets (10 versus 435), so they are not same-data numbers even apart from the detector difference.
- Appendix (lines 866-1140): the multi-view algorithm estimates variance parameters from pairs and triplets and then assigns observations to fruits at the 95% level (step 3, lines 1105-1107). The 95% rule is the same algorithm as in Table 3; no alternative counting-rule arm with real data is reported.
- The multi-view algorithm itself has no without-aggregation arm reported. Single-image counts are not summed as a comparison; the text says the plot count "could not be estimated using single images only" (lines 569-572), with no numbers.
