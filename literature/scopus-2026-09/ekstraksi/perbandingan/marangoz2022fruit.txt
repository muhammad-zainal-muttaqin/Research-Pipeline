C1 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: The old approach's count drops late in the run because nearby fruits are merged into one cluster, which decreases volume accuracy (lines 429-432). The authors attribute the new method's gain to its more accurate TSDF map and better clustering, which merge less often (lines 433-435). The SE gain is stated for the MA0 and MA50 counts only, and the low average accuracy of the all-fruit SE row is attributed to clustering failures with merged neighbours and overestimated shapes (lines 436-443).
