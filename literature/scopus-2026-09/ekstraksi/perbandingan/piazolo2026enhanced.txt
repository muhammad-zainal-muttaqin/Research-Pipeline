KEY: piazolo2026enhanced
CROP AND DATA: grape (Vitis vinifera, UAV RGB video of a vineyard, Sentís et al. 2021 dataset). Test data: 136 test frames (Table 7, lines 1222-1226), 147 annotated grapes (AG) in the test subset (lines 821-823). Reference: hand count (HC) of visible clusters in the video, 150 foreground + 36 background = at least 186 (lines 824-832; Table 6 lines 883-890). Tracking reference is annotation (MOTS-style labels, line 1236-1237); counting reference is HC.
SAME-DATA COMPARISON: yes

C1 TYPE: TRACKER
C1 ARMS: Five trackers run with the same uniform Mask R-CNN detector (line 839-840) on the same test subset: SORT (Bewley et al., 2016), DeepSORT (Wojke et al., 2017), ByteTrack (Zhang et al., 2022), SORT+ (authors' extended matching: IoU, Mahalanobis and Euclidean distances), DeepSORT+ (authors' extended matching with the classification/re-ID network).
C1 RESULTS (Table 5, lines 762-797, mean over 1000 bootstrap trials ± 95% CI as printed):
  - MOTA: SORT 37.69% ± 0.33%; DeepSORT 42.25% ± 0.37%; ByteTrack 41.53% ± 0.37%; SORT+ 41.50% ± 0.40%; DeepSORT+ 42.71% ± 0.37%. Best: DeepSORT+.
  - IDF1: SORT 60.42% ± 0.09%; DeepSORT 66.93% ± 0.21%; ByteTrack 66.01% ± 0.19%; SORT+ 66.48% ± 0.18%; DeepSORT+ 67.10% ± 0.19%. Best: DeepSORT+.
  - IDsw (lower is better): SORT 73.50 ± 1.20; DeepSORT 17.05 ± 0.29; ByteTrack 26.09 ± 0.33; SORT+ 28.24 ± 0.49; DeepSORT+ 14.90 ± 0.23 (bold in print). Best: DeepSORT+.
  - MOTP: SORT 65.41% ± 0.08%; DeepSORT 65.46% ± 0.07%; ByteTrack 65.44% ± 0.08%; SORT+ 65.40% ± 0.07%; DeepSORT+ 65.39% ± 0.08%. Best (highest): DeepSORT. Differences are in the second decimal.
  - Counting (Table 6, lines 882-900; count of tracks / counting accuracy): SORT 313 / 32.7%; DeepSORT 195 / 95.2%; ByteTrack 238 / 72.0%; SORT+ 192 / 96.8%; DeepSORT+ 186 / 100%. Best: DeepSORT+.
  - Text states DeepSORT+ has "the highest MOTA score of 42.7% (±0.4%) and an IDF1 score of 67.1% (±0.2%), with only 15 ID switches over 186 counted tracks" (lines 852-855). The authors also say that the overlapping 95% CIs of DeepSORT and DeepSORT+ make the best tracker "inconclusive" (lines 857-859).
C1 WHERE: Table 5 (lines 760-797); Table 6 (lines 870-900); text lines 846-865 (Section 5.1), 901-909, 933-941, 1022-1025.
C1 QUOTE: "DeepSORT+ consistently outperforms the other tracking algorithms in almost every metric, achieving the highest MOTA score of 42.7% (±0.4%) and an IDF1 score of 67.1% (±0.2%), with only 15 ID switches over 186 counted tracks." (lines 852-855)
C1 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: Counting accuracy is said to be higher for DeepSORT, SORT+ and DeepSORT+ "likely due to the use of the Mahalanobis distance (Mahalanobis, 1936) as their only common similarity measure" (lines 849-851). The top DeepSORT variants are attributed to the classification network: "As only the classification network distinguishes the top-performing trackers DeepSORT ... and DeepSORT+, from the other algorithms, it proves highly effective in generating accurate feature vectors" (lines 860-864). ByteTrack and SORT+ are described as "viable alternatives" (lines 933-938). The gap between SORT and the others is attributed to matching: "integrating additional similarities in the matching process can make SORT a viable tracker" (lines 905-909).
C1 CONDITIONS: UAV RGB video, vineyard, small grape clusters (0.01%-0.17% of the frame, lines 1005-1007, 1078-1079); detector bbox mAP 19.4% and segmentation mAP 14.5% (lines 841-843). Frame rate 59.94 fps, flight speed 0.7 m/s, altitude 3 m AGL, dense canopy, no leaf removal (Table 7, lines 1204-1217; lines 1190-1193). Overall counting: the authors describe DeepSORT, SORT+ and DeepSORT+ as having at most 5% overcount (lines 846-848).

C2 TYPE: COMPONENT

C2 RESULTS (Table 5 and Table 6): MOTA SORT 37.69% vs SORT+ 41.50% (SORT+ higher); IDF1 60.42% vs 66.48% (SORT+ higher); IDsw 73.50 vs 28.24 (SORT+ lower, better); MOTP 65.41% vs 65.40% (SORT slightly higher, difference within 0.01 points); count 313 (32.7%) vs 192 (96.8%) (SORT+ closer to the 186 hand count). Best: SORT+ on every metric except MOTP.
C2 WHERE: Table 5 (lines 768-787); Table 6 (lines 877-899); text lines 1096-1099 and 1120-1122; lines 901-909.
C2 QUOTE: "Table 5 in Section 4 shows that SORT+ achieves significantly better results compared to the original SORT (Bewley et al., 2016) with MOTA increasing from 37.7% to 41.5% and ID switches decreasing from 73.5 to 28.2." (lines 1096-1099)
C2 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: The enhanced matching process is credited with the change: "The enhanced matching process significantly reduces the ID switches in SORT (Bewley et al., 2016) from 74 to 28 in SORT+, improves the counting accuracy from 32.7% to 96.8% and greatly increases the tracking accuracy." (lines 1120-1122). The authors also say the SORT+ improvement needs no extra data or training: "integrating additional similarities in the matching process can make SORT (Bewley et al., 2016) a viable tracker and without requiring additional data and training." (lines 906-909)
C2 CONDITIONS: Same as C1.

C3 TYPE: COMPONENT
