KEY: ramos2018measurement
CROP AND DATA: Coffee; 30 marked branches (10 with all nine collection times), about 90 frames per branch from 15 s video, 63 held-out branches for evaluation; reference: manual count of unripe, semi-ripe, ripe fruit per branch
SAME-DATA COMPARISON: no
NOTES: The only arms evaluated by the authors on the same branches are linear vs zig-zag camera movement (lines 980-1001), reported only as points per cloud (Table 4, lines 962-974: average 48,675 linear vs 20,609 zig-zag; upper 74,417 vs 25,606; lower 22,933 vs 15,613), not as a fruit count or identity metric, so criterion (c) is not met. The authors explain it by SfM discarding tracks with more than one key point in the same image ("makes tracks inconsistent", lines 989-995). The comparison with the 2D method of an earlier paper (ripeness percentage error 12% against 3.7%-8.7%, lines 1441-1444 and 1610-1613) uses a value from that paper and is not a same-data arm.
