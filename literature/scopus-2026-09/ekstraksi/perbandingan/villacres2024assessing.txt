NOTES:
- MC-FDR is computed against the union of all four cameras. Subsets that include more cameras are measured against a reference that contains their own detections, so the gain with more cameras is partly built into the metric. The authors do not discuss this.
- The text (lines 688-692) gives a single-camera range of 86.3% to 88.3% for camera 1, which matches Table 1.
- The text (line 693-694) gives 95.3% to 97.5% for the best pair, matching Table 2 for camera{1∪3}.
- Table 2 and Table 3 rows for pairs with camera 4 were not written into the recorded notes; they are not reported here.
- The text says "about 9%" (line 690); the table values give 93.2 - 86.3 = 6.9 for test 1 and 92.6 - 87.3 = 5.3 for test 2 against camera 1 alone, so the "about 9%" figure is not checked here.
