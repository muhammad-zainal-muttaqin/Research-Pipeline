C1 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: Occlusion. "It can be observed that incorporating multiple points of view reduces the occlusion of grape bunches, making them easier to detect and track." (lines 377-379). Undercounting attributed to occlusion: "This can be expected because of occlusions, making underestimation more likely than overestimation, except in cases where there are a lot of FPs." (lines 416-418). Multi-view limitation: "tracking using three views presented difficulties due to motion blur from UAV movement, causing the camera to lose track of the fruit, and impacting the continuity of ID numbers through frames." (lines 461-464).
