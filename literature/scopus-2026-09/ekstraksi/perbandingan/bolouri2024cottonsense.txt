CROP AND DATA
Cotton (Gossypium hirsutum), four fruit stages (Square, Flower, ClosedBoll, OpenBoll). RGB-D video from two Intel RealSense D435i cameras on a tractor, Lubbock TX, July to November 2021. Enumeration evaluation uses 16 videos collected September 9 to October 12, 2022 from dryland rows, hand-counted (lines 873-878). Thesis, Texas Tech University, August 2023 (lines 1-5 of front matter and line 409).

SAME-DATA COMPARISON: no

NOTES
- The enumeration-by-tracking result (average R2 0.93, Table 2.3, lines 1171-1187) is one arm, a modified SORT tracker with per-track category voting (lines 816-852), compared only against hand counts. No second linking or counting arm is evaluated on those 16 videos.
- The comparison to Sun et al. (R2 0.91 for OpenBoll, lines 1173-1175) uses numbers from another paper, excluded by the rule.
- The per-image counts against ten experts (R2 0.97 total, 0.85 per category, lines 1149-1154) are a detector-count comparison on 80 unannotated still images, not a same-data counting-rule comparison with another arm.
- The FP32 versus FP16 TensorRT comparison (lines 920-924) is a detector-optimisation comparison and is excluded.
- The Faster R-CNN baseline cited for Sun et al. (lines 1061-1063) is another paper's model, excluded.
- Tuning thresholds Tenu = 100 and Tobs = 300 frames were set from a rule of thumb (lines 856-861); no sweep is reported.
