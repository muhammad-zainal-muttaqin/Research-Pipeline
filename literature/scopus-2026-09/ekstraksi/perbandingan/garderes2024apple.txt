KEY: garderes2024apple

CROP AND DATA
Apple (Malus) trees in a row. Video in an INIA Las Brujas (Uruguay) orchard, filmed with a Samsung Galaxy S21 FE mounted on a Jackal robot, one pass up and one pass back along the row (lines 431-444). Labelled clips DS-INIA-1 (about 20 s, 139 apples) and DS-INIA-2 (about 25 s, 200 apples) (lines 449-454, 712-714). Unlabelled full-row videos DS-INIA-IDA, DS-INIA-VUELTA, DS-INIA-COMPLETO; 2133 apples harvested from the row (lines 442-444, 455-462). Simulator video DS-SIM (ROS/Ignition, 61 apples) (lines 446-448). Detectors trained on DS2 (YOLOv5s, YOLOv8n) (lines 519-520, 694-696). CLEI 2024 conference paper, IEEE Xplore.

QUOTE: "Como se puede notar en el cuadro III, los mejores resultados en HOTA y MOTA se dan con OCSort en todos los casos, mientras que los mejores resultados de EAIDs (error absoluto en la cantidad de IDs) son siempre menores con StrongSort, y los de IDF1 varían entre StrongSort y OCSort." AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: "Esto induce a pensar que, si bien el seguimiento es mejor con OCSort, la asociación de los identificadores con los objetos rastreados es más exacta con StrongSort." (lines 730-733). Speed: OCSort and ByteTrack about 30 ms, StrongSort 3 to 4 times slower (lines 733-736).

Times are per frame, on the authors' hardware (Google Colab, lines 471-477), which is not stated.

QUOTE: "En general, los resultados de cantidad total de manzanas son considerablemente mayores a la cantidad real de manzanas." (lines 753-754)
AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: "Lo que es más claro es que se están detectando una gran cantidad de manzanas de filas posteriores, lo que altera el conteo de la cantidad total de frutas." (lines 758-760). The authors also say "visualmente no parece que haya una gran variación en los identificadores asignados a las frutas" (lines 756-757). Their conclusion for this video: StrongSort is closest to the real count (lines 1125-1127), with all results affected by second-row apples and lighting (lines 1130-1133).
CONDITIONS: No ground truth for these videos; the reference is the harvest total of 2133 for the row, which the authors note is not known to be exactly the number visible in the video (lines 442-444). The full-row video includes the turn at the row end (lines 749-752). The Suma column is the sum of the two half-row videos, not a separate run.

C3 TYPE: COUNTING-RULE (post-hoc correction of tracker counts)
ARMS: (a) Coefficient method (R*CA): predicted count multiplied by a factor equal to the mean of real/predicted ratios from training runs (lines 996-1006). (b) Linear regression (Reg.): line fitted from predicted to real counts on training runs (lines 991-995). Both are applied to the same tracker outputs (YOLOv5 and YOLOv8 with StrongSort, ByteTrack, OCSort) on the same test videos (lines 984-990, 1016-1019).
RESULTS (Table V, lines 1020-1050; mean relative error ± SD):
- V1 5 (GT 375): coefficient 373, 0.06 ± 0.04; regression 366, 0.04 ± 0.03.
- V1 10 (GT 722): coefficient 672, 0.07 ± 0.03; regression 491, 0.32 ± 0.15.
- V3 5 (GT 362): coefficient 348, 0.04 ± 0.02; regression 354, 0.02 ± 0.09.
- DS-INIA-COMPLETO (GT 2133): coefficient 2533, 0.20 ± 0.19; regression 1261, 0.45 ± 0.28.
WHERE: Method lines 960-1014; results lines 1016-1070; conclusions 1137-1154.
QUOTE: "Sin embargo, estos resultados no parecen ser consistentes cuando las características de los datos evaluados difieren de las de los datos de entrenamiento." (lines 1141-1143)
AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: For V1 5 and V3 5 the test instances use the training parameters (lines 974-976), and the authors attribute the coefficient's stability to the number of trees: "Particularmente, el modelo de regresión lineal se destaca cuando la cantidad de árboles se mantiene, con un error relativo menor al 10 %, pero aumenta al 30 % al tener el doble de árboles en el video evaluado." (lines 1143-1146). Their recommendation: regression when the tree count matches training, coefficient when it differs (lines 1150-1154). For V3 5, far-row apples are small and rarely detected, unlike the real environment (lines 1071-1076).
CONDITIONS: Both correction models are fit on 20 simulator instances with 5 trees per row and constant parameters (lines 969-973). Test set is 5 instances (3 with training parameters, 1 with 10 trees, 1 with 3 rows) plus the real full-row video (lines 974-983). Table V values are averages over the detector-tracker combinations; the paper does not give per-combination numbers here (lines 1056). The regression error on DS-INIA-COMPLETO is 45 % in Table V and 30 % in the conclusion (line 1146), so the two reports differ. The text at line 1067 gives 32 % for V1 10, which matches the table.
