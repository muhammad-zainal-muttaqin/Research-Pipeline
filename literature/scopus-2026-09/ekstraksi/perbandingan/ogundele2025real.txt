C1 QUOTE: "The DeepSORT algorithm, while powerful for tracking objects with distinct appearances such as pedestrians, proved not ideal for strawberries." (lines 1225-1226)
C1 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: DeepSORT: "The core issue lies in its reliance on a Re-ID module for appearance matching." The subtle similarity between fruits and lighting changes "led to frequent feature-matching errors, resulting in numerous misidentifications", giving "significant over-counting and a consequently lower R2 of 0.842" (lines 1226-1231). SORT, Norfair, and MOTPY, which rely on motion prediction, "often failed to re-establish the track upon reappearance, leading to fragmented trajectories and a high number of undercounts" after leaf occlusion (lines 1232-1236). ByteTrack's advantage is attributed to "leveraging ByteTrack's proven robustness to temporary object disappearances" (lines 1207-1209).
C1 CONDITIONS: occlusion by leaves in the dense canopy (lines 1233-1236); irregular, non-linear motion of the manually guided platform, where the simple Kalman filter prediction was "often insufficient to bridge the gap during periods of occlusion" (lines 1236-1239). Frame rate not stated.
