CROP AND DATA: sweet pepper (red bell pepper) in protected farming, RGB-D point clouds from an Intel RealSense F200 on a robot arm (dataset from Sa et al., ref. 21); test data: 5 trees (Table 1 and Table 2), 86 fruits at least partially visible in the scene (Table 1 total); the 3D scene was downsampled before processing (lines 582-586); reference type: manual counting by human inspectors of all images (lines 209-211), and ground-truth positions used for false negatives (lines 665-666); the number of annotated images is not stated.
SAME-DATA COMPARISON: yes

C1 TYPE: MECHANISM (single-scene 3D instance separation: subtractive clustering vs RANSAC shape fitting; the arms differ only in how points of adjacent fruits are grouped into one fruit, not in linking across views)
C1 ARMS: proposed subtractive clustering (density-based cluster centres on the point cloud, Section 2.2.3) vs RANSAC (shape fitting, Section 3.2, lines 733-742). Both applied to the same tree point cloud (Figure 8 is stated to use "the same" original point cloud as Figure a, lines 741-742). Whether RANSAC received the same colour-filtered and outlier-filtered input is not stated.
C1 RESULTS: Subtractive clustering (proposed): TPR 90.69% (78 of 86), FPR 6.97% (6 of 86), FNR 9.31% (8 of 86). RANSAC: TPR 86.05% (74 of 86), FPR 3.49% (3 of 86), FNR 13.95% (12 of 86). Proposed is best on TPR and FNR. RANSAC is best on FPR (3.49% vs 6.97%).
C1 WHERE: Table 1 (lines 677-726) for the proposed method; Table 2 (lines 755-795) for RANSAC; result prose lines 797-806; Section 3.3 lines 807-823.
C1 QUOTE: "Based on the RANSAC algorithm, a TPR of 86.05% was obtained, which was worse than the TPR of 90.69% based on the proposed algorithm." (lines 797-799)
C1 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: For RANSAC, the authors state: "The most important reason was that the two missing point clouds were fitted into one for the error detection of the adjacent occluded point cloud." (lines 744-746). For the higher FPR of the proposed method, they state the FPR of RANSAC "was superior to the one based on subtractive clustering, which was due to the repeated detection of the same fruit" (lines 799-801). They also state that large fruits can get two cluster centres because the radius was set for small fruit, which raises FPR (lines 814-819).
