C1 TYPE: MECHANISM
C1 ARMS: (a) Merged: fruit count from both sides merged into one 3D model by the authors' yield mapping (Sec. 4.4.4; inclusion-exclusion over connected components, per the Methods text at lines 1294-1298). (b) Sum of single sides: counts from the front-side and back-side reconstructions added without merging. Same reconstructions and same trees in both arms; the arms differ only in how the two side counts are combined.
C1 RESULTS: Harvested FCs (reference): Dataset-IV 270, Dataset-V 274, Dataset-VI 414. Merged FCs from both sides (percent of harvest as printed): Dataset-IV 256 (94.81%), Dataset-V 252 (91.98%), Dataset-VI 392 (94.68%). Sum of FCs from single sides (percent of harvest as printed): Dataset-IV 348 (128.89%), Dataset-V 411 (150%), Dataset-VI 422 (101.93%). Merged is closer to harvest on Dataset-IV and Dataset-V; on Dataset-VI the sum is closer (|422-414| = 8 vs |392-414| = 22). Merged is more consistent across datasets (91.98%-94.81% range vs 101.93%-150%). Best arm on the consistency criterion: merged. Metric is fruit count and percent of harvest; no mAP or precision/recall.

The only overlap statement is about canopy volume: "there is a substantial overlap between two-sides reconstructions in modern orchards" (lines 1793-1795). Reason for the merged arm's consistency: not stated beyond "much more consistent".
C1 CONDITIONS: Orchard is modern high-density, trees not well trimmed, so the visible fraction from one side varies (lines 1851-1854). Platform: handheld RGB-D camera moved along the row (lines 1940-1941 and Sec. 5.5). Frame rate and speed for these datasets: not stated in Sec. 5.3.
