KEY: xia2022culling
CROP AND DATA: Apple and orange orchards, fruit counting in video-derived image sequences (Agronomy 2022, 12, 440). Videos from iPhone 8 at 1080 x 1920 (lines 186-206); 120 apple and 120 orange sequences, split equally into train and test. Matching test: 20 groups of two adjacent images for apple (828 fruits) and 20 for orange (793 fruits), randomly selected from the test data (lines 556-564). Yield test: 20 apple sequences (910 fruits) and 20 orange (844 fruits) from the test data (lines 623-642, 708-710). Reference: for matching, pair annotation of duplicate fruits (lines 186-206); for yield, ground truth count minus duplicates from the pair-annotation tool (lines 186-206).
SAME-DATA COMPARISON: yes (implied, not stated as identical pairs; see NOTES)

C1 TYPE: MECHANISM
C1 ARMS: Same 20 apple and 20 orange groups of adjacent images from the test data. Arm A: classical DeepCompare model (reference matcher). Arm B: the authors' fruit matching model, which is a DeepCompare-based network on grayscale patches with Kuhn-Munkres one-to-one pairing of fruit patches between adjacent images (lines 673-677, 583-584, 654-659). The arms differ in the learned matcher and in the assignment step; the authors did not run a variant with only one of these changed.
C1 RESULTS (Table 4, lines 585-613; Accuracy / Precision / Recall / F1-Score):
  - Apple, DeepCompare: 0.937 / 0.503 / 0.592 / 0.544
  - Apple, Ours (Kuhn-Munkres): 0.975 / 0.793 / 0.840 / 0.816
  - Orange, DeepCompare: 0.966 / 0.701 / 0.776 / 0.737
  - Orange, Ours (Kuhn-Munkres): 0.985 / 0.853 / 0.875 / 0.864
  - Best: Ours (Kuhn-Munkres) on all four metrics for both apple and orange.
C1 WHERE: Table 4, lines 585-613; text lines 581-584, 556-564; abstract lines 55-62; conclusion lines 727-729.
C1 QUOTE: "The experimental results demonstrate that the Kuhn–Munkres algorithm in the fruit matching model can significantly improve the recognition power for duplicate fruits." (lines 582-584)
C1 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: The Kuhn-Munkres step gives one-to-one pairing: "the Kuhn–Munkres algorithm can pair fruits one-to-one to achieve the optimal matching of the fruits in adjacent images, helping our model prevent fruits from being double counted, caused by repeat detection in successive images" (lines 654-657). The text says "our fruit matching model exhibits better performance than the former" (lines 581-582). The authors do not separate the effect of the learned matcher from the effect of Kuhn-Munkres.
C1 CONDITIONS: Adjacent images from sequences captured by moving the camera along the tree row; 1080 x 1920 video frames; CenterNet detections used as input to matching (lines 186-206, 673-677). Frame rate not stated in the read lines. Matching on all patches is brute-force and not real-time (lines 713-715). No statistical test or repeated runs reported.
