C1 TYPE: TRACKER
C1 ARMS: BoT-SORT, ByteTrack, OC-SORT, DeepOC-SORT. All four receive detections from the same YOLO11n-CLL detector on the same 8 test videos; the arms differ in the multi-object tracker. BoT-SORT adds a camera-motion-compensation module and corrected Kalman filter prediction (lines 402-408, 485-491).
C1 RESULTS: HOTA / MOTA / IDF1 / IDSW (Table 5, lines 713-745). BoT-SORT: 62.4% / 68.4% / 73.0% / 1102. ByteTrack: 56.6% / 62.0% / 67.7% / 744. OC-SORT: 60.8% / 66.0% / 70.8% / 2105. DeepOC-SORT: 60.2% / 65.6% / 71.2% / 403. BoT-SORT is best on HOTA, MOTA and IDF1; DeepOC-SORT has the lowest IDSW (403), followed by ByteTrack (744), BoT-SORT (1102) and OC-SORT (2105). Reported gains of BoT-SORT over ByteTrack, OC-SORT and DeepOC-SORT are +5.8, +1.6, +2.2 HOTA points, +6.4, +2.4, +2.8 MOTA points, +5.3, +2.2, +1.8 IDF1 points, and 1003 fewer IDSW than OC-SORT (lines 753-756).
C1 WHERE: Table 5 (lines 713-745); discussion lines 747-764; Fig. 11 visualization lines 765-792.
C1 QUOTE: "由表5可知,BoT-SORT算法在多项核心指标上均展现出最优性能，其HOTA、MOTA、IDF1、IDSW分别为62.4%、68.4%、73.0%、1102，前3项核心指标均显著领先于其他对比算法。" (lines 747-752)
C1 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: The authors attribute BoT-SORT's lead to camera motion compensation: sparse optical flow between adjacent frames corrects the Kalman filter prediction and mitigates camera shake, fast target motion and local occlusion (lines 402-408, 485-491). They note that BoT-SORT is slightly worse than ByteTrack and DeepOC-SORT on IDSW but better on the other three tracking metrics (lines 757-764), and that its camera-motion-compensation mechanism makes it the most stable in the complex orchard environment (lines 790-792).
C1 CONDITIONS: one HUAWEI P50 phone fixed at 1.5 m above ground and 1 m from the target, covering about 15 m of planting area; 30 f/s; dense leaf and branch occlusion, overexposure, motion blur and uneven illumination (lines 119-143, 163-171). A separate edge-device demo ran at 19.6 f/s on AMD Ryzen 3 2200U with NVIDIA GeForce 940MX (2 GB) (lines 872-907).

C2 TYPE: COUNTING-RULE
C2 ARMS: region-based counting, line-based counting, ID-accumulation counting. All three are applied to the same tracking output from the same 8 test videos; they differ in the counting rule. ID accumulation sums the maximum track ID; line counting triggers when a fruit centroid crosses a virtual line on the video midline; region counting counts a fruit only after its centroid stays inside a rectangular region of interest for several consecutive frames (lines 519-535).
C2 RESULTS: mean accuracy over all test sequences (Table 6, "all" row, lines 854-857): region-based 92.8%, line-based 84.0%, ID-accumulation 62.9%. Region-based is higher by 8.8 percentage points than line-based and by 29.9 points than ID-accumulation (lines 858-860); the authors also state it is highest on every individual video (lines 860-861).
C2 WHERE: Table 6 (lines 814-857); narrative lines 858-885; Fig. 8 definition of the three rules (lines 512-517); Abstract lines 15-17 and 52-56; conclusion lines 925-932.
C2 QUOTE: "区域计数法始终优于其他2种方法，在所有测试序列中平均准确率为92.8%，较划线计数法提升8.8个百分点，较ID累加计数法提升29.9个百分点。" (lines 858-860)
C2 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: The region rule adds a temporal-continuity constraint and tolerates short track loss, which reduces counting errors where occlusion is frequent (lines 881-885). In the visualized case of fruit ID 75 (frames 229-234 of video 10), short occlusion broke the track; after re-acquisition at frame 230 line counting did not trigger because the fruit had already crossed the detection boundary, while region counting still counted it (lines 863-879). No separate explanation is given for the lower accuracy of ID accumulation.
C2 CONDITIONS: 30 f/s video; short fruit occlusion by leaves and branches; direction of centroid crossing for the line rule; HUAWEI P50 camera at 1.5 m height and 1 m object distance (lines 119-129, 163-171).
