KEY: daud2022loose
CROP AND DATA: Oil palm (smallholder plantation near Sepang, Selangor, Malaysia). Test data: 10 test images of loose fruitlets (about 200 LF in total) for fruitlet counting (lines 543-544); detector data of 5013 images (700 x 1000) for tree, FFB and grabber detection, split (80,10,10), (70,20,10) and (60,20,20) (lines 472-483). Reference type: annotated bounding boxes and ROI binary masks for fruitlets (lines 307-310); annotated bounding boxes for tree, FFB and grabber (lines 441-447, 568-570). The "collected FFB" count is derived from box overlap between FFB and grabber, not from a hand count of harvested bunches (lines 540-541, 564-567); no hand count is stated.
SAME-DATA COMPARISON: no
(No C-entries. Tables II-IV (lines 571-681) vary only the detector's training split and training steps (15k, 30k, 50k) for the Faster-RCNN. These are detector-only variants and are excluded. The fruitlet counting method is reported as a single arm, with average accuracy 92.65% on 10 test images (lines 543-545, 690-691). The collected-FFB count (lines 561-567, 640-643) has no second counting arm and no quantitative count result.)
