C1 TYPE: COMPONENT
C1 ARMS: INIT = Stage 2 output only (view-guided triangulation, lines 262-394; no Stage 3 refinement). OPT-10, OPT-20, OPT-30 = Stage 2 output followed by Stage 3 differentiable refinement with clone-prune topology updates, using 10, 20 or 30 selected views (lines 521-524). Arms differ only by the presence of the Stage 3 refinement step. The authors state that Stage 3 can add or remove berries, so the refinement also changes the count (lines 423-425, 734-737). Stage 3 is a 3D refinement step, not a tracker; it is recorded as COMPONENT on the judgment that it is a multi-view 3D step that changes the count.
C1 RESULTS: Dense bunches (N_gt >= 60, 6 bunches), MAE of berry count: INIT 10.5; OPT-10 8.0; OPT-20 8.2; OPT-30 4.8 (Table 2, row ">= 60"). Best on this metric: OPT-30. All bunches (30), MAE: INIT 3.7; OPT-10 3.1; OPT-20 3.2; OPT-30 2.5 (Table 2, row "Overall"). Best: OPT-30. Berry-level OBB-IoU detection, precision/recall/F1 in %, 30 bunches (Table 3): at IoU 0.10, INIT R 34.7 / P 36.6 / F1 35.6; OPT-10 R 77.1 / P 80.4 / F1 78.7; OPT-20 R 86.0 / P 89.8 / F1 87.8; OPT-30 R 87.3 / P 89.9 / F1 88.5. At IoU 0.25, INIT R 10.1 / P 10.4 / F1 10.3; OPT-10 R 63.3 / P 65.8 / F1 64.5; OPT-20 R 72.6 / P 75.8 / F1 74.1; OPT-30 R 74.0 / P 76.0 / F1 74.9. At IoU 0.45, INIT F1 0.3; OPT-10 F1 6.3; OPT-20 F1 10.5; OPT-30 F1 9.9 (see NOTES on this row). Best on every F1 value: OPT-30 at IoU 0.10 and 0.25 (88.5, 74.9) with OPT-20 at 0.45 (10.5). C1 WHERE: Table 2 (lines 626-662, MAE by bunch range); Table 3 (lines 663-717, OBB-IoU); Section 5.4 text (lines 727-737); Section 5.5 text (lines 739-753).
C1 QUOTE: "The main gains appear in dense bunches (≥60 berries), where refinement significantly reduces under-counting, particularly at 𝑀= 30(dense MAE: 10.5 →4.8)." (lines 730-733)
C1 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: The authors attribute the gain to refinement reducing under-counting in dense bunches (lines 730-733). They describe topology updates as conservative, with berry additions dominating deletions, "consistent with the design of the clone–prune mechanism" (lines 734-737). For 3D geometry they state that "Differentiable refinement yields substantial gains at 𝜏= 0.10and 𝜏= 0.25, indicating much tighter alignment of berry centres and scales" (lines 746-747). The authors do not explain why the 0.45 threshold gain is small beyond "occlusion and noise in the ellipsoid ground truth" (lines 748-749).
C1 CONDITIONS: Handheld smartphone sequences around each bunch (lines 512-514). Dense bunches with heavy occlusion (lines 579-582). Stage 3 on a single RTX 4080 SUPER (16 GB) (lines 527-528). Stage 1 and Stage 2 costs are excluded from the Stage 3 runtime (lines 533-536). Frame rate and lighting are not stated.

C2 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: The authors state that runtime grows roughly linearly with the number of views (lines 719-720) and that M = 30 gives the highest accuracy at higher cost (lines 721-722). They give no separate explanation for why O10 has lower overall MAE than O20 (3.1 vs 3.2).

The example bunch in Figure 4 reports runtimes of about 1.4, 4.2 and 6.2 minutes for M = 10, 20, 30 (lines 764-766), which differ from the averages in Table 1.
