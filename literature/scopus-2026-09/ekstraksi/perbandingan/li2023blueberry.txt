KEY: li2023blueberry
CROP AND DATA: Blueberry (Vaccinium), 12 individual plants of different genotypes. Test data: 12 plants, each cropped from image frames of three views (top, left, right) and annotated with bounding boxes; the 12 plants were then harvested. Reference type: manual count of all potential blueberries on each harvested plant (lines 559-566). Detector YOLOv8x.
SAME-DATA COMPARISON: yes
C1 TYPE: ACQUISITION (number of views: single top, single left, single right, versus three views combined). The multi-view arm also uses MECHANISM (multiple linear regression of the three view counts onto the manual count, lines 588-596).
C1 ARMS: (1) top view alone, linear regression of the top-view YOLOv8x count on the manual count; (2) left view alone, same procedure; (3) right view alone, same procedure; (4) multi-view, multiple linear regression with three view counts as inputs (equation 1, line 591). All arms use the same 12 plants.
C1 RESULTS:
  - Multi-view: R2 = 0.772 (line 595) and R2 = 0.77 (line 598, Figure 9b caption line 625); mean absolute percentage error (MAPE) = 24.63% (line 605; Figure 9c caption line 626). Abstract and Conclusions give MAPE 24.6% and R2 0.77 (line 646-647).
  - Top view: R2 = 0.75 (line 602); MAPE = 29.86% (lines 606-607).
  - Left view: MAPE = 30.5% (lines 607-608). R2 not stated.
  - Right view: R2 not stated; MAPE not stated. Only its visibility ratio is given: 38.1% (line 573).
  - Best arm: multi-view on both R2 (0.77 versus 0.75 top) and MAPE (24.63% versus 29.86% top and 30.5% left). The text states that multi-view achieves the highest R2 (line 598) and lowest MAPE (line 604-605).
  - Visibility ratio (annotated boxes in one view divided by manual count, Figure 9a, lines 570-574): top 52.7%, left 47.3%, right 38.1%. Top view is highest.
C1 WHERE: Section 3.3 (lines 559-611); Figure 9 (a, b, c; caption lines 621-627); equation 1 (line 591); coefficients (lines 594-595); Conclusions (lines 645-647).
C1 QUOTE: "Among the 12 experimental plants, the multi-view approach emerged as the most precise, exhibiting a mean absolute percentage error of 24.63%." (lines 604-605)
C1 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: Single view covers only part of the plant, so single-view counts are partial: "Single-view images can only capture a portion of the plant, which makes it challenging to accurately detect and count all the blueberries." (lines 577-578). Spatial fusion across views is not technically feasible because the cameras have too little overlap: "It noted that there is not enough overlap among the three cameras in our imaging system (section 3.1), which means the incorporation of spatial information derived from multiple views is not doable technically." (lines 583-584). The authors chose regression for "the accessibility of technical route and the number of sampling pairs" (lines 588-589). The regression "only workable for the same imaging system (e.g., camera position and resolution) and it cannot conduct a general conclusion." (lines 585-587).
C1 CONDITIONS: Single imaging system (same camera positions and resolution, lines 585-587). Sample of 12 plants with different genotypes (line 559-560). Plant-level counts after manual cropping of frames per plant (lines 561-563). No held-out validation of the regression is stated in lines 559-611. Visibility is computed relative to manual count (lines 570-572).
