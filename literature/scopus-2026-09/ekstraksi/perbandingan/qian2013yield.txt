KEY: qian2013yield
CROP AND DATA: Fuji apple (富士苹果), hedgerow-type (篱壁型) trees, 10 years old, orchard about 0.24 hm2 (lines 90-97, 324-325). Test data: 40 trees, 80 images (lines 106-109): two images per direction per tree were taken from SE and NW, and one of each pair was selected. Modeling set: 20 odd-ranked trees; validation set: 20 even-ranked trees (lines 143-145). Reference type: harvest (fruit mass per tree weighed at harvest 13-25 Oct 2011, lines 137-141).
SAME-DATA COMPARISON: yes
C1 TYPE: ACQUISITION
C1 ARMS: (a) patch count from the SE side only; (b) patch count from the NW side only; (c) sum of SE and NW patch counts (two sides). Same 40 trees, same 20-tree modeling set, same linear regression for all three arms; the arms differ only in which side(s) of each tree are imaged. Patch pixel-area arms (参数3, 参数4, 参数6) are a separate feature family and are not counted as a comparison here.
C1 RESULTS: modeling set (20 trees), R2 and NRMSE as printed in Table 1 (lines 202-241): (a) SE patch number R2 0.50, NRMSE 0.18; (b) NW patch number R2 0.37, NRMSE 0.21; (c) SE+NW patch number sum R2 0.81, NRMSE 0.11. Best arm: (c) on both metrics. Text lines 245-246 give R2 0.81 for (c); lines 259-261 state that (c) "其估测效果最好" (its estimation effect is best). Validation set (20 trees), model (c) only: NRMSE 0.16 (lines 273-275). No single-side model was run on the validation set.
C1 WHERE: Table 1 (lines 202-241); text lines 243-246 (R2 ranking); lines 253-258 (NRMSE ranking); lines 258-264 (best arm, three-model comparison); lines 265-269 (SE vs NW); lines 271-276 and Fig. 4 (validation); lines 281-289 (validation discussion, cites 图3)
C1 QUOTE: "在以图斑数量为特征参数的3 个模型中，以东南方向和西北方向识别出的图斑数量之和为特征参数的模型，其估测效果最好，这是由于果树自然生长的特性，在不同方向的结果率存在较大差异，若以单方向识别出的图斑数量为特征参数构建整株果树产量的模型，存在着估测结果偏高或偏低。" (lines 258-264)
C1 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: The authors attribute the single-side weakness to fruit distribution: "在不同方向的结果率存在较大差异，若以单方向识别出的图斑数量为特征参数构建整株果树产量的模型，存在着估测结果偏高或偏低" (lines 261-264). The SE arm beats the NW arm, which they link to lighting during capture: "从东南方向获取图像时大部分时间处于顺光或侧顺光，有利于提高成熟果实的识别率" (lines 267-269). For the sum model's errors, overestimates are attributed to one fruit split into several patches and to double counting of fruit seen from both sides, especially in the middle of the tree (lines 296-303); underestimates to overlapping or touching fruit merged into one patch and to occluded fruit lost (lines 304-312).
C1 CONDITIONS: Mature, bagged-then-unbagged, well colored red fruit; capture 11 and 12 Oct 2011, 10:00-15:00 (lines 99-101); camera "Cannon G7", fixed focal length, JPG 800x600, neighboring trees excluded where possible (lines 102-106); harvest 13-25 Oct 2011. Stated limit: the model is for hedgerow-type or near-hedgerow Fuji trees (lines 324-327). Backlit or low-light images are listed as a limit (lines 320-321).
