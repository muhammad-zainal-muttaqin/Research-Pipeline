C1 QUOTE: "After the 3D reconstruction correction step, the model has an L1 loss of 203, an error mean of −0.2% and a standard deviation of 7.8%, demonstrating a large performance gain in both accuracy and consistency." (lines 648-651)
C1 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: The correction matters more for oranges. Oranges are not on a trellis, so there is more occlusion and depth variation, and "many trees in background rows are visible from the camera. As a result, there many more outliers and double counted fruit for oranges, and the effect of the 3D reconstruction step is much more pronounced." (lines 707-715). Apples are on a trellis with less occlusion and controlled night light, so "the effect of this correction is much more muted, due to the fact that the structured environment for the apple dataset makes the tracking problem easier." (lines 695-697, 702-706). Over-counting in oranges comes from visible oranges in background rows (lines 655-662).
C1 CONDITIONS: oranges: natural daylight, camera carried by an operator at walking speed (lines 616-618, 624-626); apples: night with external flash, utility vehicle at about 1 m/s (lines 620-621, 626-627). Frame rates 10 Hz (orange) and 6 Hz (apple) (lines 619, 623). Occlusion and depth variation are described as higher for oranges (lines 707-713).
