KEY: hani2020comparative
CROP AND DATA: Apple, orchard rows in Minnesota. Yield test data: Dataset 1, 2, 3 (videos from both sides of a row; Table 1 lines 625-653 lists 6, 10 and 6 trees for these). Harvested ground truth counts: 270, 274, 414 apples. Reference type: harvest counts (harvested FCs, Table 4 lines 1606-1632).
SAME-DATA COMPARISON: yes
C1 TYPE: COMPONENT (second-side merging of fruit counts from both sides of a row, compared with summing the per-side counts without merging; the summed arm is the MECHANISM alternative)
C1 ARMS: (a) Merged FCs from both sides: counts from front and back videos merged using a consistent geometric representation of the row; (b) Sum of FCs from single sides: front-side count plus back-side count with no merge. Each arm is run with both counting methods, GMM and ResNet50, on the same Datasets 1-3.
C1 RESULTS: Metric: fruit count (FC) and percentage of harvested count, as printed in Table 4.
  Dataset-1 (harvested 270): merged GMM 256 (94.81%); merged ResNet50 258 (95.56%); summed GMM 348 (128.89%); summed ResNet50 347 (128.52%).
  Dataset-2 (harvested 274): merged GMM 252 (91.98%); merged ResNet50 268 (97.81%); summed GMM 411 (150%); summed ResNet50 405 (147.81%).
  Dataset-3 (harvested 414): merged GMM 392 (94.68%); merged ResNet50 405 (97.83%); summed GMM 422 (101.93%); summed ResNet50 430 (103.86%).
  Best arm: merged ResNet50 is closest to harvest on all three datasets (errors 4.44%, 2.19%, 2.17%). The summed GMM arm has the lowest error on Dataset-3 (1.93%, text line 1603), but it over-counts by 28.89% to 50% on Datasets 1 and 2.
C1 WHERE: Table 4 (lines 1606-1632); Figure 16 (a) and (b) (lines 1641-1682); text lines 1599-1605 and 1633-1639.
C1 QUOTE: "Next, we investigate the performance of the GMM and ResNet50 based counting methods when a consistent geometric representation of both sides of a row is available." (lines 1633-1635) and "Figure 16b and Table 4 show the shortcomings of adding fruit counts from individual sides independently." (lines 1600-1601)
C1 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: Summed single-side counts "vary considerably across datasets for both" and "over-count by up to 50% on others, which makes finding a consistent mapping of these counts to the actual yield tedious and error-prone" (lines 1601-1605). For the merged arm the authors state that both methods "provide more consistent estimates compared to the independently summed fruit counts" (lines 1634-1636). The mechanism of the over-count (the same fruit seen from both sides) is not stated in this section.
C1 CONDITIONS: Videos from both sides of a row; the camera counts only visible apples and "does not see any apples that are contained within the tree foliage" (lines 1638-1639). Dataset-1 was acquired in late September with yellowing leaves, which the authors link to more false positives (lines 1722-1725; section 6.6.1, not the yield result itself).
NOTES:
- Table 1 lists four datasets (lines 625-653), but Section 6.5 uses only Datasets 1-3 (line 1598: "We use the three test datasets"). The yield comparison therefore excludes Dataset 4.
- Section 6.3 (Table 3, lines 1255-1286) compares GMM with ResNet50 counting on patches. It is excluded: both arms count within each image, with no association across views or sides, and the arms differ only in counting method.
- The GMM and ResNet50 detection comparisons (Section 6.2 and Figures 11-13, text before line 1251) are detector-only comparisons and are excluded.
- Text line 1601 gives the summed-count range as "GMM(101.93% ∼150%) and ResNet50 (103.86% ∼147.81%)", which matches Table 4.
- Text lines 1634-1635 give merged ranges GMM 91.98%-94.81% and ResNet50 95.56%-97.83%; these match Table 4 (GMM Dataset-3 is 94.68%, inside the range).
- Text line 1637 gives ResNet50 errors of "2.17% −4.44%", which matches Table 4 (2.17%, 2.19%, 4.44%).
- Table 4 header row lists the summed arm under "Sum of FCs from single sides"; the text calls it "summing up counts from both sides" (line 1602). The two labels describe the same arm.
