KEY: hu2023fruit
CROP AND DATA: Apple (Fruit Detection and Counting in Apple Orchards, Sensors 2023, 23, 5903). Test data: 3 videos (IDs 0, 1, 2); manual count per video 134, 92, 64; total 290 fruit (Table 3, lines 694-756). Reference type: manual counting (Table 3 column "Manual Counting"); counting protocol for the manual count not stated.
SAME-DATA COMPARISON: yes

C1 TYPE: TRACKER
C1 ARMS: Three tracking arms on the same 3 videos, with counts summed over the video: SORT; Cascade-SORT; "Our" tracker (SURF cascade matching, per line 805). The arms differ in the tracker. The detector for SORT and Cascade-SORT is not stated; the text attributes part of the Cascade-SORT gain to the detector (lines 792-794).
C1 RESULTS: Overall (all 3 videos, manual 290): SORT count 373, MAE 0.286; Cascade-SORT count 333, MAE 0.148; Our count 310, MAE 0.064. Our is best on overall MAE. Per video, column "MAE": video 0 (manual 134): SORT 185 / 0.380; Cascade-SORT 159 / 0.186; Our 148 / 0.104. Video 1 (manual 92): SORT 113 / 0.228; Cascade-SORT 106 / 0.152; Our 99 / 0.076. Video 2 (manual 64): SORT 75 / 0.171; Cascade-SORT 68 / 0.062; Our 63 / 0.016. Our is best on every video. The "MAE" column is a relative error, not the formula MAE (see NOTES).
C1 WHERE: Table 3, lines 694-756; text lines 787-794.
C1 QUOTE: "As for Cacade-SORT, since a large part of the final overall performance of the multiple objects is affected by the performance of the detector, the overall result is improved by 0.084 MAE, thanks to the more powerful detection effect of our model." (lines 792-794)
C1 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: SORT is worse because "SORT is too simple for image processing and does not take into account some complexities in the agricultural environment" (lines 789-790). Cascade-SORT's gain is attributed to the detector, not to the association (lines 792-794). The authors do not explain why Our is better than Cascade-SORT beyond the detector claim.
C1 CONDITIONS: Apple; 3 videos; frame rate not stated; FPS not reported; lighting and shading vary between videos (lines 795-798).

C2 TYPE: MECHANISM
C2 ARMS: Yolov7 alone (no tracking; per-frame detections summed, as the text implies at line 791, "makes it difficult to avoid too many repeated counts") vs the tracker arms (SORT, Cascade-SORT, Our). The Yolov7 arm uses the baseline Yolov7 detector, whereas "Our" uses Yolov7-CA (line 805; abstract lines 26-27). The detector therefore differs in addition to the mechanism.
C2 RESULTS: Overall: Yolov7 count 495, MAE 0.706; SORT 373 / 0.286; Cascade-SORT 333 / 0.148; Our 310 / 0.064. Every tracker arm is better than Yolov7 on overall MAE; Our is best. Per video, MAE: video 0 Yolov7 241 / 0.798; video 1 Yolov7 157 / 0.706; video 2 Yolov7 97 / 0.426.
C2 WHERE: Table 3, lines 694-756; text lines 787-791; abstract lines 26-30.
C2 QUOTE: "The counting results show that our proposed method has a larger improvement compared to SORT and Yolov7, with an improvement of 0.222 and 0.642 in the overall MAE, respectively, which may be due to the fact that SORT is too simple for image processing and does not take into account some complexities in the agricultural environment, while the simple use of Yolov7 makes it difficult to avoid too many repeated counts." (lines 787-791)
C2 AUTHORS' EXPLANATION: The Yolov7-only arm is worse because "the simple use of Yolov7 makes it difficult to avoid too many repeated counts" (lines 790-791). The authors attribute the gap to repeated counting without tracking.
C2 CONDITIONS: Apple; 3 videos; frame rate not stated; no FPS reported; lighting and shading vary between videos (lines 795-798).

NOTES:
- Table 3 column labeled "MAE" is a per-video relative error, |count - manual| / manual, not the MAE defined by formula (5) at lines 675-685. Check: video 0 SORT, |185 - 134| / 134 = 0.381, printed 0.380. The overall values in the text match this definition: 0.286 - 0.064 = 0.222 (line 788) and 0.706 - 0.064 = 0.642 (line 788 and abstract line 30). The overall "MAE" is the value for the pooled totals (e.g. SORT 373 vs 290), which differs from the mean of the per-video values. Whether the printed overall value is the pooled relative error or an average of per-video values is not stated. The values are copied as printed.
- Detector confound: the detector of SORT and Cascade-SORT arms is not stated; the "Our" arm uses Yolov7-CA (line 805). C1 therefore mixes tracker and detector effects, as the authors acknowledge at lines 792-794 for Cascade-SORT.
- Yolov7 arm (C2) uses a different detector from the tracker arms (Yolov7 vs Yolov7-CA), so C2 is confounded with detection.
- Lines 795-803 state that performance is not stable across videos and that ID switch and MOTA should be added as metrics. These were not measured.
